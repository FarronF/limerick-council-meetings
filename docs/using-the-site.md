# How to Use the Site

This guide explains how to search and browse the Limerick City and County Council meeting records.

## Search the records

Go to **Search** to search the archive. Enter a word, name, topic, or motion in the search box. Put double quotes around words to search for an exact phrase, for example `"active travel"` or `"housing estate"`.

For example, searching for `bus lane` looks for both words in a document, even if they appear separately. Searching for `"bus lane"` looks for the exact phrase, with the words together in that order.

Use the filters beside the results to narrow your search. You can select more than one option, and selections across different filters are combined:

- **Category** narrows results by record category.
- **Council Body** selects Full Council, a municipal district, or a committee.
- **Year** or **Year & Month** limits results to a time period.
- **File Type** selects agendas, minutes, or meeting overviews.
- **Meeting Type** selects regular, special, annual, or other meeting types.

Filter counts update as you search. Faded options have no matches for the current search and selections. Choose **Clear all filters** to remove selected filters; this does not erase the search text.

Results show a document title, meeting date, and a text excerpt. Select a title to open the record. If there are more than 20 results, choose **Load More Results** to display the next set.

Use **Sort by** above the results to order them by relevance (the default), newest meeting date, or oldest meeting date.

## Browse the archive

Choose **Browse Archive** in the navigation to browse records by year and then by month. Open a meeting to see its available agenda, minutes, and overview documents. This can be useful when you know when a meeting took place but do not have search terms.

## Keep in mind

Some scanned PDFs are processed with optical character recognition, so extracted text may contain errors. Minutes may be filed with the later meeting at which they were approved, rather than the meeting they describe. Images and diagrams are omitted from the text. See [About](about.md) for more information about the records and this archive.

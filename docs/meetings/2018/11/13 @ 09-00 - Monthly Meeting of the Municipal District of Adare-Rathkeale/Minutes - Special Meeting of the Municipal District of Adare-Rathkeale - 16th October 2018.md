**MINUTES OF PROCEEDINGS AT SPECIAL MEETING OF THE MUNICIPAL DISTRICT OF**

**ADARE-RATHKEALE HELD IN DESMOND CASTLE, ASKEATON, CO. LIMERICK ON**

**TUESDAY, 16TH** **OCTOBER, 2018 AT 2.30 P.M.**

**PRESENT IN THE CHAIR:**

Councillor A. Teskey, An Cathaoirleach.

**MEMBERS PRESENT:**

Councillors Keary, McMahon, O’Donoghue and Sheahan.

**APOLOGIES:**

Councillor O’Brien.

**OFFICIALS IN ATTENDANCE:**

A/Senior Executive Engineer, Service Operations West Division (Mr. K. Murphy), Senior

Architect, Office of Public Works (Mr. G. Rourke).

**1.**

**Desmond Castle**

Members welcomed the Senior Architect from the Office of the Public Works (OPW) to

the Desmond Castle site. The Senior Architect accompanied the Councillors on a tour of

the Desmond Castle site and informed them of the current programme of works which

the OPW are currently undertaking.

Following discussion it was agreed Members would make contact with the Senior

Executive Officer of the OPW to discuss their proposal of securing extra manpower to

expediate the restoration project.

Members thanked the Senior Architect, OPW, for the informative tour of the site.

This concluded the Meeting.

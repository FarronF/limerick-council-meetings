1.

Local Property Tax

Circulated, report from the Mayor dated 4th July, 2025, setting out the background to

the Local Property Tax – Local Adjustment Factor 2026. The Council was being requested

whether to pass a resolution to vary the basic rate of Local Property Tax for the year ending

31st December 2026.

The Príomh Chomhairleoir informed the Members that the Special Meeting had been

convened to set the rate of Local Property Tax (LPT) for 2026 and that Members had the

power to vary the LPT by a maximum of plus or minus 15% on the basic rate. She outlined

that the purpose of the Special Meeting was solely to set the rate, and that no discussion

2

would take place regarding its use. The Príomh Chomhairleoir noted that once the LPT was

agreed, dates could be set for engagement between the Members, the Mayor and the

Executive on how the LPT would be used. These discussions and debates would form part of

the budgetary process for 2026.

The Mayor outlined the advantages of retaining the LPT rate at plus 15%. He outlined

the benefits of setting a multi-annual rate for the remainder of the term but agreed it would

be wiser to set the rate for one year due to possible changes in legislation which could allow

wider variation. The Mayor stated that he looked forward to working with the Members to

agree how the LPT would be allocated.

Following discussion, Members expressed their support for maintaining the LPT rate

at 15% above base rate for 2026, noting that there would be no change for the people of

Limerick for the year ahead. Members commended Limerick City and County Council’s

proactive and consistent approach in setting the LPT rate in previous years, and noted that

they looked forward to working with the Mayor and the Executive on the budget process and

how the LPT would be used to promote the interests of the people of Limerick.

Councillor Benson stated, for the record, that her Party was not in favour of increasing

the LPT above the base rate.

Proposed by Councillor Collins (M);

Seconded by Councillor Sheahan;

And Resolved:

“That, in accordance with Section 20 of the Finance (Local Property Tax) Act, 2012, as

amended, Limerick City and County Council hereby determines that the basic rate of Local

Property Tax (LPT) should stand varied upwards by fifteen per cent (15%) for the year ending

31st December, 2026, in respect of relevant residential properties situated in the

administrative area of Limerick City and County.”

This concluded the Meeting.

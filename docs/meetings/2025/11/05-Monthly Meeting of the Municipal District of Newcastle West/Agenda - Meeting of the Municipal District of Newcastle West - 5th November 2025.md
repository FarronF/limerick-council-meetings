QUESTIONS

Questions submitted by Councillor J. Scanian

| will ask at the next Meeting (a) what was the average daily usage of Newcastle
West Athletic Track during 2024 and that through the current year to date, (b) the
number of national tracking events hosted in each period in question, (c) what
revenue stream was generated through both periods, (d) what was the actual
running cost of the facility for 2024 as well as in the current year to date.

I will ask at the next Meeting when overgrown trees on (a) the Ashgrove side of
Evergreen Court and (b) those on the Assumpta Park approach to The Archway will
be cut back.

| will ask at the next Meeting for when road junction lining will be undertaken at the
five junctions leading from the Castlemahon to Kilmeedy road as well as those in the
Killeedy to Broadford area.

Questions submitted by Councillor L. Galvin

| will ask at the next Meeting that the Council place (a) Narrow bridge signs at the
bridge next to the old creamery at Devon Road (b) Dangerous bend signs at the bend
next to V94NTX8 Upper Direen, Athea.

| will ask at the next Meeting for a detailed update on the road safety audit for the
unsatisfactory job done from the school to the graveyard at Athea.

| will ask at the next Meeting for (a) A road surface audit to be carried out from the
Church, Abbeyfeale to the old Garda barrack. Please include all manholes, sewerage
connections which have dropped, potholes, grates blocked and locations where the
road has sank (b) Are the NRA and TIl aware of the poor condition of the road, (c)
What short term plans are in place to make Abbeyfeale town passable to motorists.

NOTICES OF MOTION
Notices of Motion submitted by Councillor J. Scanian

twill move at the next Meeting that the rights of way leading from St. Mary's Road to
Coole Lane and that from Upper Maiden Street to The Market Yard be reopened.

| will move at the next Meeting that immediate action be taken to deal with the traffic
flow along Boherbee.

Notices of Motion submitted by Councillor L. Galvin

| will move at the next Meeting that the Council carry out a Road Safety Audit at
Dromtrasna school.

| will move at the next Meeting that Limerick City and County Council call on
Transport Infrastructure Ireland (Tll) to make the necessary funding available
immediately in order that emergency safety works be carried out on the junction at
Devon Road Cross, Templeglantine as soon as possible. In light of the many road
traffic accidents which have occurred at this junction over recent years and given
that a fatality occurred at this site on Saturday last, October 18", 2025, it is
imperative that action is taken forthwith to prevent further injury and loss of life.

Notices of Motion submitted by Councillor M. Collins

| will move at the next Meeting that Brewery Lane, Newcastle West be taken in charge
by Limerick City and County Council. The roadway was to be taken in charge by
Limerick City and County Council a few years ago and why wasn't this completed,

| will move at the next Meeting that Transport infrastructure Ireland be asked to
carry out a Road Safety Audit at Devon Road Cross, Templeglantine on the N21 and
to make necessary safety measures there to prevent further road traffic accidents.

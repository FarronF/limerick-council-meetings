1.To Discuss Mayoral Fund

To Discuss Mayoral Fund towards Christmas lights in Newcastle

West.

At the outset, the Cathaoirleach welcomed everyone to the Special Meeting of the District.

The Cathaoirleach stated that this Meeting is called to discuss the Christmas lighting in

Newcastle West and to a commitment of additional €100,000 given by Mayor Moran prior to

the October Monthly Meeting to assist towards funding for the Christmas lights in Newcastle

West.

The Mayor gave an update to the Members on his understanding of the proposed funding for

the Christmas lights. The Mayor clarified that the additional funding of €100,000 was for a

specific public realm enhancement project in The Square, Newcastle West. However, the

Members did not approve the proposal from a value for money perspective at the last

Meeting. The Members and the Mayor wished to ensure the funding would remain in the

Newcastle West Municipal District.
---
2

Subsequently, the Mayor was informed that €67,753.83 (inclusive of VAT) was allocated from

Limerick City and County Council’s own resources. The Mayor felt that an additional €100,000

was excessive. The Mayor was agreeable to a further €15,000 allocation from the Mayoral

Fund for festoon lighting in Bridge Street, Newcastle West, bringing the total allocation to

€82,753.83.

A discussion ensued around consultation with Members, timeframes and community

engagement. The Members requested that earlier consultation take place in respect of the

Christmas lights programme for all County towns take place by February/March 2026 going

forward.

At the conclusion of the Meeting, it was agreed with the Members in conjunction with the

Mayor that the following would be spent on Christmas lights for Newcastle West in 2025:

(a) €67,753.83 (inclusive of VAT) from Limerick City and County Council’s own resources

(b) €30,000 additional from the Mayoral Funding for festoon lighting to be spent before

the end of November 2025.

(c) The outstanding €70,000 assigned to Newcastle West Public Realm should be

examined in consultation with the local community by the 31st December, 2025.

This concluded the Meeting.

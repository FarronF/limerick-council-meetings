MINUTES OF PROCEEDINGS AT DRAFT BUDGETARY PLAN
MEETING OF THE MUNICIPAL DISTRICT OF NEWCASTLE
WEST HELD IN ARAS WILLIAM SMITH OBRIEN,
NEWCASTLE WEST, CO. LIMERICK, AND ONLINE, ON

WEDNESDAY, 15™ OCTOBER, 2025, AT 10.00 AM.
PRESENT IN THE CHAIR: Councillor M. Collins, Cathaoirleach.

MEMBERS PRESENT:

Councillors Foley, Galvin, Ruddle and Sheahan.

OFFICIALS IN ATTENDANCE:

Mayor of Limerick (Mr. J. Moran), Director, Rural, Culture, Community and Tourism
Development (Mr. S. Duclot), A/Senior Executive Officer, Municipal Districts (Ms. K. White),
Meetings Administrator, Newcastle West (Ms. H. Supple), Senior Executive Engineer,
Newcastle West Municipal District(Mr. B. Noonan), Executive Engineer, Roads (Mr. P. Vallely),
Director of Service, Finance, Economic Development, Digital Services and ICT (Mr. M. White),
Financial Accountant, Finance (Mr. P. Murnane).

APOLOGIES:
Councillor Scanlan.

1. Draft Budgetary Plan 2026

Circulated, Report of Mayor of Limerick dated 8" October, 2025, setting out the background
and legal provisions relating to the Draft Budgetary Plan, along with details of the Draft
Budgetary Plan 2026 for the Municipal District of Newcastle West in the total sum of
€278,600.

Following discussion, Councillor Sheahan proposed that this item would be postponed until
the November Meeting of the District. This was seconded by Councillor Collins and agreed by
all the Members.

This concluded the Meeting.

Signed:
Cathaoirleach

4.

Questions
Question submitted by Councillor Carey

| will ask at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District if funding will be
made available for the collapsed wall at Ballingaddy [Old] Graveyard and, if so, is there a
timeline for commencement of works.

Question submitted by Councillor M. Ryan

| will ask at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District for the number
of people/families classed as homeless/using homeless services in the Cappamore-Kilmallock
Municipal District area.

Question submitted by Councillor M. Ryan

| will ask at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District for an update
on the numbers of qualified applicants on the Housing Waiting List for the Cappamore-
Kilmallock Municipal District and the list of areas of choice of qualified applicants.

Question submitted by Councillor Teefy

| will ask at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District for an update as
to when the installation of Cats Eyes will be completed on the sections of the R512 and R513
roads where they have not yet been installed. The reflective markers are important for road
safety particularly on these very busy rural roads.

Notice of Motions

Notice of Motion submitted by Cllr Conway & Cllr Carey

We will move at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District that an
inspection be carried out at Sherins Cross, Dromin to determine what actions are deemed
necessary to make this crossroads safer.

9. Notice of Motion submitted by Cllr Teefy

| will move at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District that road
resurfacing and improved safety works be carried out on the R514 Cahercorney road.
Sections of this road are in poor condition and safety measures are particularly needed in the
vicinity of Cahercorney Graveyard.

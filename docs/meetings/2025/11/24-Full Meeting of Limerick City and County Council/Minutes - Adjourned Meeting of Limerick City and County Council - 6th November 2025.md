MAYORAL FUND

At the outset, and with the permission of the Príomh Chomhairleoir, a discussion took place

regarding the conduct of Members within the Chamber and creating a respectful

environment. Some Members expressed disappointment and felt that the individuals

responsible should be identified, as they did not wish to be associated with such behaviour.

It was noted that if any Member felt they were being bullied, there were procedures available

to address issues. The Mayor informed the Members that a solution had been identified for

the ongoing CCTV project. Concerns were also raised regarding the funding allocated to

Newcastle West Municipal District from the Mayoral Fund.

On the proposal of Councillor O’Hanlon and seconded by the Príomh Chomhairleoir the

Meeting was adjourned for ten minutes.

On resumption of the Meeting, the Members raised the issue of conduct at meetings.
---
On the proposal of the Mayor and seconded by Councillor Benson the Meeting was adjourned

for ten minutes.

On resumption of the Meeting, some Members expressed concerns in relation to personal

remarks that had been made and stated that such comments were unacceptable. The Mayor

apologised for remarks made, explaining that he had not intended to make them personal

and noting that some comments may have been taken out of context.

On the proposal of the Mayor and seconded by Councillor Gavan the Meeting was adjourned.

This concluded the Meeting.

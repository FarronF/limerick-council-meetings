A Chomhairleoir, a chara,

The Priomh Chomhairleoir, Councillor Dan McSweeney, has called a Special
Meeting of Limerick City and County Council, to be held in the Council Chamber,
Dooradoyle, and Online, on Tuesday, 18" February, 2025, at 4:00 p.m. in order
to pass a Vote of Sympathy on the passing of former Member and Chairman of
Limerick County Council, Eddie Wade, RIP.

ADJOURNED NOTICES OF MOTION

Notices of Motion submitted by Councillor T. Ruddle

| will move at the next Meeting that Limerick City and County Council maintain our
public park/Demesne to a much higher standard for the enjoyment of the public in
terms of grass cutting and hedge cutting.

| will move at the next Meeting that the Square area of Newcastle West is cleaned
and maintained. There is grass growing up through the cobbles, lamp posts, tables
cleaned etc. The Council should have a system in place to clean this area to a high
standard every year.

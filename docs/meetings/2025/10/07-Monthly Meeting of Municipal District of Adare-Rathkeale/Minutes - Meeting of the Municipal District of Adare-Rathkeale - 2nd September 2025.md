**M21 Adare Bypass Project**

Members welcomed Ger Carey, Senior Executive Engineer, Mid West National Road

Design Office to the meeting. It was agreed Item 8 on the Agenda would be taken in

connection with this item.

**8.**

**Question submitted by Councillor B. Collins**

**I will ask at the next Meeting that the Mid West National Road Design Office of**

**Limerick City and County Council provides an update on the 3km extension of the**

**Adare Bypass from the end of the motorway to the start of the current Bypass**

**construction at the access junction opposite the old golf course.**

The Senior Executive Engineer, Mid West National Road Design Office updated Members

on the current status of the M21 Adare Bypass Project. He advised that the current

contractor cannot be used to complete the proposed extra 3km extension. The Council

are currently pursuing other means for delivering this section of the project separately.

Councillor Collins queried if there is a commitment from Government to fund these works.

The Senior Executive Engineer, Mid West National Road Design Office advised this section

of roadway would cost approximately in the region of thirty to forty million euro to

construct with currently no commitment to funding by Central Government. The Senior

Executive Engineer added current indications are positive that funding will be made

available and the Mid West National Road Design Office would have to make an

application on this at the end of 2025. Councillor Collins requested a public statement be

released by the Mid West National Road Design Office to inform the general public of this

update. She further requested a site visit for Members to the Adare Bypass Project be

arranged when feasible. The Senior Executive Engineer, Mid West National Road Design

Office asked the Meetings Administrator to revert with numbers and a suitable date for

this visit and he would arrange same.

**9.**

**Question submitted by Councillor B. Collins**

**I will ask at the next Meeting that Limerick City and County Council provides an update**

**to the Councillors at the next meeting of the Adare/Rathkeale EA on the draft**

**proposed routes which are being considered as a solution to the current NM20 plan**

**that is indicating that all traffic travelling from the southern or Cork direction will have**

**to travel through the village of Patrickswell to access the Dublin (M7) motorway.**
---
5

**REPLY:**

The N/M20 Cork to Limerick Project is being developed by Limerick City

and County Council in conjunction with Cork County Council and Cork City

Council. The project is currently at Phase 3 Design and Environmental

Evaluation. Draft design proposals for the N/M20 Cork to Limerick Project

were presented to the Elected Representatives, Property Owners and the

wider public with a Project Update on the 16th June 2025 as part of a non-

statutory public consultation.

The N/M20 project team provided a detailed briefing on the N/M20 Cork

to Limerick Project to the meeting of the Municipal District of Adare-

Rathkeale on the 8th July 2025 which addressed the ongoing further

design development for local connectivity from the M20 at Attyflin

Junction.

The current proposals for the N/M20 project do not require all traffic

travelling from the southern or Cork direction to travel through the village

of Patrickswell to access the Dublin (M7) motorway. Traffic from Cork

would continue along the proposed M20 which will merge free flow with

the existing M20 at Attyflin. The request for consideration of further local

connectivity at Attyflin is currently being considered by the N/M20 project

team and potential options under consideration are being appraised by

the various environmental and design specialists within the team.

A Special Meeting has been requested by five Elected Members to receive

an update from the N/M20 Design Team on the proposed scheme and to

discuss the above issue. This Special Meeting is likely to be early

September and therefore it would be appropriate to address any queries

regarding the above reply in person at the Special Meeting before the

Elected Members given the close timeframe between the Municipal

District of Adare-Rathkeale Meeting and the proposed Special Meeting.

item 1: Minutes:

The Minutes of the Community, Leisure, Arts, Heritage and Culture Strategic Policy
Committee held on 9" July 2025 were proposed by Cllr. D. Butler and seconded by Mr.
J. Ward and adopted.

Item2: Matters Arising

No matters arising were brought forward.

Item 3: Presentation on the Draft Limerick Local Sports Plan

Mark O'Connell, Director of Repucon Consulting, presented Final Draft of Limerick Local
Sports Plan. Members were informed that the main aim of the plan is to provide
opportunity for every Limerick resident to be active. Mr O’Connell explained extended
consultation process used to ensure, the needs of local communities and stakeholders
are reflected in the plan. Importance of synergy with local, national and international
policy was also noted. Priorities and goals of the Plan were presented to the members,
together with action plan that emerged from them.

Mr. Pete Moles asked if the plan intends provision for collaboration with non-sporting
organisations. Mr O’Connell advised of consultation process being open to all
organisations and reassured, that direction of the plan has wider aspect, including
community, social, cultural and well-being and not limited to sport.

Members acknowledge work that was done and pointed out importance of engagement
with Transition Year students as well as need to support athletic facilities on the north
site of the city.

item 4: Presentation on the Library Tablet Lending Project in partnership with
Vodafone Foundation

Presentation was given by Noreen O'Neill, Senior Executive Librarian on the Library
Tablet Lending Project in partnership with Vodafone Foundation. Senior Executive
Librarian informed members that this Limerick Library program was shortlisted for the
National Age Friendly Award. Main purpose of this project is to increase access to digital
resources and improve digital skeels for older people. This program allows people to loan
a tablet from their local library for extended periods of time. All the devices are loaded
with mobile internet and have pre-installed app’s including digital literacy training,
specially designed for elderly users. Program is very popular and brings very positive
feedback from the users.

Acting Chairperson Cllr A. Talukder congratulated the team on the nomination to the
National Age Friendly Awards and asked where the is the biggest demand to date.

Senior Executive Librarian advised that currently the demand is higher in the county than
city and that the devices can only be loaned to limerick residents.

Item 5: Age Friendly Ireland — National Update

Dara McGuigan, Age Friendly Regional Programme Manager gave an update on the
national Age Friendly Programme. Members were informed of the role of the Shared
Service hosted by Meath County Council, as well as local program structure. Information
was provided on the Healthy Age Friendly Homes Programme that aims to support older
people to remain living in their own homes, or homes appropriate to their needs, for as
long as possible, and to avoid admittance to residential care. The referral process was
explained including contact details for Limerick programme coordinators. Regional
Programme Manager mentioned other programs currently being undertaken by Age
Friendly Ireland nationally and locally, as well as forthcoming events including the
National Age Friendly Awards.

Acting Chairperson Cllr A. Talukder asked for contact details for to the Limerick Healthy
Age Friendly Homes Programme coordinators to be shared with all the members. It was
agreed that the presentation including contact to the coordinators will be circulated to
all.

Ms M. Hurley asked if Age Friendly Homes team would have a list of trusted tradesmen
available that could be shared with elderly people often struggling to find appropriate
help in fixing house maintenance issues.

Mr McGuigan advised that he is not aware of any tradesman list, however advised that
Healthy Age Friendly Homes Programme can support homeowners in specific services
for example home adaptations needed after hospitalisation.

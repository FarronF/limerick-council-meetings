2. Declaration of interest

The Meetings Administrator stated that, in line with Part 15 of the Local Government Act,
2001, as amended, and the Code of Conduct, she was inviting those present to notify the
Cathaoirleach of any declaration of interest in any item due for discussion at the Meeting.
No such interest was declared.

3. Deputation

The Chairman welcomed the Deputation from Bruff -Dromin Athlacca— Ballinlee Community
Action (Marie-Clare Power, John Quirke, Bette Browne, Biddy Hayes and Marianne Nagle.

The Deputation spokesperson, John Quirke, proceeded with the Groups presentation,
explaining that the group were seeking the support and guidance from the Municipal
District members on the matter of wind turbine development in their community. They
asked that where possible, that Limerick City and County Council work with the Department
of Housing Local Government and Heritage to influence the wind farm development
process, to ensure it aligns with the needs of local families, environment and written
statements of Limerick City and County Councils Development Plan 2022 -2028.

Following this presentation the group then withdrew from the meeting.

Discussion ensued among the Elected Members following the withdrawal of the Deputation.
It was agreed to send a request the Environment & Climate Action Department to provide a
presentation to members on Limerick City and County Councils Wind Energy Policy.

4. Request for Deputation to attend.

Following discussion among members, the Members agreed to the request from Limerick
and Clare Education and Training to attend the October Meeting of Cappamore Kilmallock
Municipal District.

recreation park in a suitable area in the town of Kilmallock be re-allocated to road

Questions

6. Question submitted by Councillor Teefy

| will ask at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District for an update
on when the Bus Stops and Bus Shelters at Holycross and Boher will be completed.

Reply:

The design for the bus stops and shelters for Boher has been completed by the Limerick
Active travel Office. One bus shelter will be installed before the end of 2025. The second
shelter will not be installed until 2026 due to supply issues. It should be noted that the
Limerick Active Travel office will need to get formal approval from TI! for the bus shelters
installation and this process is currently underway.

The preliminary design of proposed new official bus stops and shelters in Holycross is
progressing. Land acquisition will be required to deliver the bus stops at the existing
crossroads. The Limerick Active Travel Office will be engaging a Consultant shortly to
prepare the final design. Subject to funding and landowner agreement, it is proposed to
deliver the Holycross works next year

7. Question submitted by Councillor Conway

| will ask at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District for an update
on traffic management in Sarsfield St., Kilmallock.

Reply:

Following an examination of Sarsfield Street, it has been determined that the introduction
of a pedestrian crossing in the vicinity of Kings Castle/Kings Castle car park/Merchant’s
House would be beneficial for vulnerable road users.

The location of the crossing has not been determined as the proposed development of the
Merchants House site would impact the position of the crossing. A decision on the
progression of the Merchant's House project is expected shortly. If the proposed
development progresses, the Consultants design brief would be amended to include for a
pedestrian crossing on Sarsfield Street.

if the Merchant's House project does not proceed in the short term, the Roads Engineer will
finalise a design and location for a new pedestrian crossing and make a submission for the
Dept. of Transport to consider funding its construction through their Low-Cost Safety
Programme.

An update on Sarsfield Street will be made to the October meeting of the Cappamore-
Kilmallock Municipal District

Notice of Motions

9 - Notice of Motion Councillor PJ Carey

| will move at the next Cappamore/Kilmallock Municipal District meeting that additional car
parking spaces be planned for and costings provided at Radharc na Cille Estate, Kilmallock
[as per case number: 1037450] with a view to be included in the 2026 Schedule of Works.

The Notice of Motion was proposed by Cllr Carey, seconded by Cllr Stokes, and agreed.

Reply:

The Cappamore-Kilmallock Roads Engineer will review the existing car parking arrangements
in the Radharc Na Cille estate, Kilmallock and determine if additional car parking can be
marked on the existing hard surface areas.

Any additional parking outside of this measure would result in existing green areas within
the state being reduced, thereby reducing the amenity value for residents.

It should be noted that the current City and County development plan identifies a need of a
minimum green space within housing estates of 15%.

It should be further noted that the cost of constructing additional car parking in the estate
could only be considered through an allocation from GMA or a specific allocation from the
Development Fund. The other funding sources included in the Schedule of Municipal District
Works would not contain a budget for such works.”

11— Notice of Motion — Councilor Conway

| will move at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District that CCTV be
provided for the Kilmallock River Park and Glenfield Housing Estate.

Councilor Conway advised the meeting that, in proposing his motion, that he was making
specific reference to the elderly residents of the estates of River Park and Glenfield who are
impacted by anti-social behavior in their estates. He asked that Limerick City and County
Council take action under the estate management Department to identify the locations in
conjunction with An Garda Siochana at which these issues are most acute.

Reference was made to the earlier meeting of the day with the District Superintendent who
had requested issues such as these be reported to the Gardai.

This motion was seconded by Councillor Stokes and agreed.
Reply:

The Council fully recognizes the importance of public safety and community wellbeing, and
we appreciate the concerns raised in relation to this area. However, the legal and
operational framework governing Community CCTV in public spaces has changed
significantly in recent years. The introduction of GDPR in 2018, followed by a comprehensive

audit by the Data Protection Commissioner, has resulted in much stricter controls on the
use of surveillance technologies by public bodies.

At present, only An Garda Siochana and Local Authorities are permitted to install CCTV in
public spaces, and even then, only under specific legislative provisions such as the Garda
Siochana Act, the Circular Economy Act, and the Garda Siochana (Recording Devices) Act
2023. Any proposed installation must be authorized by the Garda Commissioner and must
meet stringent statutory and data protection requirements. This includes demonstrating
that the system is necessary, proportionate, and justified.

The process for preparing and submitting an application is extensive, typically requiring
12-18 months of preparatory work followed by a further potential 12+ months awaiting
approval. Emergency installations are not feasible under the current framework.

Limerick City and County Council currently operates a comprehensive community CCTV
network across the City and County. More significantly, the Council is currently operating
within a constrained environment at the moment. At present, our focus must remain on
sustaining and supporting the existing community CCTV infrastructure. Under these
circumstances, we are not in a position to progress new installations at this time.

Please be assured that the request has been noted and will be retained for future
consideration should the regulatory or funding environment change.

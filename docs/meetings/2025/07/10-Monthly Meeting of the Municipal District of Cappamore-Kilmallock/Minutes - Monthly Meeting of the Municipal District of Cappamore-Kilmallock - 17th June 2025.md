3. Erlars Gate Theatre Presentation

The Cathaoirleach welcomed Ms. Ciara Moloney from Friars Gate Theatre to the Meeting
and thanked her for attending. She proceeded to give a PowerPoint presentation to the
Elected Members on the activities of the Friars Gate Theatre.

She outlined how the theatre was a 130 seat venue and was the only full time theatre in the
County Limerick/North Cork area. The theatre hosted many events, including workshops,
drama classes, art exhibitions, drama camps and screened movies and documentaries. She
explained that the theatre was once a cinema and had been converted in to a theatre by the
local drama society in the late 1990's.

She also confirmed that the theatre was non-profit and that its annual turnover was
approximately €200k (€196,917.67 in 2024). The primary support to the theatres activities
came from Pobal (CSP 2023-2027 for Wages & DAF & CCIF Projects), Limerick City and
County Council (Projects & GMA), Creative Ireland (Projects), Ballyhoura Development
(Projects — Leader (in the past), the Arts Council (Annual activities funding up until to 2024).

She thanked the Members for the GMA funding received previously from the Elected
Members of the Cappamore-Kilmallock District and she looked forward to continued
support from the District and Limerick City & County Council in the future.

The Members thanked Ms Moloney for her presentation and on the work carried out to
date. They expressed disappointment at the Arts Council lack of funding in 2025 and agreed
to support any proposals to meet the Minister and officials in the Dept. to advocate for Arts
Council funding support, given the important role the theatre played in County Limerick.

5. Deputation from Hospital Family Resource Centre

The Cathaoirleach welcomed the members of the Delegation from Hospital Family Resource
Centre to the Meeting. They outlined how the Mayor, following a recent visit to their
Resource Centre, had advised them to give a presentation to the Elected Members of the
District to update them on their activities.

They outlined that the Resource Centre was established in 2003, and serves the rural
communities & towns of Hospital, Ballylanders, Galbally, Knockainey and Kilteely primarily.
Current staff numbers comprise 13 full time staff as well as 60 volunteers. They are one of
121 Family Resource Centre’s which form a national community development and family
support programme throughout Ireland.

They explained how their primary asks under the Mayoral Programme Included:
Social inclusion and partnership working: Training support in softer skills and wellbeing;
Community hub and rural amenities supports; digital connectivity.

Mental Health initiatives: On the spot counselling and support; support for organisation’s
and community groups supporting better mental health.

Grow peer support networks: Use the Mayoral Social Entrepreneurship Fund to finance
mental health project.

Preventative Health: Support for community based volunteer organisation's conducting
activities which lead to better health outcomes in their communities.

again on the 22™ September 2025. This initiative is aimed at addressing the impact physical

8. 2025 National Famine Commemoration Ceremony

The Cathaoirleach welcomed the City and County Librarian and the Senior Executive Librarian
to the Meeting. The City and County Librarian proceeded to give a PowerPoint presentation
to the Elected Members to update them on the outcome of the 2025 National Famine
Commemoration Ceremony which had taken place in Kilmallock on the 17" May, 2025.

The ceremony was attended by President Michael D. Higgins; Patrick O’Donovan, Minister
for Arts, Culture, Communications, Media and Sport; John Moran, Mayor of Limerick, along
with other dignitaries and invited guests and was very well received.

The Members noted that the Ceremony had been a resounding success and very well
organised. They agreed that it was a significant honour for the District, and Limerick, that
the ceremony was held in Kilmallock, and that the weather helped as it was so good on the
day. They thanked all involved in Limerick City & County Council, and the various
Government Departments, along with local groups, who had all worked so hard and
contributed to what had been a very successful national ceremony.

Questions

10. Question submitted by Councillor Conway

| will ask at the next Monthly Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District if a
one-way traffic system is being considered by Limerick City & County Council for Millmount
Estate, Kilmallock.

Reply:
The Municipal District does not have any proposals to revise the existing traffic management
arrangements in the Millmount Estate. If the residents collectively have a request for the

introduction of a one-way system, the Roads Engineer will review the request and determine
an appropriate course of action.

11. Question submitted by Councillor Conway

| will ask at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District for an update
on flooding issues at Maiden Hall, Effin, Kilmallock, Co. Limerick.

Reply:

The Cappamore-Kilmallock Road Maintenance Team has assessed the existing drainage
network, and has determined that the network is clear and operating adequately. There is an
adjacent drainage network within the functional area of Cork County Council that should be
examined to determine if this is having an impact on Maiden Hall. The Roads Engineer for the
Cappamore-Kilmallock MD is liaising with his counterpart in Cork County Council to resolve
the drainage issues in Maiden Hall.

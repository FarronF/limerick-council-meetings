MINUTES OF PROCEEDINGS OF ANNUAL MEETING OF THE MUNICIPAL DISTRICT OF
CAPPAMORE-KILMALLOCK HELD IN ARAS MAINCHIN SEOIGE AND ON-LINE, ON TUESDAY
17™ JUNE 2025 AT 2.00PM.

PRESENT IN THE CHAIR:

Councillor M. Ryan, Cathaoirleach.

MEMBERS PRESENT:

Councillors Carey, Conway, O’Sullivan, E. Ryan, Stokes and Teefy.

| INA DANCE:

Meetings Administrator (Mr. M. Leahy); Senior Executive Engineer (Mr. T. McKechnie);
A/Senior Executive Officer (Ms. K. White), Clerical Officer (Ms. Helen O'Neill).

Apologies: A/Director of Services (Mr. H McGrath)

1. Welcome

The Cathaoirleach welcomed everyone to the meeting and thanked the Members for their
support and hard work in the activities of the Municipal District during the previous 12
months. He noted that the Members had worked well together on behalf of the District and
that this was key to delivering on the many objectives across the Municipal District.

He also thanked the management and staff of Limerick City & County Council for their
engagement during the previous 12 months. In particular he acknowledged the work of the
staff, both indoor and outdoor, working in the Municipal District and thanked them for their
efforts during the past year. He outlined how working on behalf of the Municipal District
had been an honour and privilege. He also wished to acknowledge his Leas Cathaoirleach,
Cllr Conway, for his support and assistance during the year.

The Members congratulated Cllr Ryan on his year as Cathaoirleach, and acknowledged the
fair and impartial manner he conducted meetings and discussions with the Members and

The outgoing Cathaoirleach, Councillor M Ryan invited nominations for the Office of
Cathaoirleach for the coming year.

Proposed by Councillor O'Sullivan;
Seconded by Councillor N Stokes;

“That Councillor Greg Conway be appointed Cathaoirleach of the Municipal District of
Cappamore-Kilmallock to hold office until the next Annual Meeting or until a successor has
been appointed”.

As there were no other nominations, the Elected Members declared Councillor Conway
elected as Cathaoirleach of the Municipal District of Cappamore-Kilmallock.

Cllr Conway stated that it was an honour for both himself and his family to be elected as
Cathaoirleach of the Municipal District. He thanked the outgoing Cathaoirleach for his
guidance and assistance during his first year as a newly elected Councillor, which he said
was a learning process for all new Councillors in particular.

He also thanked the management and staff of Limerick City & County Council for their help
and assistance during the previous 12 months, and he looked forward to working with
everyone as Cathaoirleach in the year ahead.

3. To elect a Leas-Cathaoirleach of the Municipal District of Cappamore-Kilmallock to
hold office until the next Annual Meeting or until a successor has been elected.

The newly elected Cathaoirleach then invited nominations for the office of Leas-Cathaoirleach
of the Municipal District of Cappamore-Kilmallock for the forthcoming year.

Proposed by Councillor Stokes:
Seconded by Councillor M. Ryan:

“That Councillor O'Sullivan be appointed Leas-Cathaoirleach of the Municipal District of
Cappamore-Kilmallock to hold office until the next Annual Meeting or until a successor has
been appointed”.

As there were no other nominations, the Cathaoirleach declared Councillor O’Sullivan
elected as Leas-Cathaoirleach of the Municipal District of Cappamore-Kilmallock and wished
him well for the forthcoming year.

The Members congratulated the newly elected Cathaoirleach and Leas Cathaoirleach on
their election and wished them well for the twelve months ahead. They agreed on the
importance of all working constructively together in the Municipal District.

A suggestion was made by some Members that in the fifth year of the term, that
consideration could be given to electing a Cathaoirleach outside of current political
arrangements. However, some Members noted that the election of Cathaoirligh was based
on the results of the 2024 Local Elections and that the current arrangement for nomination
and election of positions would stand.

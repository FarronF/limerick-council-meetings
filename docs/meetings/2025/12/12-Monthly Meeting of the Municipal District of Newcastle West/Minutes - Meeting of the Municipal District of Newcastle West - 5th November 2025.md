disappointment at this proposal.

covering. Following discussion, it was agreed that the Director would seek clarification on this

matter and revert to the Members at the next Meeting of the District.

Reference was also made to the provision of €30,000 from the Mayoral Funding to be spent

on festoon lighting before the end of November, 2025.

***I will move at the next Meeting that Limerick City and County Council call on Transport***

***Infrastructure Ireland (TII) to make the necessary funding available immediately in order***

***that emergency safety works be carried out on the junction at Devon Road Cross,***

***Templeglantine as soon as possible. In light of the many road traffic accidents which have***

***occurred at this junction over recent years and given that a fatality occurred at this site on***

REPLY:

19. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor F. Foley

***I will move at the next Meeting that funding be provided for (a) Works on the bridge at***

***Secondglass, Mountcollins, (b) Bridge at Meenkilly, Abbeyfeale and (c) The stone walls at***

***Fealesbridge, Abbeyfeale.***

The Motion was seconded by Councillor Collins.

REPLY:

**(a)**An engineering inspection was undertaken at the bridge at Secontglas, Mountcollins. This

inspection has identified that the bridge requires extensive rehabilitation works to the

damaged section of wall and the existing composite steel/concrete deck which needs to

be replaced with pre cast concrete slabs. Due to lead in time for the supply of these pre

cast units, Limerick City and County Council bridge contractors will mobilise to site in early

January 2026 to undertake all works required which are estimated to take from 8-12

weeks to complete. The nature of the works along with the constraints of the road layout

will require a full road closure for the duration of the works.

**(b)**The bridge at Meenkilly, Abbeyfeale will be inspected to determine its condition.

Following the findings of the inspection, the bridge will be added to the list of bridge

structures requiring rehabilitation works within Limerick City and County Council, for

consideration for inclusion in the annual bridge rehabilitation programme in future years,

based on priority of need.

**(c)**The stone walls at Fealesbridge, Abbeyfeale have been inspected. The sections of

damaged wall do not form part of the bridge structure as they are located on the approach

walls to the bridge abutments and lie outside of the extents of the bridge structure. In this

regard it will not be considered for repair under the bridge rehabilitation works

programme. The roads operations team in Newcastle West will review possible funding

streams with a view to carrying out works in 2026.

In proposing the Motion, Councillor Foley referred to the condition of the bridges at the above

locations and to a request for funding to repair these bridges. Councillor Foley expressed

urgency in particular to the carrying out of repair works to the Fealesbridge in Abbeyfeale.

NOTICES OF MOTION
Notices of Motion submitted by Councillor T. Ruddle

| will move at the next Meeting (a) What is the up-to-date position with Visce
Eireann on new connections to the sewer at Newcastle West, (b) Have we obtained
extra capacity for the construction of new houses in Newcastle West, and (c) When
will there be a full upgrade on the sewer.

| will move at the next Meeting that the bollards at Calvary graveyard be changed to
a plastic type bollard as they are leading to a large amount of damage to cars who
park there as they are too low for people to see them.

Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor J. Scanian

| will move at the next Meeting that the public lighting on the westward bound bus
stop fronting the Courtenay Lodge be upgraded in the interest of safety.

Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor L. Galvin

| will move at the next Meeting for a pedestrian crossing to serve both the school and
the playground in Carrigkerry village and flexible bollards to be placed on the footpath
to prevent parking.

Notices of Motion submitted by Councillor M. Collins

| will move at the next Meeting that a pedestrian crossing and bollards be installed at
the new playground in Carrigkerry which will also act as a safety measure for the
National School.

| will move at the next Meeting that a safety crossing be installed at Assumpta Park,
Newcastle West to aid older persons, disability sector and children crossing in safety.

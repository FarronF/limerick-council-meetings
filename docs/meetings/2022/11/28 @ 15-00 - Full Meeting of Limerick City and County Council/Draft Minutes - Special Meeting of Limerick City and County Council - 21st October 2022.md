MINUTES OF PROCEEDINGS AT SPECIAL MEETING OF LIMERICK CITY AND

COUNTY COUNCIL HELD IN THE COUNCIL CHAMBER, DOORADOYLE,

LIMERICK, AND ONLINE ON FRIDAY 21ST OCTOBER, 2022 AT 3PM.

PRESENT IN THE CHAIR: Councillor F. Foley, Mayor.

MEMBERS PRESENT:

Councillors Benson, Butler, Carey, Collins (B), Collins (J), Collins (M), Daly, Donegan, Galvin,

Hartigan, Keary, Kiely, Kilcoyne, Leddin, McSweeney, Mitchell, Murphy, Novak Uí Chonchúir,

O’Brien, O’Donoghue, O’Donovan, O’Hanlon, O’Sullivan, Ruddle, Ryan (E), Ryan (M), Scanlan,

Secas, Sheahan (J), Sheahan (K), Sheahan (M), Sheehan (C), Slattery, Talukder, Teefy, Teskey.

Apologies from Councillor Collopy

OFFICIALS IN ATTENDANCE:

Chief Executive (Dr. P. Daly), Deputy Chief Executive and Director, Support Services (Mr. S.

Coughlan), Director, Housing (Ms. C. Curley), Director, Planning, Environment and Place

Making (Ms. N. Gallagher), A/Director, Economic Development and Enterprise (Mr. V.

Murray), Meetings Administrator (Ms. L. Flannery), Administrative Officer, Corporate Services

(Mr. M. Leahy), Senior Staff Officer, Corporate Services (Ms. A. Foley).

Apologies from Director, National and Regional Shared Services, Mr. K. Lehane.

The Mayor opened the meeting by welcoming the Members to the Special Meeting that was

called to fill the casual vacancy on the Council after the sad passing of Councillor Jerry

O’Dea, RIP.

1.Support Services

(a)

Filling of Casual Vacancy

Already circulated, report of the Senior Executive Officer, Corporate Services, Governance

and Customer Services, dated 14th October, 2022.

The Meetings Administrator advised that the Council was required to consider filling of casual

vacancy on the Council consequent on the passing of Councillor Jerry O’Dea, RIP, on 25th June.

She confirmed that the procedure was set out in Section 19 of the Local Government Act,

2001, as amended, a copy of which had been circulated to Members. She also confirmed that

all the required documentation in relation to the co-option had been received and was in

order.

Proposed by Councillor M. Collins;

Seconded by Councillor C. Slattery;

<span data-pagefind-ignore>[[PAGE_END:1]]</span>

2

“That, Mr. Joe Pond, Annacotty, Castletroy, Limerick, be co-opted to the Council to fill the

casual vacancy consequent on the passing of Councillor Jerry O’Dea, RIP, on 25th June.”

As there were no other nominations, the Mayor declared Mr. Joe Pond co-opted as a Member

of the Council for the Metropolitan District of Limerick and welcomed him as a Member of

the Council.

Councillor Pond then took his seat in the Chamber.

The Mayor congratulated Councillor Joe Pond on his co-option and extended a very warm

welcome to him. He acknowledged that he had previously sat as a Member of the Council

and looked forward to working with him again. The Members congratulated and welcomed

Councillor Pond back to the chamber. They noted that it was a historic first as Cllr Pond was

the first Elected Member to be co-opted twice to Limerick City & County Council, having been

previously co-opted in 2016. They acknowledged his experience in politics and the work that

he does in his local community. They wished him well in his new role and noted it was a great

day for his family and his community.

The Deputy Chief Executive, on behalf of Management and Staff, joined in congratulating

Councillor Pond and welcomed him back to Limerick City and County Council saying that he,

and all the Staff, looked forward to working with him again.

Councillor Pond addressed the Meeting and thanked the Mayor, Elected Members and Chief

Executive for their warm welcome. Councillor Pond acknowledged and thanked Councillor

M. Collins for proposing his co-option and Councillor Slattery for seconding the nomination.

He added that he was honoured and humbled to be selected by his Fianna Fáil party for co-

option onto the Council, and looked forward to working as an Elected Member again.

Councillor Pond continued by paying tribute to his predecessor, the late Councillor Jerry

O’Dea, acknowledging all the hard work he did and stated that his loss was still felt by many

people. He concluded by thanking his family for their support and looked forward to working

with the Elected Members and officials in the future.

(b)

Filling of Vacancies on Strategic Policy Committees

The Meetings Administrator advised that the Council was required to consider the nomination

of a replacement to the following Strategic Policy Committees consequent on the passing of

Councillor Jerry O’Dea, RIP:

(i)

Economic Development, Enterprise and Planning Strategic Policy

Committee.

(ii)

Community, Leisure and Culture Strategic Policy Committee.

<span data-pagefind-ignore>[[PAGE_END:2]]</span>

3

 **Economic Development, Enterprise and Planning Strategic Policy Committee.**

On the proposal of Councillor Collins (M) seconded by Councillor Ryan (E), it was agreed that

Councillor Joe Pond would be appointed to replace Councillor Jerry O’Dea, RIP, on the

Economic Development, Enterprise and Planning Strategic Policy Committee

 **Community, Leisure and Culture Strategic Policy Committee**

On the proposal of Councillor Collins (M) seconded by Councillor Ryan (E), it was agreed that

Councillor Joe Pond would be appointed to replace Councillor Jerry O’Dea, RIP, on the

Community, Leisure and Culture Strategic Policy Committee.

This concluded the Meeting.

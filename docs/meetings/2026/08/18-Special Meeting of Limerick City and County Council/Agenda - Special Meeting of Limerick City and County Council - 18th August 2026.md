17th August, 2026.

**To: The Mayor, Príomh Chomhairleoir and**

**Each Member of Limerick City and County Council**

A Chomhairleoir, a chara,

The Príomh Chomhairleoir, Councillor Tom Ruddle has called a Special Meeting of Limerick

City and County Council to be held in the Council Chamber, Dooradoyle and Online,on

**Tuesday, 18th** **August, 2026 at 9:00 a.m.** in order to pass a Vote of Sympathy on the passing of

former Member and Cathaoirleach of Limerick County Council, Michael O’Kelly, RIP.

Is mise le meas,

**Aedín Foley,**

**Meetings Administrator.**

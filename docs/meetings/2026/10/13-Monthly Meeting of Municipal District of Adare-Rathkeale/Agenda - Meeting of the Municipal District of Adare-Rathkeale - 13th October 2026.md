QUESTIONS
Question submitted by Councillor J. O’Donoghue

| will ask at the next Meeting that Limerick City and County Council provide copies of all
completed wind-farm noise survey and monitoring reports and to publish these reports on
the Council’s website, or otherwise make them readily available to members of the public
on request, as environmental records.

Question submitted by Councillor J.O’Donoghue

| will ask at the next Meeting that Limerick City and County Council confirm the number of
wind-farm noise survey reports currently held by the Council, the wind farms to which they
relate, and the dates on which the surveys were undertaken, in table format.

If the Council considers that any such reports, or parts thereof, cannot be disclosed, please
identify the legal basis for withholding them.

Question submitted by Councillor T. Harti

| will ask at the next Meeting, further to the resolution adopted by Limerick City and County
Council in September 2025 concerning the referral of open and future wind-farm noise and
shadow-flicker complaints to the HSE for investigation, to provide confirmation:

Questions

4. Question submitted by Councillor Carey

| will ask at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District if,
on foot of surveys undertaken from my January 2026 Motion, funding will be
sought for low level wildlife-friendly lighting at the West Wall Walkway,
Kilmallock.

5. Question submitted by Councillor Stokes

| will ask at the next meeting of the Cappamore - Kilmallock Municipal District
what concessions or remedies, if any, are available under procurement rules
that Limerick City and County Council can apply in relation to contractors
contracted following a tendering process. When contractors with machinery,
etc. tendered into the local authority last January, diesel was 90c a litre and it is
now €1.62 (bulk price). As seen in the recent severe weather event, contractor
machines are covering a lot of area at a loss, and these contractors have been
providing services to the local authority for the last two decades.

6. Question submitted by Councillor Stokes

| will ask at the next meeting of the Cappamore -Kilmallock Municipal District
for an update on the replacement of the housing technician in the Municipal
District Office in Kilmallock.

| will ask at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District for
an update on the proposed pedestrian crossing and bus shelters at Holycross,
Bruff and a timeline as to when these works will be delivered.

8. Question submitted by Councillor Conway

| will ask at the next Meeting of the Cappamore — Kilmallock Municipal District
for clarification as to what meetings have taken place to date between
management of Irish Rail and Limerick City & County Council in relation to the
possible construction of a footbridge at the railway bridge, Railway Road,
Kilmallock R512, and also advise if any future meetings are planned regarding
same.

10.

**TO: THE CATHAOIRLEACH AND EACH MEMBER OF THE HOME AND SOCIAL DEVELOPMENT**

**STRATEGIC POLICY COMMITTEE**

A meeting of Limerick City and County Council Home and Social Development Strategic Policy

Committee will be held in the Council Chamber, Dooradoyle and online on MS Teams on 8th October

2026 at 3pm – 5pm.

**AGENDA**

1. Confirm Minutes of the most recent meeting held on the 25th June 2026

2. Matters arising from the minutes

3. Home and Social Development SPC Meeting dates for 2026/2027

4. Short Update on Rightsizing Voids

5. Update on review of Tenant Handbook

6. Presentation on the Colbert Quarter Development and HIIF allocation

7. Presentation on the Housing Delivery Action Plan 2027-2030

8. Update on Home and Social Development SPC Work Programme 2025-2029

9. **Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor M. Ryan (to be referred to the Home and Social**

**Development Strategic Policy Committee)**

I will move at the next Meeting that Limerick City and County Council and Mayor Moran,

along with the Department of Housing, would consider options under the first home scheme

of developing serviced site estates throughout the city and county towns and villages

whereby the developed site becomes the equity portion or part thereof in assisting people

to purchase their first home.

Signed: Cllr. Noreen Stokes, Chairperson, Limerick City and County Council

***2.*Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor G. Ward** ***(referred from July Plenary***

***Meeting)***

I will move at the next Meeting of Limerick City and County Council that this Council

acknowledges the critical need to improve and protect road users with the necessary

clearance for wide agricultural machinery. I would like to propose that all village and

town centres under the renewal schemes would adopt a hatched road marking in

the centre of the road to allow for the need of a safe passage for larger vehicles. It is

the utmost importance for the protection of pedestrians to stop machinery having to

mount or encroach on pedestrian/cycleways to pass through.

***3.*Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor U. Gavan (*referred from September***

***Plenary Meeting)***

I will move at the next Meeting of Limerick City and County Council that this Council

in conjunction with the National Transport Authority and Transport Infrastructure

Ireland carry out a full review of our motorway access and junction access within the

metropolitan area in light of the increased car usage with the view to opening access

to Carew Park to traffic.

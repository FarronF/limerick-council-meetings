NOTICES OF MOTION

Notices of Motion submitted by Councillor L. Galvin

| will move at the next Meeting that Colbert Road and the McMahon car park plans
be drawn up and costing to be done, and that this be delivered before we progress
the Main Street works as agreed in the Part 8 process.

Notices of Motion submitted by Councillor L. Galvin

| will move at the next Meeting that, where retention planning permission is sought
for a residential development and the size of the development is below the
threshold for development contributions, it should be exempt from any levy. This
exemption should form part of the Development Contribution Scheme and,
therefore, a new Development Contribution Scheme would need to be adopted.

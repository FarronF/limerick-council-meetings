| will move at the next Meeting for a safe pedestrian access to the Greenway at Murphy's
bend, Ballaugh.

The Motion was seconded by Councillor Foley.

In proposing the Motion, Councillor Galvin stated that a Meeting is due to take place shortly
with the engineer and landowner regarding this matter.

REPLY:

It has been recommended in a safety audit that this unauthorised access be closed as it
presents a hazard to road users due to inadequate sightlines in either direction as well as a
lack of road verge to provide refuge to walkers and other vulnerable road users.

We have explored the possibility of creating a proper access point at this location and have
completed a topographical survey of the adjacent lands to establish line and level, as well as
establishing adjacent land ownership details, all of which are in private ownership. A number
of design options have been considered but given the pronounced horizontal and vertical
alignment of the ‘humpback’ bridge, adjacent land would need to be acquired by Limerick
City and County Council, in order to establish a safe access to and from the public road. Initial
discussions with landowners regarding the transfer of lands have thus proved unsuccessful.
The lands themselves are heavily overgrown given their proximity to the bridge.

We intend to have further engagement with landowners to explore options on how best to
address this issue. In the meantime, members of the public are advised not to use the current
route until such time as a viable alternative has been put in place.

23. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor M. Collins

1 will move at the next Meeting that Limerick City and County Council put in place a park
ranger in the Demesne, Newcastle West.

The Motion was seconded by Councillor Ruddle.

In proposing the Motion, Councillor Collins referred to the seventy-seven acres of woodland,
parkland and playing pitches at the Demesne and highlighted the significant amount of illegal
dumping and antisocial behaviour occurring in the area. Following discussion, it was agreed
that the Director would liaise with the relevant Directorate regarding the provision of funding

Creating the position of a Park Ranger at the Demesne, Newcastle West would greatly aid the
upkeeping of the park as well as improving security for all park users.

its existing staff since we are already severely over-stretched fulfilling existing commitments
and duties.

A business case would need to be made for this new position as well as for required facilities

in the vicinity (dry room and toilet). This would also need to be allowed for in the annual
budgetary process before this business case can be progressed.

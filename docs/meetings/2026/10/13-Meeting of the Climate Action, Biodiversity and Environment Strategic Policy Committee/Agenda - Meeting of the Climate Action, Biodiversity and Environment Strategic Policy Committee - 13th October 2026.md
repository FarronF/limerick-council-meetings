**To: The Chairperson and Each Member of the Climate Action, Biodiversity and Environment Strategic**

**Policy Committee of Limerick City & County Council**

You are invited to attend a blended Meeting of the Climate Action, Biodiversity and Environment Strategic

Policy Committee to be held in the Council Chamber, Dooradoyle and online on MS Teams on Tuesday 13

October 2026 at 2:30pm. The business to be transacted is set out in the appended agenda.

**Signed:**

**CLLR JOE LEDDIN**

**CHAIRPERSON OF THE CLIMATE ACTION, BIODIVERSITY AND ENVIRONMENT**

**STRATEGIC POLICY COMMITTEE**

**Agenda**

1.To consider the adoption of the Minutes of the Climate Action, Biodiversity and Environment

Strategic Policy Committee that was held on 07 May 2026.

2.Waste Management Feasibility Study – Final recommendations – Arlene Mellett, Senior Executive

Engineer/Sinéad Hourigan, Administrative Officer, Environment Rural Water and Climate Action

3.District Heating Update - Aidan Finn, Senior Engineer, Environment Rural Water and Climate Action

4.Limerick City Centre and Environs Blue Green Ring Masterplan - Aidan Finn, Senior Engineer,

Environment Rural Water and Climate Action

5.Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor O. O’Sullivan *(referred from May Metropolitan Meeting)* - I

will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that this Council create a Dog

Park Policy for the city.

6.Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor S. Hickey O’Mara *(referred from September Metropolitan*

*Meeting)* - I will move at the next Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that measures are

swiftly taken to improve public lighting in Shelbourne Park, thereby helping to address safety

concerns among members of the public, particularly as we enter the darker half of the year and that

an audit of lighting in all public parks across the Metropolitan District be undertaken*.*

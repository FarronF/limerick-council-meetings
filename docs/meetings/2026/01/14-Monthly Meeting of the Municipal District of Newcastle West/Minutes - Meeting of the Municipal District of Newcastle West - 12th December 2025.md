5. Transportation and Mobility Directorate

To Receive Update from the Mid West National Road Design Office
on safety measures at the Devon Road Cross, Templeglantine, on
the N21.

Councillor Collins welcomed the item on the agenda. The Senior Executive Engineer discussed
the option that was presented to Transport Infrastructure Ireland in November for a
staggered junction and that a case is being made for funding for traffic calming measures at
this location. The Senior Executive Engineer reiterated that the recommendation has gone to
Transport Infrastructure Ireland to assess this option as it addressed the main issue of
sightlines. He stated that if approved consultants will be appointed to progress the scheme.

Councillor Sheahan raised the issue of access to the Tournafulla road at Buckley's Cross. In
replying, the Senior Executive Engineer stated that as part of the staggered junction, traffic
calming measures would be considered at this location.

Following discussion, it was agreed that Transport Infrastructure Ireland (Til) is reviewing the
option and that following a response from the TIl which is due in January, an update will be
given by the Mid West National Road Design Office on this matter.

Dear Councillor Ruddle,

| am writing to you regarding your queries in relation to the wastewater infrastructure in
Newcastlewest, Co. Limerick. Your Uisce Eireann reference number for same was 41682/96990,
which was raised on the 14/11/2025.

| understand this issue has been a source of concern for you and your constituents.
The Management Department has provided the following responses to your queries:

Q1. What is the up-to-date position with Uisce Eireann on new connections to the sewer at
Newcastle West.

Response:

Newcastlewest Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) is identified as being at capacity in the 2024
Wastewater Capacity Register. Uisce Eireann is progressing a major upgrade and expansion of
Newcastlewest WWTP to provide for the long-term needs of Newcastlewest. The project is currently
at Stage 2 Preliminary Business Case and it will take a number of years before the project has
completed the statutory approvals, the tender process and construction.

Q2. Have we obtained extra capacity for the construction of new houses in Newcastle West?
Response:

Uisce Eireann is examining options to provide some additional capacity for new connections in
advance of the major upgrade . Uisce Eireann will consider proposals from developers to provide
temporary onsite treatment to discharge standards acceptable to Uisce Eireann and the EPA in
advance of the WWTP upgrade. The developers will be able to decommission and remove the
temporary treatment once the Newcastlewest WWTP upgrade is complete.

Q3. When will there be a full upgrade on the sewer?

Response:

Uisce Eireann is progressing a wastewater sewer upgrade project in Newcastlewest. The project
includes upgrading approximately 850m of sewer to facilitate growth and new connections in
Newcastlewest. The project is currently at the detailed design stage.

Should you have any further queries, please don't hesitate to contact us on the details below. For

the latest supply and services updates, please see: |)ttos: //www.water.ie/?map=supply-and-service-

updates.

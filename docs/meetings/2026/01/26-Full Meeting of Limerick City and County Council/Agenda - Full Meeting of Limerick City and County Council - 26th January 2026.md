NOTICES OF MOTION

Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor S. Hickey-O’Mara

| move at the next Meeting that this Council write to the Minister for Health, Jennifer
Carroll-McNeill requesting the opening of a SATU (Sexual Assault Treatment Unit) in
the Midwest to support survivors who do not wish, or are not ready, to make a report
to the Gardai and wish to attend a SATU in their locality rather than having to travel
to SATUs in Cork or Galway as the current situation dictates.

twill move at the next Meeting that this Council expresses its dismay at and opposition
to the Government's intention to abolish the Triple Lock which governs the
deployment of Irish Defence Forces.

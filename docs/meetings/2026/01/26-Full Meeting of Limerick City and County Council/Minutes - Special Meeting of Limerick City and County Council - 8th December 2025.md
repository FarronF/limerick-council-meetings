**1. Disposal of Land**

**Economic, Tourism, Culture, Property and Corporate Buildings Directorate**

Circulated, copy of Statutory Notice dated 28th November, 2025, concerning Lease 650 Year -

Parcel 5, Opera Square, Limerick and Licence 650 Year – covering exclusive use of portion of

underground car parking area, together with maps.

The Director of Service, Housing stated that the approval of the Section 183 will move forward

the development of the 14 storey office building, which will accommodate 1000 staff. The

disposal of the car parking spaces will bring in significant funding of €6 million.

Members thanked the Officials for their work on the Opera Development. They raised the

issue of DAC Board Membership and emphasised the importance of having Elected Members

on the Boards referencing the Code of Corporate Governance for Local Authorities.

The Director General confirmed that a dedicated DAC and shareholder unit had been created

to manage oversight of the associated companies.

Proposed by Councillor Collins (M);

Seconded by Councillor O’Donovan;

And Resolved:
---
“That, in accordance with the provisions of Section 183 of the Local Government Act, 2001

(as amended) and Section 211 of the Planning and Development Act, 2000 (as amended), the

approval of the Council be and is hereby given to the disposal of land as follows - Lease 650

year - Parcel 5, Opera Square, Limerick ((Ground Floor 1,346 m2 approximately and Basement

1,936 m2 approximately) including airspace above in accordance with the maps attached for

identification purposes); Licence 650 year – covering exclusive use of portion of underground

car parking area, to the Office of Public Works – the consideration being - Lease: €1,167,835

plus Development Costs accrued at date of legal transfer; Licence: €6,000,000 one-off charge,

the disposal to be subject to the covenants and conditions contained within the Contract –

the Members, in resolving the above disposals, also resolve to revoke the previous Section

183 Resolution approved by the Members at Meeting held on the 14th July, 2025, in so far as

it relates to the lands we are now seeking consent for as set out above, particulars of which

were contained in Statutory Notice dated 28th November, 2025.”

This concluded the meeting.

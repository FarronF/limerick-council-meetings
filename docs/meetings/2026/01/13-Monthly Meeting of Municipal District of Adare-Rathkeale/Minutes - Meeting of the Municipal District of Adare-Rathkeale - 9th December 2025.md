**MINUTES OF PROCEEDINGS AT MONTHLY MEETING OF THE MUNICIPAL DISTRICT OF**

**ADARE-RATHKEALE HELD IN ÁRAS SEÁN FINN, NEW LINE ROAD, RATHKEALE, CO.**

**LIMERICK AND ONLINE ON TUESDAY, 9TH** **DECEMBER, 2025 AT 10.00 A.M.**

**PRESENT IN THE CHAIR:**

Councillor G. Ward, Cathaoirleach.

**MEMBERS PRESENT:**

Councillors Collins, Hartigan, Keary, O’Donoghue and Teskey.

**OFFICIALS IN ATTENDANCE:**

Director of Housing (Mr. V. Murray), Meetings Administrator (Ms. K. Butler), Senior

Executive Engineer, Roads, Traffic and Cleansing (Mr. K. Murphy), Executive Engineer,

Roads, Traffic and Cleansing (Mr. B. Barrett), Senior Engineer, LA Housing Construction

and Maintenance (Mr. B. Kidney), Senior Executive Engineer, Environment and Climate

Action (Ms. C. Lynch), Clerical Officer (Ms. L. Lenihan).

Apologies for her inability to attend were received from A/Senior Executive Officer,

Municipal Districts (Ms. K. White).

At the outset of the Meeting All Members expressed their gratitude to the Executive in

recognition of their efforts in getting the necessary works completed and Askeaton Pool

and Leisure operational again. Members were in agreement that former Director of

Finance, Sean Coughlan and former DAC Chairperson Kevin Sheahan also be thanked.

Councillor Teskey proposed the District write to both of these individuals to expresses

Members thanks and gratitude for their contribution to have the facility operational

again. This was seconded by Councillor Collins and agreed.

**1.**

**Minutes**

Circulated, copy of draft Minutes of the Monthly Meeting of the Municipal District of

Adare-Rathkeale held on 11th November, 2025.

Councillor O’Donoghue referred to Minutes and asked it be recorded that he and his

Elected colleagues had congratulated Catherine Connolly on her election as the 10th

Uachtarán na hÉireann and wished her well in her new role.

Councillor Keary also referred to the Minutes and his request for an update on the

proposed eight serviced sites for Adare village. The Meetings Administrator advised

Brendan Kidney, Senior Engineer, LA Housing Construction and Maintenance would

provide an update after the adoption of the Minutes.

Proposed by Councillor O’Donoghue;

Seconded by Councillor Keary;

And Resolved:

“That the draft Minutes, as circulated, be taken as read, adopted and signed, subject to

the amendment outlined above”.

Following the adoption of the Minutes, Senior Engineer, LA Housing Construction and

Maintenance gave Members an update on the proposed eight serviced sites for Adare

village advising his department are currently in the process of preparing tender

documents which will issue when the legals associated with landowners is finalised. He

further advised the current construction forecast would see development commence in

quarter one 2027 and current spend to date on the project is two million euros.

Discussion ensued and the following issues were raised by Members:

• Councillor Collins raised concerns over the affordability of the eight serviced sites

due to the determination of the market value of the site stating this project will

be impossible to deliver as an affordable scheme for young families who wish to

remain in the village of Adare. The Senior Engineer, LA Housing Construction and

Maintenance advised a workshop would be convened in January to discuss this

matter further.

• Councillor Keary concurred with Councillor Collins’s sentiments stating the cost of

building materials has more than doubled since the project was approved six

years previously. He further queried how Limerick City and County Council could

grant planning permission on a site without a storm water connection. The

Senior Engineer, LA Housing Construction and Maintenance advised planning

permission was given in good faith and it is hoped the storm water issue will be

resolved in due course.

• Councillor O’Donoghue queried how many houses in total are included in the

scheme. The Senior Engineer, LA Housing Construction and Maintenance advised

there will be thirty one in total, thirteen dwellings on the north side of the site,

ten dwellings on the south side will also allow for eight serviced sites.

The Director, Housing concluded there would be a workshop convened for Members

in January 2026 to discuss all concerns raised by Members.

**4.**

**Environment and Climate Action**

**Landscape Proposal for Askeaton Park**

The Senior Executive Engineer, Environment and Climate Action gave Members a

presentation on the Uisce Éireann landscape proposals for Askeaton Park which will

include the installation of large underground storm tanks, landscape measures to

disguise the area around the storm tanks, a walk and cycle track as well as replacing

timber railing and the installation of pathways. The Senior Executive Engineer,

Environment and Climate Action advised Members that following a presentation to the

Askeaton Town Team they had requested no hidden areas remain in the site following

these works and requested lighting in the interest of preventing antisocial behaviour in

the area. She further added Uisce Éireann will install ducting for CCTV if needed in the

future and added Uisce Éireann will fund works to subbase level as part of the

reinstatement contract with Limerick City and County Council funding the remainder of

the works. She concluded by advising Members that Uisce Éireann are due to lodge a

planning application in Q2 of 2026 and construction would be likely to begin in Q4 of

2027.

Councillor Hartigan expressed concern that the Ryder Cup would also be take place in

Q4 of 2027 and was concerned about the aesthetics of the site if these works should go

ahead. Councillor Hartigan also requested the Senior Executive Engineer, Environment

and Climate Action would communicate with representatives from the Askeaton Tennis

Club who are not part of the Town Team and have recently been granted a Sports

Capital Grant for upgrade works to the tennis courts in an effort to ensure works do not

encroach between these projects. The Senior Executive Engineer, Environment and

Climate Action agreed to discuss the matter with Uisce Éireann to establish a more

definite timeframe for the proposed construction and also agreed to arrange a meeting

with representatives of the Tennis Club.

Councillor O’Donoghue questioned the cost of the overall project and what Limerick City

and County Council’s contribution would be. The Senior Executive Engineer,

Environment and Climate Action advised she was awaiting Uisce Éireanns cost estimate

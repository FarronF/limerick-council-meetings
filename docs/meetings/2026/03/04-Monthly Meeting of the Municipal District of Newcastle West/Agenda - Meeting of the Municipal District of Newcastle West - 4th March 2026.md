Dore’s Cross, Templeglantine.

Questions submitted by Councillor T. Ruddle

| will ask at the next Meeting that Limerick City and County Council cut back the tree
branches which are over hanging onto the N21 at South Quay and Cullinagh,
Newcastle West. They are hanging very dangerously over the main N21 to
Killarney/Tralee.

| will ask at the next Meeting that Limerick City and County Council prioritise the
Ballintubrid road L21008 for this year’s Road Works Programme. The road isin a
very bad state of repair for the past few years.

I will ask at the next Meeting that Limerick City and County Council consider putting
ramps and raised platforms on the Knockane/Monegea road to reduce speed.

Question submitted by Councillor Scanian

| will ask at the next Meeting when Uisce Eireann will repair the water damaged area

NOTICES OF MOTION
Notices of Motion submitted by Councillor L. Galvin

| will move at the next Meeting that the black material on the fences at Kilconlea,
Abbeyfeale be replaced/repaired with a different material as it keeps blowing down.

| will move at the next Meeting that the Council calls on the Minister of State with
responsibility for the Office of Public Works to expedite the flood relief scheme for
(a) Athea and (b) Abbeyfeale.

Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor M. Collins

| will move at the next Meeting that Limerick City and County Council will carry out
necessary safety works, lighting and pedestrian crossing at Dromcollogher National
School. As part of these works, | would ask that St. Joseph's Park be included in
these works also. | am bringing this Motion on foot of recent correspondence from
the Principal and pupils of the school.

Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor T. Ruddle
| will move at the next Meeting that Limerick City and County Council repaint the

pedestrian crossings in Newcastle West that require it in particular the crossing from
Modini shoe shop to Fitzgerald’s Corner.

Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor J. Scanlan

I will move at the next Meeting that a safety review be conducted of traffic and
travel conditions on the R522 between Castlemahon Cross and Danganbeg.

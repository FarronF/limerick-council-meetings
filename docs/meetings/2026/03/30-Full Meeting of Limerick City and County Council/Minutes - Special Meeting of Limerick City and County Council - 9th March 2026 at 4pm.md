MINUTES OF PROCEEDINGS AT SPECIAL MEETING OF LIMERICK CITY AND

**1.**

**Date for March Meeting**

The Príomh Chomhairleoir welcomed everyone to the Special Meeting which had been

convened to consider re-fixing the date for the March Meeting of the Council. The proposed

date to hold the meeting was Monday, 30th March, 2026.

On the proposal of Councillor Collins (M), seconded by Councillor Sheahan, the date of the

March Meeting of the Council was re-fixed to Monday, 30th March, 2026.

This concluded the Meeting.

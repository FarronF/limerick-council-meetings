**Transportation, Mobility and Public Realm Directorate**

**Croom Square Project**

The Executive Architect, Transportation, Mobility and Public Realm gave Members a

presentation on the proposed revitalisation of the former Post Office building and Town

Square project in Croom. He advised funding of €50,000 for consultancy has been

approved from the Town & Village Renewal Scheme with further funding applications to

be applied for when available adding works will be carried out with prior consultation with

Croom Community Development Association.

The Executive Architect, Transportation, Mobility and Public Realm outlined the concept

and possible uses for the completed project which includes the refurbishment of the

former Post Office into a potential tourist office and tea room. The Town Square will

include a paved outdoor space with seating and planting to be used for public gatherings,

events and performances and will include a sculpture dedicated to the Maigue Poets. An

extension to the rear of the Post Office will be a large multipurpose space for a variety of

community uses.

In Q1 2026 cleaning and painting works have taken place on the former Post Office

building as well as the preparation of design options. Public consultation for input and
---
3

feedback, development of preferred design and submission of Part 8 Planning Application

will take place in Q2 2026.

Discussion ensued, the overall project was welcomed by Members with the following

concerns raised:

• Councillor O’Donoghue queried the cost of the project. He also added safety at

that junction with Main Street is paramount and must be taken into consideration.

• Councillor Teskey stated the footpaths in Croom are poor quality and need to be

upgraded.

• Councillor Collins requested if the removed parking spaces at this location would

be reassigned elsewhere in the town. She also stated a connectivity to the

Rathkeale Greenway be factored in for the town as part of the project.

• Councillor Keary requested a formal plan for Croom be presented to Members

before the Part VIII is completed for the location and also requested the building

should be mirrored to look complete and repurposed for social housing

accommodation.

The Director of Housing informed Members grant funding has been secured for the initial

design and phase one of the project adding Karen Burke, Town Centre First Officer will

eventually be carrying out a Town Centre First plan for Croom, however, as the building is

in a prominent location in the town painting had to be carried out to improve the look of

the building for the present time. He added there is adequate parking within the town

centre currently and the project has been welcomed by the local population in Croom.

The Director of Housing further added the building would be difficult to demolish and it

would be preferred to encourage tourism back into the town by turning it into a tourist

office and tea room.

The Executive Architect, Transportation, Mobility and Public Realm concluded the design

would be carried out within the organisation rather than externally and he told Members

he would take all comments and concerns outlined during the course of the meeting into

account and revert when a final design is complete.

**Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor J. O’Donoghue**

**I will move at the next Meeting to receive an update on the Croom Distributor road and**

**for any other road improvements being considered for the immediate Croom area in the**

**next ten years. I ask that all recent traffic surveys be available along with a**

**representative from the NM20 road design team.**

Councillor O’Donoghue advised the meeting he was proposing this motion due to

concerns among the local population of Croom regarding traffic congestion at the T
---
4

junction on main Street and how this congestion could be alleviated as a ring road for the

town is not possible. Councillor O’Donoghue requested a distributor road now be

considered for the town and be installed prior to the construction of the N/M20

motorway. Councillor O’Donoghue further requested traffic surveys be carried out in the

town to record the high volume of traffic including lorries and buses entering the town on

a daily basis.

The N/M20 Project Coordinator, Mid West National Road Design Office circulated a list of

traffic surveys carried out over the last five to six years for Croom advising more are due

to be carried out this year and added there is a permanent traffic counter north of Croom

currently in operation. He further advised that all traffic surveys can be found on the

website www.corklimerick.ie and added the N/M20 project is coming to the end of Phase

3 stage and statutory documents are being finalised with a business case to go to

Government which will determine the start date of the project with the hope it will be

approved by Q4 2026.

Councillor O’Donoghue informed the N/M20 Project Coordinator, Mid West National

Road Design Office that the main street in Croom needs to be a one way system adding a

traffic management plan needs to be in place before any plan is presented to Government.

Councillor Keary queried the cost of the distributor road project. The Director,

Transportation, Mobility and Public Realm advised the project would cost in the region of

€5 million to €10 million adding she would liaise with the Department of Transport and

revert on the matter when an update becomes available.

**Reply:**

While there is currently no funding in place to complete the final phase of

the Croom Distributor Road to connect the scheme to the R516 Bruff Road.

Should such funding be provided, Limerick City & County Council would

need to prepare a final design and tender process prior to proceeding to

construction.

The only other road improvement project of significance in the area is the

N\M20 Cork to Limerick Project.

**12.**

**Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor J. O’Donoghue**

**I will move at the next Meeting for a presentation on what will remain under the**

**control of Limerick City and County Council with regards to water and wastewater**

**services in the District.**
---
7

The Senior Engineer, Environment, Climate Action and Shared Services informed

Members staff currently employed by Limerick City and County Council on behalf of

Uisce Eireann have to make a decision to transfer to Uisce Eireann or remain with

Limerick City and County Council over the next two years. If they opt not to transfer

they will be reallocated within Departments of Limerick City and County Council.

Councillor O’Donoghue in proposing this Motion expressed concern regarding the loss of

local knowledge from existing Council staff transferring to Uisce Eireann and also the

difficulties expressed by Elected Members trying to contact Uisce Eireann on local issues

with water and sewerage breakdown in the District.

The Director, Housing added that over the past year Uisce Eireann have been

documenting and recording local knowledge from staff in their own systems for

locations of pipes and valves etc. He also advised Members can contact Uisce Eireann

through their call centre. Councillor Collins stated she has always received an immediate

response from Uisce Eireann when contacting them through social media platforms.

Discussion ensued in relation to Members difficulty in contacting various utility

companies and Councillor Keary requested for the next meeting contacts for local utility

companies such as Uisce Eireann, Eircom, ESB and Bord Gais.

**Reply:**

Limerick City and County Council no longer provides public water or

wastewater services. This function has now transferred to Uisce Eireann.

The Rural Water Section provides a regulatory, testing and grant

administration function to the Private Water Schemes (including Group

Schemes) in Limerick.

**I will move at the next Meeting that we as Elected Members request Limerick City and**

**County Council adequately fund the three St. Patricks Day parades that will take place**

**across the Adare-Rathkeale Municipal District in 2026.**

and agreed.

supported next year.

**Reply:**

The LCCC Economic and Tourism Development Department gives financial

assistance to St. Patrick's Day Parades and St. Patrick's Festivals through

two open calls:

• The St. Patrick's County Parades Support and the Festivals and Events

Grant Scheme.

o Two supports available. Town support €1,800. Village support

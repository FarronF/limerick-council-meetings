NOTICES OF MOTION

Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor A. Teskey

| will move at the next Meeting that this District write to the Minster for Transport to
request that the Department set about new regulations in order to streamline the
registering of primarily vintage and classic vehicles that have no vehicle registration
allocated to them or vehicles with a current registration that an individual requires a log
book in his or her name.

Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor A. Teskey

| will move at the next Meeting that Limerick City and County Council undertake a review
of its current procedures for the assessment of complaints relating to wind turbine noise
and shadow flicker.

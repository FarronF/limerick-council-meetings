NOTICES OF MOTION

21. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor C. Slattery

I will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City
and County Council make improvements to the traffic and lighting layout at the entrance to
Woodlawn Park given the fact that two new housing schemes have been established in the
area.

22. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor E. Secas

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that this District
write to the NTA, TFl and Bus Eireann to request that the TFl 90 minute fare system is
expanded to Limerick, as changing buses would be very beneficial for uptake, given the
length of bus routes in the city.

23. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor S. Kiely

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that a choice based
letting system be explored for Council housing.

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City
and County Council advance the land acquisition agreements for the land required for the
N69 Brick Lodge to Clarina Village Walkway as a matter of urgency.

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City
and County Council carry out a further examination of making some units available within
the 24-unit Barnakyle Housing Scheme for Affordable Purchase.

26. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor J. Pond

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City &
County Council would put in place or trial a one way traffic system from the junction of
Carrigeen/Roskeen Estates to the small roundabout near Annacotty Village on
Saturdays/Sundays to alleviate traffic congestion in the vicinity of the Aisling Annacotty
Football Club.

27. mitted by Council . Hor J. Pond

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that funding be
secured from the Department of Justice/Local Government and Heritage to retain the
Moyross CCTV Monitoring Centre as this infrastructure assists Gardai in tackling crime and
anti-social behaviour all across Limerick.

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that communication
boards are installed in Council run public spaces. Communication Boards assist neuro
divergent residents, those with disabilities, those experiencing ill mental health, and others,
to communicate their needs in public spaces. The Right to Communication is a fundamental
human right enshrined in the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, ensuring that the
communication needs of residents are upheld by the local authority is crucial.

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City
and County Council conduct a comprehensive audit of all locations across the Metropolitan
District where cycle paths intersect with road junctions, to ensure adequate signage is in
place to clearly indicate right of way for both cyclists and motorists, specifically calling out
where cyclists do not have right of way. And install additional signage, specifically at busy
junctions requesting cyclists to wait and dismount when crossing.

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City
and County Council conduct a speed survey with a view to installing additional traffic
calming measures in Lower Park, Corbally.

3. HOUSING DIRECTORATE
Housing Delivery Progress Update.

The Cathaoirleach welcomed Brendan Kidney, Senior Engineer, Housing and Kathy O’Grady,
Administrative Officer, Housing Support to the Meeting.

They proceeded to give an update to the Members on housing delivery progress which
included:

¢ The target for new builds in the Municipal District was 203, whilst 223 units are likely
to be delivered;

¢ Acquisitions-26 units approved and a further 14 being considered;
e Leases-6 units approved and 1 being assessed;

¢ Currently 21 Void properties undergoing refurbishment works in the Cappamore
Kilmallock Municipal District;

¢ 4 properties were recently returned to Limerick City and County Council and are in
the process of being inspected to determine the level of refurbishment works
required;

¢ €4.5 million in funding available for second hand acquisitions, which includes the
Tenant in Situ scheme. However, this funding does not cover refurbishment works for
Tenant In Situ scheme properties so at this moment in time, the local authority is
unable to progress any TIS properties as the Council does not have internal funding
available to cover required refurbishment works;

¢ Currently 22 LCCC led Affordable Sale properties are planned for the Municipal
District including in Glenfield, Kilmallock and Bottlehill, Doon;

e Approximately 840 social housing units are currently in the Cappamore Kilmallock
Municipal District.

The Cathaoirleach and Elected Members thanked the Senior Engineer and Administrative
Officer for the update. The Members agreed that the provision of various housing options
including social housing, affordable housing, tenant in situ, were very important for the
Municipal District, having regard for the demand for housing that existed in the District.
The Elected Members requested further information in relation to the breakdown of the

social housing stock in the Municipal District and the Senior Engineer agreed to provide
same for the Members.

8. Question submitted by Councillor Carey

| will ask at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District for an update
on my motion from March 2021 regarding the pedestrian crossing on Sheares Street,
Kilmallock and the amendment by Cllr Donegan regarding the National School pedestrian
crossing.

Reply:

Since this original motion in 2021, Limerick City and County Councils Traffic Calming Policy
Document adopted by the Elected Members in 2024, identifies that a tabletop ramp should
only be used as a last resort and that all other traffic calming measures should be
considered first.

it also identifies that speed ramps are not permitted on National, Regional or Strategic
Roads. If a recommendation from the Roads Engineer is for the installation of a tabletop
speed ramp, this decision must be detailed in an assessment report and a final decision is
then made by the Senior Engineer for Roads, Traffic and Cleansing or their delegate.

Please note that the Municipal District Office has recently arranged for the two overhead
lights on this crossing to be upgraded to improve visibility, while the crossing point itself
was also fully upgraded in terms of tactile paving, new signals, road markings and signage in
2022.

10. Question submitted by Councillor Teefy

| will ask at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District if there any
plans/guidelines for Columbarium Walls or Ashes Plots in the Municipal District in view of
the increasing numbers of cremations.

Reply:

The Council are aware of the need to provide for the interment of ashes in several of its
cemeteries due to an analysis undertaken as part of the Burial Ground Interment Strategy.

As a result, the Council is providing Columbarium Walls or Ashes plots in some cemeteries.
The decision on whether to provide a Columbarium Wall or Ashes plots or a combination of
both is largely dependent on demand in a particular location based on population density
and associated costs.

In the Cappamore Kilmallock Municipal District, the Council has already identified an area
for a small number of ashes plots in Ballingaddy and Abington cemeteries with other
opportunities being Investigated. The Council will be making provision for Columbarium
Walls and ashes plots as part of any new cemetery in the Municipal District.

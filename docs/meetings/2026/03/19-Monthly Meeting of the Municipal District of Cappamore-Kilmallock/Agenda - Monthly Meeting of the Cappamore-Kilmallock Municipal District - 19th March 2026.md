Questions

6. Question submitted by Councillor O’Sullivan

| will ask at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District
that following recent damage to Ballaghaderg Bridge (Mitchelstown Golf Club)
has Limerick City and County Council engaged with Cork County Council to
access any structural damage to the underside of the Bridge.

7. Question submitted by Councillor Carey

| will ask at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District for
an update on my previous motion regarding the proposed footpath linking
Fairyfield to Kilmallock Town and if this will be considered for future Active
Travel funding.

8. Question submitted by Councillor Carey

| will ask at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District if
funding is being sought by Limerick City & County Council for tree removing and
re-surfacing works at the council-owned Kilmallock Business Park.

9. Question submitted by Councillor Teefy

| will ask at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District for
an update on the installation and completion of the electric vehicle charging
points planned for the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District, with reference
to my previous enquiries on this issue, including the number installed to date
and the timeline for completion of same.

Notice of Motions

10. Notice of Motion submitted by Clir O’Sullivan

| will move at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District
that Limerick City and County Council would carry out a pedestrian safety
survey on Lower Main Street, Kilfinane, as parking on both sides of the street
reduce the option of using the footpaths and diverts pedestrians to use the road
to access the town.

11. Notice of Motion submitted by Clir Carey

| will move at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District
that the proposed lease for a site at Kilteely Graveyard be finalised by Limerick
City and County Council.

12. Notice of Motion submitted by Clir Conway

| will move at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District
that Limerick City & County progress with the installation of extra public lighting
and new and continued footpaths in the village of Knockainy Village, Co.
Limerick.

Question submitted by Councillor 8. Beasley (deferred from January Meeting of
Limerick City and County Council)

| will ask at the next Meeting of Limerick City and County Council regarding illegal dumping
enforcement activities across Limerick:

(a) How many individuals and/or entities have been fined or prosecuted for illegal
dumping offences in each of the last five years (2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025).

({b) Of these enforcement actions, please provide a breakdown between: -
Domestic/residential offenders - Commercial/business offenders - Breakdown by
Municipal/Metropolitan District area.

(c) What is the total value of fines issued for illegal dumping in each of the last five
years, and what percentage of these fines have been successfully collected.

(d) How many reports or complaints of illegal dumping were received by the Council
in each of the last five years, and what is the ratio of reports received to
enforcement actions taken.

(e) What resources are currently dedicated to illegal dumping enforcement, including:
- Number of enforcement officers assigned to this issue - Budget allocation for
enforcement activities - Technology used (cameras, vehicle tracking, etc.)

(f) What are the most common locations or "hotspots" for illegal dumping across the
Council's functional area, and what targeted measures have been implemented at
these locations.

(g) How many court prosecutions were initiated for illegal dumping offences in each
of the last five years, and what were the outcomes of these prosecutions
(convictions, dismissals, pending).

(h) What is the total cost to the Council of: - Investigating illegal dumping incidents -
Cleaning up illegally dumped waste - Legal and court costs associated with
prosecutions for each of the last five years.

(i) What preventative measures or public awareness campaigns has the Council
undertaken to reduce illegal dumping, and what metrics are used to evaluate their
effectiveness.

We will move at the next Meeting of Limerick City and County Council that:

All proposed amendments relating to draft documents shall be submitted in writing
24 hours before the relevant meeting, and all proposed amendments shall be
uploaded and available to councillors on SharePoint.

Proposed amendments and any counter proposals to amendments shall be discussed
in the Council Chamber only.

Proposed adjournments shall be substantiated before proposing and seconding, in
alignment with current Standing Orders.

All items discussed during adjournments shall be raised in the Council Chamber prior
to proposing and seconding following the end of the adjournment if pertaining to the
meeting at hand.

Item 78 on current Standing Orders is revised to: “every motion or amendment shall
NOT be moved and seconded, before being discussed or put to the meeting”.

All afternoon meetings shall break after every 2 hours for a 30-minute recess
regardless of adjournment, and following this, will continue only for an additional 1-
hour. Any adjournments following this 1-hour continuation shall be to another date.
All morning meetings shall break after every 2 hours for a 30-minute recess regardless
of adjournment, and must complete by 6pm of that day, or be adjourned to another
date.

**1.Riverfest Open Funding Call**

The Head of Economic and Tourism Development advised the Members that a Competitive

Open Call for Riverfest Limerick 2026, specifically Riverfest Creative Programming (Family

Friendly) Open Call and Riverfest Night-Time Economy Event Programming Open Call, was

issued on 6th March 2026 via Limerick.ie and social media platforms.

He noted proposals were invited from cultural organisations, creative practitioners, and

community groups to enhance the City’s festival offering, outlining the proposed activities

applicants were requested to submit under:

• Strengthen and enhance the core Riverfest programme.

• Highlight Limerick’s cultural identity and its connection to the River Shannon.

• Support Limerick’s growing status as a vibrant urban destination and gateway to the

Wild Atlantic Way.

• Promote Limerick as a centre of creative excellence, showcasing local, national, and

international talent.

The Head of Economic and Tourism Development outlined that the Creative Programming

(Family Friendly) Open Call had a maximum budget of €30,000, advising Members that 18

applications were received, highlighting the total amount requested was €103,399. The

Night-Time Economy Event Programming Open Call also had a maximum budget of €30,000.

He further advised that 6 applications were received under this call and highlighted that the

total amount requested was €66,330.

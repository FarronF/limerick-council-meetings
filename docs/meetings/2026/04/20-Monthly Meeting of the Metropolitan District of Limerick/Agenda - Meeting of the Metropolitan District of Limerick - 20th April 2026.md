QUESTIONS
9. Question submitted by Councillor Donoghue

| will ask at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick if the Kings Island Flood
Defences project will include any design proposals to demarcate to the public the locations
and extent of medieval archaeology discovered during the excavations for these works.

10. Questions submitted by Councillor Donoghue

| will ask at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick for an update on works
to upgrade the footpaths on Shannon Street, and the footpaths on O'Connell Street between
Hartstonge Street and Cecil Street, including a footpath widening in front of civic buildings
such as the Belltable to allow for congregation of crowds.

11. mitted by Council

| will ask at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick is there any grant or
incentive scheme to encourage retail shop owners to improve their shop front signage.
Some shop fronts/signage on William Street are of poor quality and not complying with
regulations.

12. Question submitted by Councillor Donoghue

| will ask at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick for an update on
opening and closing times for the People’s Park and the Baggott Estate, the process followed
by the Park Rangers in advance of closing times, and an update on the locations for opening
and closing times in these parks.

13. Question submitted by Councillor Hickey-O’Mara

| will ask at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick why the footpath
extension outside Belltable Theatre, 69 O’Connell Street, which was initially proposed as
part of the redevelopment works on O'Connell Street, was not completed.

14. Question submitted by Councillor O'Donovan

| will ask at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick for an update on the
second stage of the O’Connell Street Revitalisation Project as there is an urgent need to
resurface footpaths here especially outside the Belltable Theatre.

NOTICES OF MOTION

23. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Pond

i will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City &
County Council would engage with Uisce Eireann to kick start the process of installing a
wastewater treatment plant for the village of Montpellier to stop the flow of untreated
sewerage entering the River Shannon.

24. of Motion submitted b

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that all residential
streets between Barrington Street and New Street, on both sides of O'Connell Avenue and
including Lord Edward Street and the Rosbrien Road are reviewed and a traffic strategy is
developed to ensure greater safety for pedestrians, reduced congestion for residents, and
clear and frequent crossing points for pedestrians.

25. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Donoghue

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City
and County Council write to the Minister for Transport and the National Transport Authority
seeking clarification on the level of funding to be allocated to the delivery of BusConnects
Limerick under the National Development Plan Review 2025 and the Transport Sectoral
Investment Plan 2026-2030. This letter must also request a clear multi-annual funding
commitment and delivery timeline for the project, and further confirmation of the specific
capital investment planned for Limerick within the national BusConnects programme, given
the strategic role of BusConnects in delivering sustainable mobility, supporting compact
urban growth, and enabling Transport Oriented Development in line with national policy.

26. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor O’Donovan

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that a reusable cup
scheme be piloted in partnership with local cafes and 2GoCup to reduce single use coffee
cups in Limerick City.

27. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor O’Donovan

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick for speed reduction
measures to be installed in Huntsfield Estate, Dooradoyle due to the significant speeding
currently in the estate.

28. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillors O’Sullivan, Butler, McSweeney, Kiely
and Dovle

We will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City
and County Council write to Taoiseach Micheal Martin and Tanaiste Simon Harris to formally
request a Limerick City Taskforce be created, as has been established in Dublin and Cork.

29. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Doyle

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City
and County Council write to the Minister for Health requesting that the double shingles
vaccination be given free of charge to those 80 years of age and over. The cost to pensioners
of the two-dose shingles vaccination service — Shingrix is €487 (€243.50 per vaccination
dose) in a retail pharmacy.

30. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Hickey O’Mara

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick District that the
status and current location of the two long granite seats that were previously located on
Bedford Row be shared and that these public amenities be returned to their original
location.

31. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Kiely

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that tabletop ramps
be installed at the pedestrian crossing at Orombanna and the speed be reduced to 50km per
hour.

32. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Hickey O’Mara

damage at Greystones Park, no vehicles are licenced to park and trade on the green area and

Replies to the following Questions on the Agenda were circulated to all Members prior to the

6.

Question submitted by Councillor L. Galvin

***I will ask at the next Meeting for a detailed update on the public convenience, Abbeyfeale.***

REPLY:

There was a workshop held with Elected Representatives on 28th January, 2026, to discuss

options for delivery of a Public Convenience Facility in Abbeyfeale. Several options were

presented including costings. As presented the most cost-effective solution is for Limerick

City and County Council to purchase a toilet facility. Limerick City and County Council will

continue to explore options for the maintenance and cleaning of the toilet.

The Abbeyfeale Traffic Management Plan project will provide funding for the purchase and

installation of the public convenience and maintenance costs for 2026 and 2027. It is

proposed that the public convenience would be installed as part of the upcoming Grove

Crescent Carpark Link works.

Beyond this maintenance period (from 2028 onwards) the Elected Representatives will need

to make budgetary provision for the ongoing maintenance of the toilet.

7.

Question submitted by Councillor L. Galvin

***I will ask at the next Meeting for*** ***a detailed update on (a) Devon Road junction (b) Dore’s***

***Cross, Templeglantine.***

REPLY:

**(a)**In November 2025, the Mid West National Road Design Office (MWNRDO) submitted an

updated Feasibility Report to Transport Infrastructure Ireland (TII), seeking approval and

funding to progress the scheme to detailed design, preparation of tender documents and

construction.

Following its review of the submission, TII requested further clarification on certain

aspects of the updated Feasibility Report. The additional information sought has since

been provided by the MWNRDO and a response from TII is currently awaited.
---
4

TII has allocated funding in 2026 to enable MWNRDO to undertake additional surveys and

site investigations to assist with the preliminary design of the staggered junction option

recommended in the Feasibility Report.

**(b)** Limerick City and County Council have developed a number of design options for

footpath delivery in the eastern side of Templeglantine village, extending the existing

footway to Dore’s Cross. Limerick City and County Council have submitted an updated

feasibility report to Transport Infrastructure Ireland for consideration.

8.

Question submitted by Councillor L. Galvin

***I will ask at the next Meeting*** ***for a detailed update on Daly’s building, Abbeyfeale.***

REPLY:

Limerick City and County Council are currently in the process of procuring a Design Team to

take the project to a shovel ready stage, as the building is a Protected Structure and is located

in the Architectural Conservation Area, the proposal requires input from conservation

specialists. The proposal will advance through the planning consent process, over the

coming months and stabilisation works, if deemed necessary, will be completed, subject to

available resources.

14. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor L. Galvin

***I will move at the next Meeting that the Council calls on the Minister of State with***

***responsibility for the Office of Public Works to expedite the flood relief scheme***

***for (a) Athea and (b) Abbeyfeale.***

***(a)**Athea Flood Relief Scheme***

The Motion was seconded by Councillor Scanlan.

In proposing the Motion, Councillor Galvin requested that this Motion be referred to the

Corporate Services Section for inclusion on the Agenda for the next Full Council Meeting.

REPLY:

The preferred option for the Athea Flood Relief Scheme (FRS) was submitted to OPW for

formal option approval (PR3) on 15 August 2025. Since then, Limerick City and County Council

(LCCC) has regularly requested updates from OPW on the status of this approval process.

OPW has responded that the scheme remains under review with no current timeline advised

for a decision.
---
6

Limerick City and County Council will now write directly to the Minister of State with

responsibility for the Office of Public Works (OPW) to request a formal update on the status

of the scheme.

***(b)**Abbeyfeale Flood Relief Scheme***

The Motion was seconded by Councillor Foley.

In proposing the Motion, Councillor Galvin discussed the urgency of the matter. He stated

that in Abbeyfeale, three private residences and the Golden Vale Mart were flooded

previously.

REPLY:

The Senior Executive Engineer has met with the Office of Public Works and a flood map review

request form has been submitted to them so that they will revisit their CRFAMS map. A

scheme viability review was also requested for the area that has been flooded so as to see if

it now feasible for a flood scheme to be provided to protect these properties.

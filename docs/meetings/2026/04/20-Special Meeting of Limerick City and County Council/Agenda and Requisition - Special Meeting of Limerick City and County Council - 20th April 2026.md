Section 139 of the Local Government Act 2001 (as amended) resolution in
relation to proposed works at Boro Park, Janesboro.

Section 139 of the Local Government Act 2001 (as amended) sets out that an Elected Council or Joint
Body as the case may be, may by resolution, direct that those works shall not proceed.

Taking into consideration that the existing Boro Park, Janesboro is currently zoned as “City Centre” in
the 2022-2028 Limerick Development Plan. Section 12.4 of the Limerick Development Plan sets out in
the “Land Use Zoining Matrix” that Leisure/Recreation Facilities is a generally permitted use on lands
zoned “City Centre “and is a type of development which that the Council accepts in principle in the

relevant zone.
Conscious of the increased housing needs across the City and County and to prevent any delay in

Gelivering units by taking staffing resources and public funds to advance a Part 8 Planning
Application for the said site when a more suitable site should be advanced as a priority.

Accordingly in the common good, of advancing housing delivery as a priority and for the local area
and taking into consideration that the Boro Park, Janesboro, is the only safe green space for many
existing residents to enjoy we the undersigned wish to propose this Council approves this resolution
under Section 139 of the Local Government Act 2001 (as amended) that the Council shall not proceed
with any further such works to advance the delivery of SMART Housing on the said site.

Cle Sard, KS,

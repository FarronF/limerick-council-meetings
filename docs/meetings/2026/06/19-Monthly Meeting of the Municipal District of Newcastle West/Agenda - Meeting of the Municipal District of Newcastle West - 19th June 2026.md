NOTICES OF MOTION
Notices of Motion submitted by Councillor T. Ruddle

| will move at the next Meeting that the Council consider traffic calming measures in
Ballyagran village, passing the Church, School and Dolans shop. The speed here is
excessive at times.

| will move at the next Meeting that the Council write to the Office of Public Works in
relation to the very limited opening hours of the Castle. This year it did not open until
May 21st and is closing again in Mid -September. This is our biggest tourist attraction
to draw people into the town.

Notices of Motion submitted by Councillor L. Galvin

| will move at the next Meeting that the proposed works for Colbert Terrace,
Abbeyfeale be brought forward as the road is in poor condition.

| will move at the next Meeting that Limerick City and County Council calls on the
Mayor to initiate proceedings to vary the County development plan to facilitate/
zone retail sites on the Killarney Road, Abbeyfeale and that this Motion if passed be
forwarded to the Mayor's office and also placed on the next full Council agenda.

Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor J. Scanlan

I will move at the next meeting that official contact be made by our roads
directorate with Uisce Eireann the ongoing matter of water damage to the Milford

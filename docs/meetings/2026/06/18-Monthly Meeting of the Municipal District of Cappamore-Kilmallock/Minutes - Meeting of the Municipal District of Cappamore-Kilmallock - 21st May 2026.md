MINUTES OF PROCEEDINGS AT MONTHLY MEETING OF THE MUNICIPAL DISTRICT OF
CAPPAMORE-KILMALLOCK HELD IN ARAS MAINCHIN SEOIGHE, KILIMALLOCK, CO. LIMERICK
AND ONLINE ON THURSDAY, 21% MAY, 2026 AT 2PM.

PRESENT IN THE CHAIR: Councillor G. Conway, Cathaoirleach.
MEMBERS PRESENT;

Councillors Carey, O’Sullivan, M. Ryan, Stokes, Teefy.
Apologies: Cllr E. Ryan.

OFFICIALS IN ATTENDANCE:

Meetings Administrator (Mr. M. Leahy); A/Senior Engineer, Ryder Cup (Mr T. McKechnie);
A/Senior Executive Engineer, Roads, Traffic and Cleansing (Mr. D. Corcoran); A/Senior

The Cathaoirleach welcomed everyone to the Meeting. On behalf of the Elected Members he
expressed condolences to Cllr Eddie Ryan on the recent passing of his sister Margaret Angela
O'Sullivan, Aherlow View, Galbally and formerly of Tooting, London. The A/Senior Engineer,
Ryder Cup offered his condolences on behalf of management and staff.

As a mark of respect, the Monthly Meeting was adjourned for 15 minutes.

On the resumption of the Meeting, the Cathaoirleach welcomed Mr T. McKechnie to the
meeting and he and the Elected Members expressed their best wishes and a speedy recovery
to Mrs. P. Liddy, Director of Services, following her recent accidental fall.

The Cathaoirleach and Elected Members noted the great success of the RAS Tailteann event
which had taken place the previous day, with the finishing stage in Kilmallock. They thanked
all those involved who worked so hard in the planning and preparation for the event including
Paul Gammell; the Kilmallock Town First Scheme; Kilmallock Tidy Towns Committee;
Kilmallock Tourism; Limerick City & County Council, An Garda Siochana, and everyone else
involved.

The Cathaoirleach and Elected Members noted that this was the last Municipal District
Meeting that Helen O'Neill would be involved with, as she is retiring in June 2026. They
referred to her 47 years service with Limerick County Council, and latterly Limerick City &
County Council, most of which was spent in the Kilmallock Office. They thanked her for all her
work, especially in relation to the organisation and preparation for the Monthly Municipal
District Meetings, and they wished her well in her retirement.

applications for the Municipal District from City Hall to the Kilmallock MD Office and that
this process would be completed in the coming months.

QUESTIONS

5. Question submitted by Councillor Stokes

| will ask at the next meeting of Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District that, having regard
to EV charging points currently being introduced in the Municipal District at: Municipal
District Offices, Kilmallock/Sycamore Drive, Bruff/The Square, Caherconlish/Monastery
Road, Doon; are there plans to roll out EV Charging Points to more towns and villages across
the Municipal District? If so, what is the timeline for the provision of same?”

Reply:

The installation of EV charging points at Kilmallock/Sycamore Drive, Bruff/The Square,
Caherconlish/Monastery Road, Doon are currently progressing. These installations will be
monitored and reviewed to establish usage data when the chargers are operational. A
programme for further installations is being developed and will include data available from
the existing network locations. Further locations for the next installations will be discussed
with ZEVI and will be progressed subject to approval and available funding.

The Notice of Motion was proposed by Cllr Teefy, seconded by Clir M. Ryan, and agreed.

Reply:

A Traffic Calming scheme was completed on the N24 at Boher in the early 2000's. The
scheme included the construction and installation of a number of traffic calming measures
over a 500m section of roadway. These included central islands, ghost islands, public
lighting, road narrowing, gateway signage, lane separation, and delineation signage.

However, the Municipal District Office will carry out a revised traffic survey to assess current
traffic volumes and speed at this location.

ANY OTHER BUSINESS

Grass cutting

The A/Senior Executive Engineer, Roads, Traffic and Cleansing confirmed that grass cutting
on the National Roads in the Municipal District would commence in the coming weeks and
that hedge cutting would also take place in advance of road works schemes. The Elected
Members highlighted the importance of grass cutting at junctions and other areas so as to
improve visibility and safety at junctions for all road users. It was also noted that individual
landowners had a responsibility with regard to cutting back hedging along the boundaries of
their properties also.

R513

The A/Senior Executive Engineer, Roads, Traffic and Cleansing referred to the discussions at
the previous Monthly Meeting in relation to the speed limit on a section of the R513 outside
Ballylanders. He outlined how an assessment of vehicle speeds along this stretch of road
was carried out at two separate locations in December 2023. The results of the surveys are
as follows:

Location 1: The Mean (average) speed was 69 km/h, the 85th percentile speed was 78 km/h
and the posted speed limit was 80 km/h;

Location 2: The Mean (average) speed was 67 km/h, the 85th percentile speed was 76 km/h
and the posted speed limit was 80 km/h.

He confirmed that the surveys resulted in a V85 (the speed at which 85% of the traffic is
travelling at or below) of 78km/hr and 76km/hr, and that these results therefore confirmed
that the existing posted speed limit was correct for the section of road in question,

Also, he outlined how the sparse nature of development along this road would not warrant
a 60km/hr speed limit as per Chapter 7 of the Guidelines for Setting and Managing Speed
Limits in Ireland.

The Members noted the comments of the A/Senior Executive Engineer, Roads, Traffic and
Cleansing and also the data referred to in the reply. They stated that the 85" percentile
speed stated of 78km/h and 76km/h referred to in the analysis report is too high and
therefore warranted a review of speed limits at this location. They believed that this was
also in the context of the number of serious road accidents and fatalities at this location and
the frequency of same.

The A/Senior Engineer, Ryder Cup noted the comments of the Elected Members and
explained that any road speed limit was a maximum speed limit, and that road users also
had to take responsibility for their speed having regard to road conditions, etc. The A/Senior
Executive Engineer, Roads, Traffic and Cleansing also confirmed that a further assessment of
vehicle speeds along this stretch of road would be carried out in the coming weeks to obtain
updated data.

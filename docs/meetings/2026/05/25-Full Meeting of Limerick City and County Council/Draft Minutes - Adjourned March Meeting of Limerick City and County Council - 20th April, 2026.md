**Notice of Motion submitted by John Moran, Mayor of Limerick**

***I will move at the next Meeting that this Council agrees, subject to the necessary***

***administrative, legal, procurement and data protection arrangements being put in place,***

***and with financial support secured so as to protect existing core services in 2026, that all***

***meetings of the Full Council held after the adoption of this resolution shall be live-streamed***

***and recorded in full by audio and video means, and that such recordings shall be made***

***publicly available on the Council’s official website (**www.limerick.ie**) no later than two***

***business days following the conclusion of each meeting, without unnecessary barriers or***

***registration requirements.***

The Príomh Chomhairleoir noted that at the March Meeting, this Motion was discussed and

an amendment was proposed and seconded “that all Statutory Meetings be recorded”.

Members expressed support for the recording of Meetings in the interest of transparency.

Some Members expressed the need for transparency across all statutory meetings,

highlighting that, as no costings were available, a decision on the matter could not be made

at this time.

Councillor Collins (M) proposed to defer this item until full costings were available. This was

seconded by Councillor Collins (B).

The Deputy Director General advised Members that the legal advice received in relation to

qualified privilege had been circulated, as requested. He confirmed that costings would be

arranged and noted that any agreed livestreaming or recording of meetings would require a

procurement process with site surveys at each location to determine the final costs. He

outlined the difference between public and private statutory meetings, noting that public

meetings were those that may be attended by the public and press.

The Deputy Director General noted that Standing Orders would have to be amended to

facilitate the recording of Meetings and advised that at least one half of the total number of

Members of the Council must vote in favour of a change to Standing Orders.

Some Members were not in favour of live-streaming and recording of meetings, noting that

resources could be used better elsewhere, while other Members strongly supported the live-

streaming and recording of meetings, highlighting the benefits of showing the work carried

out in the Chamber.

Members discussed including all statutory meetings, in the amended motion. They

emphasised again the importance of knowing the final costs before a decision could be made.

Members thanked the Executive for circulating the legal advice on qualified privilege,

expressing disappointment that there had not been enough time to consider the contents as

it had been issued shortly before the Meeting commended. They requested ‘bad faith’ and

malice’ in the legal advice and sought further legal clarification on definitions.

In discussion the Mayor acknowledged that the Standing Orders would need to be amended

before live-streaming and recording of meetings could commence but indicated that he

wished to proceed with his original Motion, subject to amendment of Standing Orders.

Following discussion, it was agreed, on the proposal of Councillor Ryan (E), seconded by

Councillor Sheahan, to take a 15-minute adjournment.

Upon resumption, it was agreed to defer the item to a Special Meeting to allow time for

costings and further legal advice to be obtained, and to agree on the scope of the meetings

to be live-streamed and recorded. It was agreed to hold a Workshop in advance of the Special

Meeting to facilitate detailed discussions with the Elected Members.

**Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor L. Galvin**

***I will move at the next Meeting of Limerick City and County Council that this Council initiates***

***proceedings on the Sheehy's Hardware site, Killarney Road, Abbeyfeale to facilitate re-***

***zoning of lands from Enterprise and Employment to Retail.***

The Motion was proposed by Councillor Galvin and seconded by Councillor Foley.

In proposing the Motion, Councillor Galvin expressed disappointment that the Council did not

consider commencing the process to rezone the lands at Sheehy's Hardware site, Killarney

Road, Abbeyfeale, in advance of a decision being made on the relevant retail planning

application.

Members expressed their support for the Motion, noting that more appropriate zoned lands

were needed to support investment and development in the area.

**REPLY**: Legally, the Council cannot initiate a standalone process to re-zone the Sheehy

Hardware site. An amendment to the Abbeyfeale Local Area Plan (LAP) is not possible,

as the Planning and Development Act 2024 no longer provides for the preparation or

amendment of LAPs. Under the new legislation, the Development Plan will instead

incorporate all settlements that were previously covered by LAPs.
---
**4**

A variation to re-zone the Sheehy Hardware site cannot proceed in isolation. It would

require a full review of the entire settlement, followed by a variation of the

Development Plan to include the zoning and associated objectives for the town.

Due to legislative changes currently under consideration, it is expected that the

timeline for commencing the next Development Plan review may be delayed by up to

two years. In the interim, the Forward Planning team commits to bring forward a

series of variations to the Limerick Development Plan over the coming years to

integrate the ten existing LAPs. The timing of these variations will depend on factors

such as the expiry dates of the current LAPs and housing growth pressures within

individual settlements.

In addition, any future changes to retail zoning outside settlement boundaries will

require a review of the retail strategy, which forms part of the Development Plan.

Following discussion, the Director General agreed to consider the views expressed by the

Elected Members and to review the potential for economic development in the area.

The remaining items on the Agenda were deferred to the May Meeting of Council in order to

facilitate the commencement of the Special Meeting at 5:00 p.m.

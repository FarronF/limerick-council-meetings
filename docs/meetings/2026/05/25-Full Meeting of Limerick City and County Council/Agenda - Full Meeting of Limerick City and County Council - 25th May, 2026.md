MAYOR’S QUESTIONS
Question submitted by Councillor E. O’Donovan

| will ask the Mayor for an update on the actions and progress of the Transport Sub-Group
in relation to the ongoing delays to the BusConnects programme in Limerick. To ask
specifically:

- Whether the Minister for Transport and NTA has attended, or has been invited to attend,
any meeting of the Transport Sub-Group to discuss the delays and future delivery
timelines for BusConnects in Limerick; and

- Whether any progress has been made in relation to my request, raised at the September
2025 meeting of Limerick City and County Council, for Bus Route No. 1 to service the
Mulcair Road area.

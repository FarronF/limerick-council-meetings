**1.**

**Minutes**

Circulated, copy of draft Minutes of the Monthly Meeting of the Municipal District of

Adare-Rathkeale held on 10th March, 2026.

Councillor Keary referred to Item 7 in the minutes of the Monthly Meeting of 10th

March, 2026 regarding the approval of an allocation of General Municipal Allocation

(GMA) monies to Knockfierna Heritage Society. He stated that it was previously agreed

as a group of Councillors for the District, that no further GMA funding would be

allocated to either of the Knockfierna Committees until such time as they resolved

ongoing issue between the two groups.

Some of the other Members agreed that while they would not refuse GMA funding as it

is an important amenity for the local community, they agreed that payment of GMA

funding approved at the March Meeting should be deferred until the two groups work in

conjunction with each other to resolve ongoing issues between the two groups.

Councillor Teskey disagreed and stated that the monies he had allocated to Knockfierna

Heritage Society were intended to assist with the cost of hedge cutting and

maintenance, etc and he believed that it should be sanctioned and paid.

The Director clarified that the Members did not propose to refuse the GMA funding, and

that the proposal was to defer the payment of the money until issues between the two

groups are resolved. He suggested that a Private Briefing would be organised for the

Members to resolve the issue.

**3.**

**Transportation and Mobility Directorate**

**Schedule of Municipal District Works for the Adare-Rathkeale Municipal District 2026**

Circulated, report of the Director of Services, dated April, 2026 outlining the Draft

Schedule of Municipal District Works (SMDW) 2026 for the Municipal District of Adare-

Rathkeale.

The Senior Executive Engineer, Roads, Traffic and Cleansing gave Members a

presentation on the various aspects of the Schedule of Municipal District Works for

2026. Discussion ensued in relation to funding issues and Councillor O’Donoghue asked

the Director to request additional funding for the Adare-Rathkeale Municipal District

adding the difference in funding between the City and County for Active Travel projects

is substantial.

Councillor Collins stated she fully supported Councillor O’Donoghue’s sentiments adding

that footpaths within the Adare-Rathkeale Municipal District require attention and

asked if an assessment can be carried out on all footpaths in the villages within the

Municipal District with a view to getting funding to upgrade same.

The Director advised he had discussed this issue with Patricia Liddy, Director of

Transportation, Mobility and Public Realm who informed him that she is constantly

requesting additional funding with the Department of Transport due to increase cost of

materials to carry out maintenance works.

He stated that funding for Active Travel projects is provided for by the National

Transport Authority and is allocated at national level. He also confirmed that while the

Municipal Districts get some funding for Active Travel schemes, that it is primarily aimed

mostly at large urban areas, to encourage more people to use public transport and cycle

lanes, etc.

Proposed by Councillor O’Donoghue;

Seconded by Councillor Collins;

And Resolved:

“That, the Draft Schedule of Municipal District Works for the Municipal District of Adare-

Rathkeale 2026 be adopted in accordance with Section 103A of the Local Government

Act 2001”.

**6.**

**A.O.B**

Councillor O’Donoghue referred to a meeting held in Croom recently in relation to the

proposed new M20 route and the potential loss of the Croom bypass. He stated that

concerns were raised that if the proposed route goes ahead, that large agricultural

vehicles will be forced to travel into the main street in Croom and, as a result, a petition

is now circulating to prevent this from happening.

Councillor O’Donoghue asked the Members to support the residents of Croom with this

petition. Members noted that the Mid West National Road Design Office already had to

redesign the Attyflyn to Adare Bypass section of road and the Patrickswell Bypass

section also. They agreed that the engineers should be anticipating likely bottlenecks

and disruptions that could be caused as a result of new road developments. They asked

if the M20 team could attend the May Municipal District Meeting or provide a Briefing

to the Members so as to discuss the issues raised in relation to Croom.

The Director advised that the Mid West National Road Design Office are constrained by

Traffic Infrastructure Ireland (TII) at National level and were required to adhere to

National Standards in relation to road design works, etc. The Members asked that if the

Mid-West Road Design Office are to attend a meeting with the Members that a

representative from Traffic Infrastructure Ireland would also be invited to attend with

them to address this issue.

This concluded the Meeting.

1. Limerick Development Plan 2022-2028 — Material Contravention

Pursuant to Section 34(6) of the Planning and Development Act 2000 (as amended) to
consider the Director General’s Report and proposal to grant permission in respect of a
material contravention of the Limerick Development Plan 2022-2028 for the following
development:

Pig. Ref. 25/60670: Change of Use of of 4 vacant and derelict cottages to be linked to form 1
café/bar with outdoor terrace, associated conservation and refurbishment of, and minor
alterations to, existing one and two storey structures, single storey rear extension and all
ancillary site works at 1-4 Toll Cottages, Verdant Place, Limerick.

UESTIONS

Questions submitted by Councillor L. Galvin

| will ask at the next Meeting can the Council open inlets from a public road to a
farmers land where there is no dyke to carry the surface water.

| will ask at the next Meeting for a detailed update on the Railway Yard including the
old storehouse in Abbeyfeale.

I will ask at the next Meeting for a copy of all the contracts for grass cutting in this
District.

NOTICES OF MOTION
Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor J. Scanian

| will move at the next Meeting that the Director of Service, Transportation, Mobility
and Public Realm, to trail average speed cameras along the R520 and R522 and to
revert to our Municipal Council with the outcome. Findings are needed in the
interest of safety.

Notices of Motion submitted by Councillor L. Galvin

| will move at the next Meeting that the Council carries out a safety audit at
Fitzgerald's farmhouse, The Hill, Abbeyfeale and carry out appropriate measures for
child safety.

| will move at the next Meeting that the Council includes grass collection to all
contracts for all estates in the District of Newcastle West.

Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor F. Foley

| will move at the next Meeting that an audit be carried out in the Municipal District
on all our disabled parking spaces and that proper signage is in place.

Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor T. Ruddle
! will move at the next Meeting that Limerick City and County Council consider

putting ramps/raised platforms on the Boherbue road (by SMI school) to help reduce
excessive speed here.

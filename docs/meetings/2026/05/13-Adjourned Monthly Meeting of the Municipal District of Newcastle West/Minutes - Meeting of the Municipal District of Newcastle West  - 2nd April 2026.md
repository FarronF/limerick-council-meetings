April Meeting of the District.

The Cathaoirleach and Members referred to the closure of Wyeth Nutritionals in Askeaton,

the closure of the plant. Concern was expressed for the workers affected by the closure and

to the long-term impact on the local economy. The Members referred to the importance of

encouraging employment back into the facility to keep employment in the region. Councillor

Galvin also made reference to the announcement of job losses at Kostal Ireland in Abbeyfeale.

Regeneration, Community, Sport and Rural Development

Directorate

The Town Centre First Officer commenced by stating that various projects are underway in

the District. She introduced the Acting Senior Executive Engineer, Rural Development, to the

Meeting who then gave a presentation on RRDF Projects. The Acting Senior Executive

Engineer gave a comprehensive overview of RRDF Category 1 projects which include West

Limerick Tourism Hub Fuller’s Folly and Limerick Greenway Hubs Development Projects. He

also referred to RRDF Category 2 projects which include Limerick Greenway Hub at

Abbeyfeale.

**Fuller’s Folly Project Update**

• Current Status – Detailed Design Ongoing

• Construct Tender Issue Q2 2026

• Estimated Construct Start Q4 2026

• Estimated Construction Completion Q1 2028

He outlined to the Members the Project Details which include Refurbishment of existing

buildings and courtyard, development includes a café, toilets, interpretation/exhibition

spaces and viewing platforms at roof level, new glass structure, resurfacing of courtyard and

public realm works adjacent to the river with improved linkage to the Desmond Castle site.

**Limerick Greenway Hubs Development Projects (Ardagh, Newcastle West and Barnagh**

**Station House Update).**

**(a)Ardagh:** Tender Assessment Ongoing, Estimated Construction Start Q2 2026 and

Estimated Construction Completion Q3 2027.

The Acting Senior Executive Engineer outlined to the Members the Project Details which

include Refurbished Station House with Café, toilets and visitor experience, train carriage,

carpark, playground, campervan parking area, refurbished goods shed and extensions to

Station House and Goods Shed.

**(b)Newcastle West Car Park:** Construction Works commenced September 2025, Estimated

Construction Completion June 2026.

The Acting Senior Executive Engineer relayed details of the project to the Members which

included provision of car park with 80 spaces including disability spaces, EVCP spaces and

Limerick Greenway Hub at Abbeyfeale. The funding for other projects including Fuller’s Folly

was raised.  Reference was also made to the Irish aviator and sportswoman, Ms. Sophie

Pierce, from Knockaderry who was born in 1896 who at the time was regarded as one of the

world’s leading aviators. The significance of William Smith O’Brien in the District was also

raised.

In replying, the Town Centre First Officer stated that consultants are currently working on an

Interpretation piece for Ms. Pierce. She also gave an update to the Members on the

application for funding for the Limerick Greenway Hub at Abbeyfeale and in replying, the

Senior Executive Engineer stated that this project was progressed at the same speed as the

other projects but issues arose with regard to ecology which impacted on progressing the

project, through the planning consent process.

The Members thanked the Acting Senior Executive Engineer’s predecessor Mr. Ben Noonan,

Senior Executive Engineer, Newcastle West Municipal District for his work in developing the

Limerick Greenway and projects outlined above.

The Members also thanked the Acting Senior Executive Engineer, Rural Development for the

comprehensive presentation.

Circulated, report of Director of Services, dated March 2026, setting out the details of the

Draft Schedule of Works for the Municipal District of Newcastle West 2026.

The Senior Executive Engineer addressed the Meeting and gave an overview of the Schedule

of proposed works planned for the District together with proposed funding for these works.

He made reference to funding received for Restoration Improvement and Restoration

Maintenance projects and to additional funding of €100,000 received this year for Tar

Patching. The Senior Executive Engineer referred to funding allocated to Drainage Works,

Low Cost Safety Schemes and Active Travel projects.

He outlined to the Members the funding of €100,000 allocated under the Development Fund

and to the funding of €95,000 General Municipal Allocation (GMA) to Roads Projects. He

referred to Bridge Rehabilitation Schemes funding allocation of €349,500 and to five bridges

in the District which are to be upgraded through Central Services.

The Members welcomed the report and the funding which was made available for the various

projects in the Schedule. The Members were satisfied with the geographical spread of

schemes in the District.

Reference was made to the funding allocated in the Schedule to the N21 Newcastle West

Bypass and the N21 Abbeyfeale Bypass and to the importance of progressing these projects

in the District.

The roads depot at Kinard, Glin was raised and if a roads crew could be designated to operate

out of Kinard Depot. In replying, the Senior Executive Engineer stated that the roads in this

area are actively managed and it may not prove efficient to have a crew operate out of Kinard

Depot. Following a brief discussion, it was proposed by Councillor Sheahan and seconded by

Councillor Collins that this matter be reconsidered.

Reference was made to the significant allocation of funding to various Active Travel projects

including the Broadford Village Boardwalk and to engagement with local communities in

these projects. In replying, the Senior Executive Engineer stated that a Part 8 planning

application is currently being prepared for the Broadford Village Boardwalk and it is envisaged

that this will be finalised shortly.

The issue of funding for works on the Dromdeeveen road in Ashford was raised and following

a brief discussion, it was agreed that the Executive Engineer, Roads would arrange a Meeting

onsite to discuss this matter.

Reference was made to the condition of other roads in the District which are not included in

the Schedule and to emergency funding being made available for these roads. In replying,

the Senior Executive Officer stated that the Director, Transportation, Mobility and Public

Realm is actively making representations to seek funding for emergency works in the District.

The Members referred to a number of other matters including timeframe for completion of

the Schedule, drainage works, patching units, cost of inflation and current staffing levels to

carry out the works outlined in the Schedule.

**(a)**The Council are preparing a Part VIII application for a new burial ground for Abbeyfeale.

The preparatory stage included a Stage 1 Road Safety Audit that identified operational

speeds on the access road significantly in excess of the speed limit of 50kph. Therefore,

the scheme requires a traffic calming scheme to be incorporated into the proposed

development and included in the Part VIII documentation. A preliminary traffic calming

scheme has been prepared and is currently being assessed by the relevant internal

departments of Limerick City and County Council. This review will inform the final design

and Part VIII documentation. It is expected that the Part VIII documentation will be

finalised and ready for public consultation in Q2 2026.

**(b)** The Abbeyfeale Traffic Management Plan is now split into stages for delivery purposes as

follows:

• Advanced Works/Demolition of the O’ Riordan’s Bar and a residential building at

Colbert Terrace (This was completed in 2024).

• Construction of the Grove Crescent Carpark Link - Contractor has been appointed and

construction is commencing in March 2026 (4 month works programme).

• Uisce Eireann will replace the watermains on Killarney Road, Main Street and Colbert

Terrace commencing in June 2026 (3 month works programme). This is funded by

Uisce Eireann directly.

• N21 Killarney Road Phase – It is planned to tender and commence works in H2 2026

with a target of having the works completed pre- Ryder Cup (September 2027).

• N21 Main Street and Colbert Terrace - It is planned to commence works post Ryder

Cup (September 2027).

At the moment we are working with our Consultant Malachy Walsh and Partners to finalise

the value engineering design exercise on the N21 Killarney Road Phase to deliver the project

within budget. Once this phase is re designed and cost estimate completed, we will engage

with the project funder TII to confirm funding prior to tender. We plan to publish the traffic

calming proposals under Section 38 of the Road Traffic Act 1994 to make the public aware of

the proposed traffic calming measures. This will take place in May.

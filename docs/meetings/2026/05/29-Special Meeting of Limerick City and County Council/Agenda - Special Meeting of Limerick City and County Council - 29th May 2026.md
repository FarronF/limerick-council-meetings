1. _— Strategic Infrastructure Development (SID)

To consider the Director General's Report in accordance with the requirements of Section 37E
(4) and 37E (5) of the Planning and Development Act, 2000 (as amended) in relation to the
proposal made by Carrow Renewable Energy Ltd. for a wind farm and associated
infrastructure in the townlands of Carrow, Moheragh, Gortaderry, Co. Tipperary; Toomaline
Lower, Toomaline Upper, Doon South, Lisgaugh, Cooga Upper, Coolyhenan, Milltown and
Killonan Co. Limerick.

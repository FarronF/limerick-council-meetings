**Item 2** **–** **District Heating** **–** **The potential for Decarbonised Heating for Limerick City & Suburbs** **–**

**Conor McDonagh** **–** **Climate Action, Environment and Water Services Department, Clare County**

**Council.**

Mr McDonagh delivered a presentation on the potential development of a District Heating network

for Limerick City and its suburbs. District heating involves the use of a single, centralised heat source

to supply heat to multiple buildings, allowing for efficient town-scale heating. A range of heat sources

can be utilised, including waste heat from wastewater treatment plants, as well as from industrial and

pharmaceutical facilities.

(Image omitted)

At a national level, the Climate Action Plan targets the delivery of 2.7 TWh of district heating by 2030,

which would represent approximately 10% of Ireland’s total heat demand. The Heat (Networks and

Miscellaneous Provisions) Bill 2024 will provide the legislative framework to support the development

and expansion of district heating networks.

It was noted that a feasibility study for the project is currently underway and is being 100% funded by

the Sustainable Energy Authority of Ireland (SEAI), with the final report expected in September 2026.

A general discussion took place on the potential of district heating and the possible future

implementation. It was agreed that the presentation would be shared with all members of the SPC.

**Item 3** **–** **To approve the Progress Report of the Limerick City and County Climate Action Plan** **–**

**presented by Kieran Reeves, Climate Action Co-ordinator.**

Mr K. Reeves sought approval from the committee a progress report on the implementation of

Limerick’s 2024–2029 Local Authority Climate Action Plan (LACAP). He outlined the background to the

LACAP, the current status of actions, selected case studies, and key priorities for 2026.

The main objectives of the LACAP are to improve energy efficiency by 50% by 2030, reduce greenhouse

gas emissions by 51% by 2030, enhance Limerick’s climate resilience, and actively engage communities

in climate action. The Plan contains a total of 98 actions, the majority of which are recurring in nature,

including ongoing maintenance and operational programmes.

Actions completed in 2025 include the Fleet Survey, Climate Champions Campaign, Noise Action Plan,

and the Biodiversity Plan. The presentation also provided an overview of ongoing and completed

projects under the five thematic areas of the LACAP, along with priorities for the remainder of 2026.

Examples highlighted included:

•

Built Environment & Energy: Pathfinder Programme

•

Communities & Partnerships: Doon Social Farm Woodland Restoration Project

•

Environment & Circular Economy: Limerick Greenway, awarded 3rd place in the European

Greenways Awards

•

Governance & Leadership: Introduction of Climate Action Week

•

Natural Environment: Demonstration of nature-based solutions through the Fosse Project

•

Travel & Mobility: Assessment of the Council fleet

Following a brief discussion, which included discussion on the retrofitting of Council buildings and

community engagement on climate action, it was agreed that the presentation would be shared with

all members of the SPC.

**The committee supported the referral of the LACAP 2024-29 Progress Report to Full Council for**

**approval (proposed by Cllr N. Stokes and seconded by Cllr S. Hickey** **O’Mara)**

**Item 4** **–** **Draft Multi Annual Work Programme for Climate Action, Biodiversity and Environment**

**Support Strategic Policy Committee** **–** **next steps - Sinead Hourigan** **–** **Administrative Officer.**

[[PAGE_END:2]]

Ms. Hourigan presented the draft Multi Annual Work Programme for the CABE SPC. The SPC

Guidelines 2024 advise that each SPC should adopt a multi-annual work programme linked to the local

authority’s Corporate Plan. The draft policy framework will be structured thematically – Waste and

Litter, Rural Water, Climate Action (including energy), Parks, Cemeteries and Environment

(biodiversity/noise/air/EU projects etc.). The Programme does not affect the current Standing Orders

– Each member may submit one agenda item relevant to the business of the SPC per meeting. New

Standing Orders are being drafted and members will be circulated the draft and updated in the process

at the next meeting.

A draft will be circulated to all members for input in advance of being submitted to the Corporate

Policy Group for approval.

Cllr Leddin thanked Ms Hourigan for the presentation advising it is very timely.

**Any Other Business** **–** **Dog Support Initiative**

Ms Hourigan asked for support from the committee to explore on-granting of funding from the Dog

Support Initiative, funded by the Department of Agriculture, to Limerick Animal Welfare subject to a

service level agreement in support of the Council’s dog control function.

Following this discussion, regarding the proposed Service Level Agreement, dog parks and dog control

members were in agreement that the idea should be progressed.

At the end of the meeting Kieran Lehane, Director of Services, Environment, Climate Action and Fire

announced his retirement and received well wishes from all.

Remaining meetings in 2026 will be held on October 13 and December 8.

**This concluded the business of this meeting.**

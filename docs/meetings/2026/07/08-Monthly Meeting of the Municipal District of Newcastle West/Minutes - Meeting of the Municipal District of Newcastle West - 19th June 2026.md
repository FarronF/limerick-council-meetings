Regeneration, Community, Sport and Rural Development

Directorate

To Receive Update on Fuller’s Folly Project.

The Town Centre First Officer briefed the Elected Members on the West Limerick Tourism

Hub at Fuller’s Folly. She outlined the project details which includes refurbishment of existing

buildings and courtyard, development includes a café, toilets, interpretation/exhibition

spaces and viewing platforms at roof level, new glass structure in between the two buildings

comprising of entrance foyer and circulation space with stairs and a lift, resurfacing of

courtyard and public realm works adjacent to river with improved linkage to Desmond

Castle site. She stated that a detailed design is ongoing and it is expected to tender for

construction in Quarter 3, 2026 and t is estimated to begin construction in Quarter 4, 2026

with completion estimated in Quarter 1, 2028.

Councillor Collins thanked the Town Centre First Officer for a comprehensive update.

Councillor Ruddle stated it is a fantastic facility but car parking may be an issue. He expressed

dissatisfaction at the length of time it has taken to get to this stage. The Town Centre First

Officer explained land had to be acquired by Compulsory Purchase Order and this is a timely

process. Councillor Scanlan thanked the Town Centre First Officer for her hard work and

stated that Fuller’s Folly is ideally located and part of the heritage of the town.

10.

Question submitted by Councillor L. Galvin

***I will ask at the next Meeting for a detailed update on the Clúid housing development at***

***Clash, Abbeyfeale.***

REPLY:

Planning was granted in December 2025 and since then Clúid Housing have been arranging

internal approvals, working on the land transfer, further design and preparation of the main

contractor tender.

The first part (pre-qualification) of the two part tender process was uploaded onto eTenders

last week to seek suitably qualified contractors for the design and build of the units. Tender

return date is 06/07/26.

Once tender returns are received the qualifying contractors will receive the full

documentation to tender for the design and build for the project. It is anticipated that this

will issue for Mid-July with tender returns expected in September.

Following this Clúid Housing will have the tender assessment to determine the most

economically advantageous tenderer.

Once the tenderer is determined Clúid Housing will seek to update the funding approvals

with the Department of Housing, Local Government and Heritage via the Capital Advance

Leasing Facility scheme and the Housing Finance Agency.

When final funding approvals are received, and the land transfer has concluded, Clúid

Housing will be in a position to sign contracts and proceed to commence on site. The aim is

to commence on site before year end or early 2027. It is estimated that the build will take

approximately 15 months.

In relation to the planning condition on the CPO of the footpath, we have requested that

the Transportation, Mobility and Public Realm Directorate progress the CPO as part of their

work programme.

16. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor L. Galvin

***I will move at the next Meeting that the proposed works for Colbert Terrace, Abbeyfeale***

***be brought forward as the road is in poor condition.***

The Motion was seconded by Councillor Foley.

REPLY:

The Abbeyfeale Traffic Management Plan is being delivered on a phased basis to coordinate

with ongoing utility works and ensure efficient delivery.

The current programme is as follows:

• **Grove Crescent Car Park Link:**

Construction is currently underway and is expected to be completed in July 2026.

• **Watermain Works (Uisce Éireann):**

Uisce Éireann will commence watermain replacement works on Killarney Road, Main

Street and Colbert Terrace in June 2026, with an estimated duration of approximately

three months. These works are being delivered directly by Uisce Éireann.

• **N21 Killarney Road Phase:**

It is planned to tender and commence works in the second half of 2026, with a target of

completing the works in advance of the Ryder Cup.

• **N21 Main Street and Colbert Terrace Phase:**

These works are currently programmed for delivery after the Ryder Cup.

The detailed design for the N21 Main Street and Colbert Terrace scheme has not yet been

completed and will require the appointment of a design team. It is anticipated that a

design team will be appointed in Q4 2026.

The proposed works at Colbert Terrace are significant and include:

• Realignment of footpaths

• Replacement of the Tesco roundabout with a junction

• Installation of a signalised junction at Main Street/Colbert Terrace junction

In the interim, Limerick City and County Council will review the condition of the road

pavement in Colbert Terrace, in consultation with Uisce Éireann.

As watermain works are scheduled in this area in the coming months, the Council will engage

with Uisce Éireann in relation to the standard and aera of reinstatement, with a view to

addressing the existing pavement defects between Main Street and Tesco as part of these

works where feasible.

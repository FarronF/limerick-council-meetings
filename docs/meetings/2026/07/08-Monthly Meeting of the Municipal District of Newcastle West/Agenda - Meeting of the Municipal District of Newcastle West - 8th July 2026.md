**Questions submitted by Councillor J. Scanlan**

**11.**

I will ask at the next Meeting that double yellow lines be put in place at the Newcastle West

road junction with Main Street, Broadford.

**12.**

I will ask at the next Meeting when repair and safety measures will be undertaken at the

Moanroe junction with the R522 as well as along Bunoke Bridge.

**Question submitted by Councillor T. Ruddle**

**13.**

I will ask at the next Meeting how much if any cut back is expected in this year's road

schedule for the Newcastle West Municipal area due to the significant rise in the cost of

materials.

**Question submitted by Councillor J. Sheahan**

**14.**

I will ask at the next Meeting for an update on the current status of Ardmoyle Estate,

Carrigkerry with regards to its ownership and future plans for the estate.

**NOTICES OF MOTION**

**Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor M. Collins**

**15.**

I will move at the next Meeting that the junction at Broadford village where the L1311 and the

R515 meet have a safety audit done to consider restricting the parking of vehicles too close to

the junction inhibiting adequate and proper sightlines.

**Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor J. Scanlan**

**16.**

I will move at the next Meeting that a Low Cost Safety Scheme be implemented at the

Clounscherick end of the Camas Road.

**Notices of Motion submitted by Councillor T. Ruddle**

**17.**

I will move at the next Meeting what plans have the Council made to provide parking facilities

for tour buses on the N21 in Newcastle West, given the substantial investment in Fullers

Folly, which will have a museum and a link up to the Castle and onto the Square. We hope to

see Newcastle West become a major tourist attraction on this route.

**18.**

I will move at the next Meeting that the Council consider putting in age friendly measures such

as extra seating in the Demesne for older people.

**Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor J. Sheahan**

**19.**

I will move at the next Meeting that Limerick City and County Council use the powers it has

over dereliction to do whatever is necessary to bring the two derelict commercial units in

Ardmoyle estate, Carrigkerry into use either as commercial or residential.

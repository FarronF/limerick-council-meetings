**ADARE-RATHKEALE HELD IN ÁRAS SEÁN FINN, NEW LINE ROAD, RATHKEALE, CO.**

Councillor G. Ward, Cathaoirleach.

**MEMBERS PRESENT:**

Councillors Collins, Hartigan, Keary, O’Donoghue and Teskey.

**OFFICIALS IN ATTENDANCE:**

Director of Service, Adare-Rathkeale Municipal District (Mr. V. Murray), Meetings

Administrator (Ms. K. Butler), Senior Executive Officer, Municipal Districts (Ms. K.

**1.**

**Regeneration, Community, Sport and Rural Development Directorate**

**Part VIII Procedure** **–** **Kilteery Pier Development**

Circulated, Mayor’s Report, dated 21st May, 2026 setting out details in relation to the

proposal for the development at Kilteery Pier. The Report had been prepared in

accordance with Section 179 of the Planning and Development Act, 2000 (as amended)

and Part VIII of the Planning and Development Regulations, 2001 (as amended).

The Town Regeneration Officer, Rural Development addressed the Meeting and advised

following discussions on the proposed development of Kilteery Pier and taking into

account submissions and observations by Members, a decision would be required by

Members on whether to approve, amend or reject the report as presented.

Councillor Collins asked if the Town Regeneration Officer would provide a breakdown of

the proposed costings for the proposed development for all queries raised by Members.

The Town Regeneration Officer, Rural Development advised the following:

• Members request to consider changing the location of the proposed structure –

The location of the structure will not be changed as a number of locations were

considered and discussed at design team and stakeholder meetings throughout

the design process.

• Members request for the site to be connected to the water main and associated

costs – Connecting the site to the water mains supply on the N69 would cost

approximately €54,000 ex. VAT in construction costs and an additional cost of

approximately €2,300 for the connection fee.

• Members request for the structure to be connected to the electricity mains,

include controlled access to the site and associated costs – Currently the

structure cannot be connected to the electricity mains as the site is not served by

a mains connection. Taking a new connection from the N69 would cost

approximately €3,600 for an ESB non-domestic connection fee plus an additional

€7,500 for ducting installation at the pier. During the design stage, the Design

Team looked at introducing bollards to restrict vehicular access to the pier,

however, this was ruled out following consultation with the stakeholders.

• Members request for associated costs for maintaining the current Part VIII toilet

provision – Current waste removal at the portable toilet installed for three

months of the summer costs €400 including VAT per year. If the toilet was open

to the public the waste removal cost would be €400 per empty

• Members request for associated costs to prepare a new Part VIII on a different

site, with full access to public toilets, with a connection to water mains and

electricity – The estimated cost to prepare a new part VIII would be €35,000 -

€50,000.

Discussion ensued in relation to the estimated costings for the project presented to

Members. Councillor Keary queried given the estimated cost of the water connection if

a well could be drilled on site to alleviate costs and also if consideration could be given

to the installation of a solar powered barrier to prevent antisocial behaviour. The Town

Regeneration Officer, Rural Development advised these options could be explored and

incorporated into the funding proposal. Members were concerned that the overall cost

of this project appeared to be excessive and queried if the scale of the structure could

be reduced further and also if the proposed structure could be relocated within the site.

The Town Regeneration Officer, Rural Development advised the current proposed

structure for the site has been reduced as far as has been possible and is in the optimal

location adding that the current proposed structure would enhance the facility for the

local community and lifeguards using the pier. She further added funding has yet to be

allocated to deliver this project and commencement will be subject to a successful

funding application under the Outdoor Recreational Infrastructure Scheme (ORIS) and

advised Members it would be unlikely additional funding would be made available for a

new Part VIII proposal at the same location.

Following further discussion Councillor Collins proposed the Part VIII be adopted as

presented and thanked and acknowledged the team for the contribution and work they

have put into the project to date.

Councillor Ward proposed a five minute adjournment to allow Members a private

discussion on this matter. This was seconded by Councillor Keary and agreed.

On resumption of the Meeting Councillor Keary advised he would second the proposal

with the following conditions included in the Part VIII Report for the project:

• Installation of a wheelchair access toilet facility.

• Public toilet facilities being made accessible at all times.

• Installation of a solar powered barrier to allow access to the site.

Proposed by: Councillor Collins;

Seconded by: Councillor Keary;

And Resolved:

“That the Part VIII in relation to proposal for the development at Kilteery Pier, and

subject to the agreed amendments, be approved in accordance with Section 179 of the

Planning and Development Act, 2000 (as amended) and Part VIII of the Planning and

Development Regulations, 2001 (as amended)”.

Councillor Teskey asked what the estimated cost of the project would be to complete.

The Executive Architect, Rural Development advised the estimated cost of this project

would be approximately €400,000. Councillor Teskey welcomed the concept of the

proposal, however, he requested the record show he could not support the costings

associated with the project. He further requested a change be made to how Part VIII

projects are dealt with in the future stating they should include an estimated itemised

schedule cost of works at initiation. Councillor O’Donoghue and Councillor Ward also

expressed concern in relation to the costings for the proposed project and Councillor

O’Donoghue asked how much funding Limerick City and County Council would be

required to provide to bring the project to the next stage. The Town Regeneration

Officer, Rural Development advised ten percent of the overall project costs would be

required to be funded by Limerick City and County Council. She concluded advising she

would convene a private workshop with Members when final project costings are

available before submitting an application to the Department for funding.

This concluded the Meeting.

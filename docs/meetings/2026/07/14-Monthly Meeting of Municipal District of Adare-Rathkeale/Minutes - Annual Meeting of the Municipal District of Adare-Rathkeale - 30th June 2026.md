**MINUTES OF PROCEEDINGS AT ANNUAL MEETING OF THE MUNICIPAL DISTRICT OF**

**ADARE-RATHKEALE HELD IN ÁRAS SEÁN FINN, NEW LINE ROAD, RATHKEALE, CO.**

**LIMERICK AND ONLINE ON TUESDAY, 30TH** **JUNE, 20265 AT 11.00 A.M.**

**PRESENT IN THE CHAIR:**

Councillor G. Ward, Cathaoirleach.

**MEMBERS PRESENT:**

Councillors Collins, Hartigan, Keary, O’Donoghue and Teskey.

**OFFICIALS IN ATTENDANCE:**

Director of Service, Adare-Rathkeale Municipal District (Mr. V. Murray), Meetings

Administrator (Ms. K. Butler), Senior Executive Officer, Municipal Districts (Ms. K.

White), Executive Engineer, Roads, Traffic and Cleansing (Mr. K. Carroll), Clerical Officer

(Ms. L. Lenihan).

The outgoing Cathaoirleach, Councillor Ward, welcomed all to the Annual General

Meeting of the Municipal District to elect the Cathaoirleach and Leas Cathaoirleach of

the Adare-Rathkeale Municipal District. He thanked the Elected Members and Executive

for their support during his term as Cathaoirleach and wished the incoming

Cathaoirleach the best for the year ahead.

**1.**

**To elect a Cathaoirleach of the Municipal District of Adare-Rathkeale to hold**

**office until the next Annual Meeting or until a successor has been elected.**

The outgoing Cathaoirleach invited nominations for the Office of Cathaoirleach for the

coming year.

Proposed by Councillor Ward;

Seconded by Councillor Keary;

“That Councillor Adam Teskey be appointed Cathaoirleach of the Municipal District of

Adare-Rathkeale to hold office until the next Annual Meeting or until a successor has

been appointed”.

As there were no other nominations, Councillor Adam Teskey was declared elected as

Cathaoirleach of the Municipal District of Adare-Rathkeale.

The Members congratulated Councillor Teskey for his election as Cathaoirleach and

wished him every success in the coming year.

On taking the Chair, Councillor Teskey thanked his proposer and seconder and said he

was looking forward to working with all Members, Management and Staff over the next

twelve months in his role as Cathaoirleach of the Adare-Rathkeale Municipal District.

Councillor Teskey referred to the recent appointment of seven Members to the Limerick

City and County Local Community Safety Partnership Committee and asked for

confirmation on how these Members were appointed to this committee.
---
2

The Director of Service, Adare-Rathkeale Municipal District advised the legislation set

out for the Local Community Safety Partnership states that seven Members from each

Local Authority would be appointed to the committee and there was a general

agreement that one Member be appointed from each Municipal District as well as the

Príomh Chomhairleoir of Limerick City and County Council. These nominations were

agreed at the Full Meeting of Limerick City and County Council in March 2026. The

Senior Executive Officer, Municipal Districts advised these nominations were put

forward from the Corporate Policy Group and Party Leaders.

Councillor Keary felt the previous Joint Policing Committee Meetings worked very well

and expressed disappointment with the new format in which these meetings will now be

held going forward and proposed the Cathaoirleach seek a meeting with the Garda

Superintendent for the Adare-Rathkeale Division in September to discuss policing issues

in the area. This was seconded by Councillor Teskey and agreed. Councillor Teskey

advised the Adare-Rathkeale Municipal District would continue to hold Joint Policing

Committee Meetings.

**2.**

**To elect a Leas-Cathaoirleach of the Municipal District of Adare-Rathkeale to**

**hold office until the next Annual Meeting or until a successor has been elected.**

The newly elected Cathaoirleach then invited nominations for the office of Leas-

Cathaoirleach of the Municipal District of Adare-Rathkeale for the coming year.

Proposed by Councillor Teskey;

Seconded by Councillor Ward;

“That Councillor Stephen Keary be appointed Leas-Cathaoirleach of the Municipal

District of Adare-Rathkeale to hold office until the next Annual Meeting or until a

successor has been appointed”.

As there were no other nominations, the Cathaoirleach declared Councillor Stephen

Keary elected as Leas-Cathaoirleach of the Municipal District of Adare-Rathkeale and

wished her well for the coming year.

Councillor Keary thanked his Elected Member colleagues for his nomination and election

and looked forward to working with his fellow Councillors and the executive in the year

ahead.

This concluded the Meeting.

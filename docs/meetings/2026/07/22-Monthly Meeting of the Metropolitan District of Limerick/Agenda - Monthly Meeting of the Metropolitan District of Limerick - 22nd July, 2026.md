11. Question submitted by Councillor Gavan

| will ask at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick what measures are
planned to encourage and facilitate cycling along the R445 corridor between Limerick City
and Castleconnell/Annacottty/Castletroy. Are there plans for segregated cycling
infrastructure/orcas and safer junction treatments on the route.

12. Question submitted by Councillor Gavan

| will ask at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick does Limerick City and
County Council have any plans for a secure box-up facility for Castletroy Playground, similar
to O'Brien's Park, for the safe storage of sport equipment and use with the park going forward.

13. Question submitted by Councillor Gavan

| will ask at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick can Limerick City and
County Council confirm if it can engage with Bus Eireann and the National Transport Authority
(NTA) to ensure that the necessary depot capacity and charging infrastructure will be in place
to support the operation of the additional battery-electric bus fleet required for the
implementation of BusConnects in Limerick?

Can the Council also confirm whether the planned BusConnects network for Limerick will be
supported by a "Tap and Go" payment system and the integrated 90-minute fare structure
currently available in other urban areas, to ensure the smooth and efficient operation of the
enhanced bus services when BusConnects is introduced in Limerick.

14. Question submitted by Councillor Reale

| will ask at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick for an update on the
festoon lights along Arthur's Quay and Clancy Strand that have not been working for a number
of months and what this Council is doing to ensure that they are brought back to working
order.

15. Question submitted by Councillor Reale

| will ask at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick how many Local
Authority inspections of private rental properties have been carried out by the Council in each
of the previous two years (2024 and 2025), and how many of these inspections identified
breaches of the Housing (Standards for Rented Houses) Regulations 2019.

NOTICES OF MOTION

25. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Leddin

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City and
County Council acquire a mobile pool, in partnership with organisations such as Swim Ireland,
to facilitate the growing demand for swimming, similar to pop-up pools in Dublin.

26. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Benson

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City and
County Council include kerb to kerb resurfacing on the Old Cratloe Road, from the junction at
the new Coonagh Knockalisheen roundabout to Redgate, as part of the current active travel
works.

27. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Leddin

County Council write to the Minister for Justice to consider the introduction of a new bye-law
to prohibit the use of e-scooters on public roads, footpaths and/or open green areas in the
Metropolitan District.

28. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Reale

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City and
County Council immediately engage with Active Travel and the National Transport Authority
to carry out an immediate safety audit of O'Callaghan Avenue and Sexton Street North
Junction, otherwise known as Johnsey's Corner, concurrent to the completion of the rest of
the TUS to City Centre Active Travel Scheme.

29. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Beasley

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City and
County Council formally engage with the Active Travel section regarding the serious road
safety concerns at the Johnsey's junction in the Thomondgate/Kileely area, where public bus
services are regularly forced onto the footpath outside the shop, and where the footpath
opposite, near the old woodworkers premises, is similarly compromised, posing an
unacceptable risk to pedestrians, cyclists and motorists; and that this correspondence seek a
permanent engineering solution to this junction, in consultation with the National Transport
Authority where appropriate, a clear timeline for remedial works, and confirmation on
whether any temporary safety measures can be put in place in the interim while a permanent
solution is progressed.

30. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Reale

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City and
County Council write to the National Transport Authority to implement a bus service on the
Condell Road ahead of the rollout of BusConnects.

31. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Hickey O’Mara

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City and
County Council write to both Minister for Culture, Communications and Sport, Patrick
O'Donovan and Minister of State with responsibility for Sport, Charlie McConalogue, to
request the reinstating and ring-fencing of funding for the Enjoy Tennis programme at
Limerick Lawn Tennis Club.

32. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Hickey O’Mara

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that relevant Council
personnel engage with residents living on the Old Cratloe Road, Pass Road and Willow Grove
and support them in their request that, as part of current roadworks on the Old Cratloe Road,
2 pump station be installed and that all 41 properties in the aforementioned areas are
connected to this station and, through this action, to a public waste system.

33. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Donoghue

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City and
County Council publish a dedicated page on Limerick.ie to launch, in January of each year, all
funding opportunities arising over the calendar year for arts practitioners and event
providers, to ensure arts and event providers are aware of forthcoming deadlines and can
prepare in advance, similar to the approach taken by the Arts Council.

34, Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Donoghue

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that all drains in urban
streets are suction cleaned on a quarterly basis, to prevent leaf and rubbish congestion that
leads to flooding whenever there are heavy rains.

35. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor O’Donovan

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City and
County Council develop and adopt a standard consultation protocol for all Metropolitan
Active Travel schemes, including proposals progressed under Section 38 of the Road Traffic
Act, to ensure that elected members receive an early briefing before any public consultation
commences, that meaningful engagement takes place with local communities at the earliest
stage of scheme development, and that a report on public consultation is presented to the
appropriate Municipal District or Metropolitan Members before a final decision is made.

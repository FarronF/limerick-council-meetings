**MINUTES OF PROCEEDINGS AT SPECIAL MEETING OF THE METROPOLITAN DISTRICT OF**

**LIMERICK HELD ONLINE ON FRIDAY 10th** **JULY 2026 AT 9:45 A.M.**

**PRESENT IN THE CHAIR:**

Leas-Chathaoirleach, Councillor Catherine Slattery.

**MEMBERS IN ATTENDANCE:**

Councillors Beasley, Butler, Doyle, Gavan, Hartigan, Hickey O’Mara, Kiely, McSweeney, Pond,

Reale, Secas.

**OFFICALS IN ATTENDANCE:**

Senior Executive Officer, Metropolitan District of Limerick (Ms. S. Reidy), Meetings

Administrator (Ms. A. Foley), Senior Staff Officer, Corporate Services (Ms. C. Sheehy), Assistant

Staff Officer, Corporate Services (Mr. T. Cronolly).

The Leas-Chathaoirleach, Councillor Catherine Slattery took the Chair and presided over the

Meeting.

**1.July Meeting**

On the proposal of Councillor Butler, and seconded by Councillor Kiely, it was agreed to hold

the July Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick on Wednesday, 22nd July 2026 at

9:45am in the Council Chamber, Merchant’s Quay, Limerick and Online via Microsoft Teams.

This concluded the Meeting.

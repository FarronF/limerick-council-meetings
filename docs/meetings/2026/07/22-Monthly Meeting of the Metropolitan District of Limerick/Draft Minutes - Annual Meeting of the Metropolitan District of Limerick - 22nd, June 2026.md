**MINUTES OF PROCEEDINGS AT ANNUAL MEETING OF THE METROPOLITAN DISTRICT OF LIMERICK**

**HELD IN THE COUNCIL CHAMBER,** **MERCHANT’S QUAY** **AND ONLINE, ON MONDAY, 22ND** **JUNE 2026**

**AT 9.45AM**

**PRESENT IN THE CHAIR:**

Councillor D. Butler, Cathaoirleach

**MEMBERS PRESENT:**

Councillors Beasley, Benson, Daly, Donoghue, Doyle, Gavan, Hartigan, Hickey-O’Mara, Kiely,

Kilcoyne, Leddin, McSweeney, O’Donovan, O’Hanlon, O’Sullivan, Pond, Reale, Secas, Slattery and

Talukder.

**OFFICIALS IN ATTENDANCE:**

Director, Environment, Climate Action and Fire (Mr. K. Lehane), Meetings Administrator (Ms. A.

Foley), Senior Executive Officer, Metropolitan District of Limerick, Park and Cemeteries (Ms. S.

Reidy), Assistant Staff Officer, Corporate Services and Governance (Ms. S. Carroll).

The outgoing Cathaoirleach of the Metropolitan District, Councillor Butler welcomed the Members,

Officials, Members of the Media and the Public to the Annual Meeting to elect the Cathaoirleach

and Leas Chathaoirleach of the Metropolitan District of Limerick.

At the outset, the Cathaoirleach noted that this would be the final Annual Meeting for the Director

of Service, Mr. Kieran Lehane, prior to his retirement in the coming months. He paid tribute to Mr.

Lehane’s significant contribution to Limerick, particularly to Limerick City, and acknowledged his

professionalism, dedication and support to Members throughout his time as Director of Services.

He also recognised Mr. Lehane’s willingness to assist Members with queries, both during and outside

office hours.

Members extended their best wishes to Kieran, his wife and family for good health and happiness in

his retirement.

In response, the Director thanked the Cathaoirleach and Members for the kind words and their

support. A presentation was made to the Director to mark the occasion.

The outgoing Cathaoirleach thanked the Executive and Staff of Corporate Services for their

professionalism and support throughout the year, ensuring that the meetings had ran smoothly and

efficiently. He also thanked his party colleagues and fellow Councillors for their support, cooperation

and constructive engagement during his term. He noted the excellent work that was carried out

during the year in the District. Councillor Butler finished by thanking his family and friends for their

support during the year, expressing his best wishes to the incoming Cathaoirleach and Leas

Cathaoirleach, and offered to support them in their new roles.

The Members congratulated Councillor Butler on his very successful year as Cathaoirleach of the

Metropolitan District, particularly for his commitment to the city centre. They commended him for

the professional manner in which he chaired the meetings and carried out his duties.

The Director, Environment, Climate Action and Fire, on behalf of Management and Staff, thanked

Councillor Butler for his work throughout the year.

**1.To elect a Cathaoirleach of the Metropolitan District of Limerick to hold Office until the next**

**Annual Meeting or until a successor has been elected**

The outgoing Cathaoirleach invited nominations for the Office of Cathaoirleach for the coming year.

Proposed by Councillor Slattery;

Seconded by Councillor O’Hanlon;

“That Councillor Joe Pond be appointed Cathaoirleach of the Metropolitan District of Limerick to

hold office until the next Annual Meeting or until a successor has been appointed”.

As there were no other nominations, the outgoing Cathaoirleach declared Councillor Pond elected

as Cathaoirleach of the Metropolitan District of Limerick and congratulated him on his election.

Members congratulated Councillor Pond on his election as Cathaoirleach and wished him every

success in the coming year.

The newly elected Cathaoirleach, Councillor Pond took the chair. He said he was honoured to be

elected as Cathaoirleach of the Metropolitan District of Limerick. He expressed his gratitude to his

party colleagues and the Members for their support. He thanked his family, friends and colleagues

for their support since his election as a Councillor, acknowledging the sacrifices made by them.

Councillor Pond acknowledged the work of Councillor Butler as Cathaoirleach over the previous

twelve months, noting in particular the provision of regular city centre updates, which were of huge

benefit to Members. He thanked Councillor Kiely for her work as Leas Chathaoirleach.

He thanked the Council Staff for their work in the city, ensuring services run smoothly. He noted

the success of Riverfest and Twilight Thursdays as well as the rapid redevelopment of the Opera

Site. He highlighted the importance of increasing footfall in the city to help businesses prosper and

grow and attract new business to the city centre. He stated that Bus Connects should be rolled out

across Limerick to support the city centre and ensure that key areas are adequately served by public

transport. He welcomed the increased Garda presence in the city and noted the need for additional

Garda resources to be allocated to Limerick. He stated that the new Garda Station in Castletroy

should be delivered in an adequate timeframe. He highlighted that housing was a concern for all

Councillors. He referred to the recent meeting with the LDA and was hopeful that with private

developers and the Council’s building programme, these would help improve the housing situation

in Limerick. He emphasised the importance of including proper infrastructure such as creches,

playgrounds and green areas in developments.

He wished all associated with the Ryder Cup every success for the event next year. He also extended

his good wishes to the Senior and Minor Hurlers in their upcoming games. He finished by saying it

was a great honour to accept the role of Cathaoirleach.

**2.To elect a Leas Chathaoirleach of the Metropolitan District of Limerick to hold Office until the**

**next Annual Meeting or until a successor has been elected.**

The Cathaoirleach invited nominations for the Office of Leas Chathaoirleach of the Metropolitan

District of Limerick.

Proposed by Councillor O’Hanlon;

Seconded by Councillor Talukder;

“That Councillor Catherine Slattery be appointed Leas Chathaoirleach of the Metropolitan District

of Limerick to hold office until the next Annual Meeting or until a successor is appointed”.

The Cathaoirleach declared Councillor Slattery elected as Leas Chathaoirleach of the Metropolitan

District of Limerick.

The Director, Climate Action, Environment and Fire, on behalf of Management and Staff, wished the

incoming Cathaoirleach, Councillor Pond and Leas Chathaoirleach, Councillor Slattery every success

in the coming year.

This concluded the Meeting.

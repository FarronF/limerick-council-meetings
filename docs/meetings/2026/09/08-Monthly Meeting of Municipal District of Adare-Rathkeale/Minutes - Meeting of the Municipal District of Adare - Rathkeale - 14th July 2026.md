**ADARE-RATHKEALE HELD IN ÁRAS SEÁN FINN, NEW LINE ROAD, RATHKEALE, CO.**

Councillor A. Teskey, Cathaoirleach.

Councillors Collins, Hartigan, Keary, O’Donoghue and Ward.

**4.**

**Transportation, Mobility and Public Realm Directorate**

**District Engineers Report**

Circulated, report of the Senior Executive Engineer, Roads, Traffic and Cleansing

outlining details of proposed works, projects and future projects planned for the District.

• Councillor Keary requested an audit be carried out on all footpaths in the District

and requested the section of footpath across from Daly’s Tyres in Holycross,

Rathkeale which is particularly bad and is a trip hazard be attended to as a matter

of urgency. The Senior Executive Engineer, Roads, Traffic and Cleansing advised

this section of footpath could be replaced under the general maintenance

budget.

• Councillor Teskey asked if repairs could be carried out on the damaged section of

the R518 between Askeaton and Rathkeale that was recently resurfaced. The

Senior Executive Engineer, Roads, Traffic and Cleansing advised surface water

under the road caused this issue and a contractor is scheduled to carry out

repairs in the near future.

• Councillor O’Donoghue referred to the N20 road at Banogue Village that was

recently resurfaced and queried why works were carried out on this section of

road as it had been resurfaced to a high standard less than five years previously.

He also queried why a cattle underpass was replaced along the same stretch of

• Councillor Keary requested an update on Ballyengland Junction. The Senior

On resumption of the Meeting Councillor Teskey queried the details of Item B, Disposal

of property at Main Street, Ballingarry, Co. Limerick. The Administrative Officer,

Property Services advised Members the property was acquired under Derelict Sites Act

and had gone to sale agreed, however, the sale subsequently fell through and the

property had to be returned to the market. She further advised derelict properties

acquired are offered to the Housing Department initially and seldom brought before

Members for disposal, however, as this property requires a significant amount of work

to bring it back to a habitable state a decision was taken to dispose of same adding the

property consists of a front wall and has a small back garden. She further advised that

the purchaser had advised the Council that he has purchased this house to renovate and

his son would reside in it when the renovation works are complete.

(a) Circulated, Report of the Head of Property Management dated 3rd July, 2026

setting out proposals for the disposal of property acquired compulsorily under

the Derelict Sites Act, 1990 at Brewery Lane, Askeaton, Co. Limerick and the

intention to bring a Section 183 Notice to Full Council in this regard, for approval.

The report, as circulated, was noted by Members.

(b) Circulated, Report of the Head of Property Management dated 3rd July, 2026

setting out proposals for the disposal of property acquired compulsorily under

the Derelict Sites Act, 1990 at Main Street, Ballingarry, Co. Limerick and the

intention to bring a Section 183 Notice to Full Council in this regard, for approval.

The report, as circulated, was noted by Members.

(c) Circulated, Report of the Head of Property Management dated 3rd July, 2026

setting out proposals for the disposal of Land incorporating playground at Main

Street, Foynes, Co. Limerick by way of 30-year lease to Foynes and District

Community Council CLG and the intention to bring a Section 183 Notice to Full

Council in this regard, for approval.

The report, as circulated, was noted by Members.

(d) Circulated, Report of the Head of Property Management dated 3rd July, 2026

setting out proposals for the disposal of Land incorporating playground at St.

Mary’s Park Estate, Rathkeale, Co. Limerick by way of 30-year lease and granting

of right of way to Pat Neville, Peter Donovan and Brian Dillon on Trust for

Rathkeale Community Council and the intention to bring a Section 183 Notice to

Full Council in this regard, for approval.

The report, as circulated, was noted by Members.

**A.O.B**

• Councillor Keary referred to works carried out at the Lantern Lodge, Adare where

two footpaths have been installed and requested an update on the proposed bus

shelter and signage installation also for the location.

• Councillor Hartigan requested John O’Callaghan, Active Travel Department attend

the September Meeting of the Municipal District to discuss bus shelter

installations for the Adare-Rathkeale District.

• Councillor Hartigan raised safety concerns at the entrance to The Orchard Estate,

Pallaskenry following an accident at the location in the previous weeks and

requested remedial works be carried out at the location.

• Councillor O’Donoghue referred to the traffic plan being prepared between

Limerick City and County Council and the Gardaí in advance of the Ryder Cup

2027 event and proposed this plan when complete be presented to Members in

advance of the event to enable Members to advise town and village

representatives of what traffic restrictions would come into effect in their

respective areas for the duration of the event.

• Councillor Collins referred to discussion with the Mid West National Road Design

Office regarding proposal to install traffic lights on the N21 Bridge in Adare when

the Adare Bypass opens in 2027. The Director of Service, Adare-Rathkeale

Municipal District requested Central Service and Mid West National Road Design

Office be requested to attend the September Meeting of the District to discuss

this issue.

• Councillor Teskey requested a representative of Irish Rail attend the September

Meeting of the District to provide an update on the Limerick to Adare rail line.

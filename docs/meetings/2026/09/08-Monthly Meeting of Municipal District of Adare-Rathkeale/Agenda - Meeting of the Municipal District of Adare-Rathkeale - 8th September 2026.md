Question submitted by Councillor B. Collins

| will ask at the next Meeting that Limerick City and County Council outline the procedure
and process that was followed in choosing the recipients of the Development Levy Funding
and how the distribution of this fund was managed, who is responsible for these decisions
and allocations and how long it has taken for each recipient to receive their allocation and
also, can improvements be made to increase the efficiency of this process going forward.

Question submitted by Councillor J. O’Donoghue

| will ask at the next Meeting that Limerick City and County Council to provide a list of all
open planning enforcement cases for all wind farms; to provide the information in table
form including the following details; date opened; enforcement 1D; description of
complaint (which planning conditions noise, shadow flicker etc) and current status.

NOTICE OF MOTIONS

Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor B. Collins

| will move at the next Meeting that Limerick City and County Council write to An Garda
Siochana, TIl and the NTA to stop bucket collections being licensed or allowed in the village
of Adare until after the bypass of the village is opened.

Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor T. Hartigan

| will move at the next Meeting that the Municipal District request additional funding to
secure the rent of a second patching truck ahead of the upcoming winter season.

| will move at the next Meeting to request an update on the Orchard housing estate in
Pallaskenry.

| will move at the next Meeting that Housing Developments with fully paid Planning
contributions to Limerick City and County Council in the last ten years have their
development contributions considered when applying as part of a Local Improvement or
Community Involvement Scheme.

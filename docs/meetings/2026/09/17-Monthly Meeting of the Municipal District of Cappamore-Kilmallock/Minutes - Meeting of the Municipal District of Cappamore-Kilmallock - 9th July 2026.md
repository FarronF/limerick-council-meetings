Some Members welcomed the disposal of land in the Kilmallock Business Park and looked

Reference was also made to additional land adjoining the Business Park that was currently for

for sale, having regard to the fact that there were no other sites now available in the Business

QUESTIONS

4. Question submitted by Councillor Carey

| will ask at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District for an update
on my motion from February 2024 regarding the procurement of landscaping inside St
Andrews Medieval! Church, Kilfinane, Co. Limerick.

Reply:

Unfortunately, despite previous targets, there has been no progress to date on the project,
due to other work commitments such as capital works at Galbally and Ballynoe. The
remaining works at Kilfinane are, however, scheduled for completion in Autumn 2026.
These works will include vegetation control, the installation of interpretive signage and
seating, subject to the approval of the National Monuments Service.

5. Question submitted by Councillor Carey

1 will ask at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District for an update
on my motion from November 2023 regarding footpath repair and public lighting on the
Charleville Road, Kilmallock and if this proposed project has been included on the list for
future Active Travel funding.

Reply:

The proposal to upgrade the footpath and public lighting on the R515 Charleville Road has
been identified as a potential scheme within the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District
for delivery under the Active Travel Programme.

However, the delivery of Active Travel projects is dependent on the level of funding
allocated annually, and the availability of funding within the Municipal District remains
limited. Notwithstanding these constraints, the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District has
successfully delivered a considerable number of Active Travel projects in recent years,
enhancing infrastructure for walking and cycling and promoting more sustainable modes of
transport throughout the area.

The Municipal District will continue to maximise the benefits of allocated Active Travel
funding and to advance projects that support sustainable and active travel objectives. While
sufficient funding has not yet been secured to deliver the R515 Charleville Road scheme, the
project remains a priority for the Municipal District and will be progressed should funding
become available in the future.

7. Question submitted by Councillor M. Ryan

| will ask at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District for the
anticipated timeline for the switching on of lighting for the completed Active Travel works at
Doon, Cappamore and Pallasgreen.

Reply:

Regarding the Cappamore & Doon projects, the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District
have completed the application process for the relevant ESB connections. Limerick City and
County Council are liaising with the ESB to complete the connection process to activate the
public lighting infrastructure for the final completion of these schemes. Limerick City and
County Council anticipate that the public lighting should be activated before year end.
Regarding the Pallasgreen scheme, Limerick City and County Council's public lighting
contractor is scheduled to investigate and resolve the issue.

NOTICE OF MOTION
8. Notice of Motion submitted by Cllr Carey
| will move at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District that

additional car park spaces be identified, and design and costings be completed for same at
Radharc na Cille Estate, Kilmallock, Co. Limerick.

*<small>Scanned page, text may contain errors. See original file for clarity</small>*  

4
The Notice of Motion was proposed by Clir Carey, seconded by Clir E. Ryan, and agreed,

The Members noted that this was a common issue in a lot of housing estates where several
family members in each household had cars. The A/Senior Executive Engineer, Roads, Traffic
and Cleansing referred to the requirement that a minimum of 15% of green area be retained
in estates, and that emergency vehicles could access estates. It was also agreed that
improved public transport options were also important in addressing the issue.

Reply:

Limerick City and County Council have reviewed the parking arrangements in Radharc na
Coille estate. Following assessment, there is no suitable space available on the existing hard
surface area that can safely accommodate any additional spaces.

Any proposal to consider removing green recreational space in the estate for the purpose of
car parking would impact negatively on the amenity value of the estate by reducing the
open space available for children and adults for play, recreation, and exercise.

In addition, the provision of any new parking facilities would require a specific funding
allocation through either the General Municipal Allocation (GMA) or the Development Fund.
The other funding streams included within the Schedule of Municipal District Works do not
provide for works of this nature.

9. Notice of Motion submitted by Cllr M. Ryan

| will move at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District that further
additional works be carried out on the Cross of Five roads junction in Murroe in order to
ensure a safe junction for all users, pedestrian, active travel, and vehicle users at this
location.

The Notice of Motion was proposed by Clir M. Ryan, seconded by Cllr Teefy, and agreed.

The Members agreed that this junction was dangerous and needed continuous
improvement to ensure it was as safe as possible. They welcomed the recent works carried
out by Limerick City & County Council at this location and they agreed that further work
should be done if possible. The A/Senior Engineer, Ryder Cup confirmed that discussions
had taken place with Transport Infrastructure Ireland (Tl) in relation to a possible funding
stream at this location.

Reply:

Limerick City and County Council undertook significant safety improvement works at the
above location in 2025 under the Safety Improvement Works Grant. The approved funding
allocation for the project was €60,000 and facilitated the delivery of a range of measures,
including the installation of high-friction surfacing, advance warning signage, road markings,
and tree pruning works.

10. Notice of Motion submitted by Cllr Stokes

| will move at the next meeting Cappamore-Killmallock Municipal District that Limerick City
and County Council write to be Minister of State for Older People and Housing, serving
across the Department of Health and the Department of Housing, Local Government and
Heritage, to increase the budget allocated by the Department of Housing, Local Government
and Heritage to ensure that grant schemes can meet the demand and reflect the increase in
construction costs.

The Notice of Motion was proposed by Cllr Stokes, seconded by Cllr Conway, and agreed.

The Members acknowledged the amount of grants being processed and approved in the
Municipal District in recent years. However, they agreed that, with the increase in
construction costs in recent years, that these cost increases were devaluing the grants
approved to people, as it meant that people had to contribute more of their own funds in
addition to grant aid, in order to get necessary work carried out.

Reply:

The number of private house grants in relation to Housing Adaptation Grants for Disabled
People; Mobility Aids Grants; and Housing Aid for Older People grants approved and paid by
Limerick City & County Council in 2025 are as follows:

776 grant applications were approved in 2025;
980 grant approvals had works fully completed and grant payments made to
approved applicants in 2025;

¢ A total amount of €6,531,165.73 was paid in relation to private house grants in 2025.

Funding for the private house grants scheme comprises 85% funding from the Exchequer
with a 15% contribution by the local authority.

The Municipal District of Cappamore-Kilmallock will write to the Minister of State for Older
People and Housing, serving across the Department of Health and the Department of
Housing, Local Government and Heritage, to seek an increase in the budget allocated by the
Department of Housing, Local Government and Heritage to Limerick City & County Council.

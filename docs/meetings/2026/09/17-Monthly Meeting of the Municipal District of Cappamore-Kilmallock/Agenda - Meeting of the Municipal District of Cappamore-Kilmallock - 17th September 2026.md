Notice of Motion submitted by Cllr O’Sullivan

| will move at the next meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District
that, where there is a proposed purchase of lands or houses by an outside
public body or local authority, other than Limerick City and County Council, and
which involves public funding, that the local authority or public body should be
requested to formally consult with this local authority for noting and approval
of the proposed purchase. If this Motion is approved, the request to be
communicated to the Chief Executives of adjoining local authorities.

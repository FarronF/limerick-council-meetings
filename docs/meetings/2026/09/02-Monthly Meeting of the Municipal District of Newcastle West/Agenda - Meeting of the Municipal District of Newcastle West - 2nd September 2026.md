ADJOURNED NOTICES OF MOTION
Notices of Motion submitted by Councillor T. Ruddle

| will move at the next Meeting what plans have the Council made to provide parking
facilities for tour buses on the N21 in Newcastle West, given the substantial
investment in Fullers Folly, which will have a museum and a link up to the Castle and
onto the Square. We hope to see Newcastle West become a major tourist attraction
on this route.

I will move at the next Meeting that the Council consider putting in age friendly
measures such as extra seating in the Demesne for older people.

NOTICES OF MOTION
Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor J. Sheahan

| will move at the next Meeting that Limerick City and County Council put speed
ramps on the approach road to Corbry Estate, Glin in the interest of safety of the
residents.

Notices of Motion submitted by Councillor T. Ruddle

| will move at the next Meeting that the Council consider putting ramps or raised
platforms before crossing at Railway gates on the Old Mill Road at both sides. People
crossing on bikes and on foot from the Greenway are in danger of vehicles travelling
at speed and not slowing down.

' will move at the next Meeting that the Council look at potential areas in Newcastle
West to provide extra car parking spaces.

Notices of Motion submitted by Councillor L. Galvin.

| will move at the next Meeting for a safe pedestrian access to the Greenway at
Murphy's bend, Ballaugh.

| will move at the next Meeting that the Council do complete drainage works at the
junction in Athea village.

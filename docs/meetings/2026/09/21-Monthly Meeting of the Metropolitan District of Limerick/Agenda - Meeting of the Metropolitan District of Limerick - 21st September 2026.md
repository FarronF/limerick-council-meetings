**8.Housing Directorate**

**Part 8 Procedure – 58 Mayorstone Drive, Limerick**

Pursuant to Section 179 of the Planning and Development Act 2000 (as amended) and Part 8

of the Planning and Development Regulations 2001 (as amended) consider the proposal for

the change of use of the existing building from office use to residential use. The

redevelopment of the original building at 58 Mayorstone Drive, to provide 2 no. apartments

consisting of 1 no. one-bed apartment and 1 no. two-bed apartment, construction of a new

first floor extension to the rear, elevational changes, revisions to the front boundary and the

demolition of a shed to the rear of the existing property and all associated site works at 58

Mayorstone Drive, Limerick.

(Report Enclosed).

**QUESTIONS**

**9.Question submitted by Councillor O’Sullivan**

I will ask at the next Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick for the date of the agreed

workshop to receive a full breakdown of costs of the 'Flavours of Limerick' festival.

**10.Question submitted by Councillor Doyle**

I will ask at the next Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick how much it would cost

to restore Tait's Monument Clock at Baker Place and to bring the timepiece and bell-ringing

mechanism back to working order, and if the Council have any plans for this project.

**11.Question submitted by Councillor Secas**

I will ask at the next Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick if funding can be

provided from the Mayoral Fund 2027 for the preparation of a funding application for a library

in Castletroy.

**12.Question submitted by Councillor Secas**

I will ask at the next Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick for an update on the

distributor road linking the Golf Links Road, from its Schoolhouse Road junction, with the

Groody Road at the southern leg of Kilbane Roundabout, further to the update provided at

the February Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick.

[[PAGE_END:3]]

**13.Question submitted by Councillor Slattery**

I will ask at the next Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick for a detailed briefing

on Christmas in Limerick.

**14.Question submitted by Councillor Kiely**

I will ask at the next Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick what progress has been

made to clean up Cals Park in Hyde Road, given the multiple recent requests.

**15.Question submitted by Councillor McSweeney**

I will ask at the next Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick for a full update on the

N69 Clarina Village to Brick Lodge Road Safety Improvement Scheme, to clearly set out:

 The total number of land agreements required to facilitate this scheme;

 The number of land agreements currently in place;

 The number of land agreements where negotiations remain ongoing;

 The anticipated timeframe for concluding all outstanding land agreements;

 Advise has the CPO process been considered to advance the land acquisition required;

and

 The anticipated timeframe for the commencement of construction works on the

scheme.

**16.Question submitted by Councillor McSweeney**

I will ask at the next Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick for a full update on the

Limerick to Patrickswell Roadspace Reallocation Project that has received funding from TII in

2026, to clearly set out:

 What is proposed to be included as part of this scheme (improved pedestrian facilities,

street lighting etc.);

 Proposed statutory process for project Part 8 or Section 38;

 Indicative timeline for project delivery; and

 Any further information deemed relevant.

**17.Question submitted by Councillor Slattery**

I will ask at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick for a full itemised

breakdown of all expenditure incurred in respect of the 'Summer in the City' programme. The

information should be provided in tabulated format and include:

 A description of each item of expenditure;

 The amount incurred;

 The location to which each item of expenditure relates;

 All payroll and staffing costs; and

 All non-payroll costs.

The breakdown should include the total expenditure incurred on the 'Summer in the City'

programme and clearly identify the overall cost associated with each individual location, The

Crescent O'Connell Avenue, Steamboat Quay, Honan's Quay, Arthur's Quay and any other

areas within the City.

**18.Question submitted by Councillor Leddin**

I will ask at the next Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick for an update on the

removal of outdated street signage, to include digital car park signs.

**19.Question submitted by Councillor Leddin**

I will ask at the next Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick, in the absence of a

corporate sponsor, to upgrade the Shannon Bridge roundabout and adjacent traffic islands,

all of which are in a poor state.

**20.Question submitted by Councillor Hartigan**

I will ask at the next Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick for answers to the

following questions in tabular form:

 How many demolition orders has Limerick City and County Council issued, within the

Metropolitan District of Limerick, in respect of private residential properties over the

past five years;

 What were the reasons for each demolition order being issued; and

 In each case, was the property deemed to pose a hazard or risk to public safety.

[[PAGE_END:5]]

**21.Question submitted by Councillor O’Donovan**

Irish Cement Community Liaison Committee, including the total amount of funding collected

to date through the Community Gain Fund, and for an update on the position regarding the

provision for three elected representatives to sit on the Community Liaison Committee.

**22.Question submitted by Councillor O’Donovan**

I will ask at the next Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick for the procedures in

place for social housing applicants who are homeless, and do not have a permanent

residential address; what documentation such applicants are required to provide; and how

disadvantage an applicant in maintaining or progressing their housing application.

**23.Question submitted by Councillor Kiely**

undeveloped land parcels suitable for residential developments owned by Limerick City and

**24.Question submitted by Councillor Gavan**

**25.Question submitted by Councillor Beasley**

Limerick City East, City West and City North, showing:

 The length of time each property has been vacant/boarded-up;

 The projected timeframe for bringing each property back into use.

Please provide the timeframes in weeks/months, broken down individually by property.

**NOTICES OF MOTION**

**33.Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Leddin**

I will move at the next Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that this District

writes to the Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage and the Minister for

Tourism, Culture, Arts, Gaeltacht, Sport and Media requesting them to enable the

Metropolitan District of Limerick to have the ability to introduce a tourism tax on hotels or

short-term rentals, and that monies collected are ring fenced for tourism and marketing

related initiatives of the Metropolitan District.

**34.Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Secas**

I will move at the next Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that this District

writes to Uisce Éireann requesting that, in the event of a burst water main, within the

Metropolitan District of Limerick, which results in an interruption to water supply,

immediate compensation be provided to all affected residents and that an adequate supply

of alternative drinking water be distributed promptly to every impacted household for the

duration of the outage.

**35.Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Secas**

I will move at the next Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that this District calls

on the Council to establish a dedicated supplementary development contribution scheme

for the wider Castletroy area, with all development contribution levies generated within the

area ringfenced and reinvested exclusively in infrastructure, community facilities, and other

strategic projects required to support the area's significant growth.

**36.Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Kiely**

I will move at the next Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that a review takes

place on the managed biodiversity areas in housing estates, especially in Kennedy Park and

Glasgow Park, to ensure residents in the area are not prevented from using it, with due

consideration for our climate targets in mind.

[[PAGE_END:8]]

**37.Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Leddin**

I will move at the next Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City

and County Council consider the introduction of a dedicated WhatsApp number to allow the

public to log calls with relevant photos of issues that require repair or attention as they

arise.

**38.Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor O’Donovan**

I will move at the next Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick for the options now

available to residents to dispose of unwanted clothing and textiles following the removal of

clothing banks, and that clear and updated information on available textile recycling options

is published by the Council.

**39.Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Doyle**

I will move at the next Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick this District writes to

the Minister of State Mr. Kevin Moran, responsible for the Office of Public Works,

requesting that the new 14 storey building in Opera Square, Limerick be named the 'Donogh

O’Malley Tower' in honour of the late Minister for Education and Limerick man, Donogh

O’Malley, who announced free secondary education sixty years ago on 10th September

1966.

**40.Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Gavan**

I will move at the next Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that this District calls

on the Minister of Enterprise, Trade and Tourism, working in conjunction with Revenue, to

urgently reform the planned increased charges to tobacco and nicotine licensing for 2027,

to ensure a tiered system of charges, so that small independent retailers will not be charged

the current proposed flat fees of €1,800 per annum, which will disproportionately impact

small businesses and most likely result in closure of many small operators across our towns

and villages

**41.Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Gavan**

I will move at the next Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that this District calls

on the Minister of Transport to immediately abandon planned transport increases from

January, maintaining affordable fares for passengers whilst committing to significant

improvements in service provision locally and nationwide.

[[PAGE_END:9]]

**42.Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Beasley**

I will move at the next Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City

and County Council carries out a traffic survey and signal-timing review of the Rhebogue

Hill/Rhebogue Road/Park Road junction with the Dublin Road, focused on the morning and

evening peak periods (8am–10am and 3pm–6pm), and reports back to Council with its

findings and any recommended remedial action, including a review of the routing of

articulated heavy goods vehicles through the junction where necessary.

**43.Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Kiely**

I will move at the next Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that this District

writes to the Department of Transport and the National Transport Authority to ask for

additional road budget specifically for bus routes in the city where there has been a serious

deterioration with visible tracks on roads across the city due to the weight of the new bus

fleet.

**44.Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Donoghue**

I will move at the next Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City

and County Council coordinates its internal stakeholders, such as Planning, Building Control,

Conservation, Roads, Active Travel and so on, to hold regular one-stop-shop clinics for pre-

planning applications to provide coordinated advice to potential applicants.

**45.Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Donoghue**

I will move at the next Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City

and County Council installs a pedestrian crossing at the junction of Newenham Street to

Barrington Street to allow pedestrians to safely cross Quinlan Street.

**46.Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Hickey-O’Mara**

I will move at the next Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that measures are

swiftly taken to improve public lighting in Shelbourne Park, thereby helping to address

safety concerns among members of the public, particularly as we enter the darker half of

the year.

[[PAGE_END:10]]

**47.Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Hickey-O’Mara**

I will move at the next Meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that this District

writes to both the Minister of Health, Jennifer Carroll McNeil and Minister for State with

responsibility for Mental Health, Mary Butler, to call on them to:

 Increase mental health spending to 10% of the total Health Budget in line with

Sláintecare in Budget 2027;

 Implement the recommendations set out by the IACP in their Pre-Budget submission

2027.

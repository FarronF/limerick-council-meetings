Question submitted by Councillor M. Collins

***I will ask at the next Meeting if Limerick City and County Council will open the access at***

***Bridge Street, Newcastle West leading to the River Arra. As part of this Motion, it is***

***important to make the necessary safety measures along the river bank.***

REPLY:

This access has been closed for 15 years as the access points and pathway do not meet any

standards in terms of safety and accessibility. If this access is to be opened, then a detailed

report and costing will have to be prepared to determine if the proposal is feasible.

8.

Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor M. Collins

***I will move at the next Meeting*** ***that an appropriate assessment and plan be drawn up for***

***traffic calming and pedestrian crossings in the town of Dromcollogher. When complete,***

***funding to be sought to complete the works as soon as possible.***

The Motion was seconded by Councillor Scanlan.

REPLY:

A Draft Public Realm and Tourism Plan was prepared for Dromcollogher in 2023 by the

Community Department of Limerick City and County Council. There was significant public

consultation associated with the preparation of the draft plan which was also presented at a

Workshop to the District Councillors in August 2023. Traffic calming, pedestrian crossings etc.

formed part of the plan which was paused due to concerns over loss of car parking spaces.

In light of this Notice of Motion is it timely to revisit what was proposed in this very detailed

draft plan and consider if the plan or elements of the plan are appropriate and could be

delivered subject to available funding.

In proposing the Motion, Councillor Collins stated that the town of Dromcollogher has grown

immensely over the last number of years, with a large secondary school and buses travelling

through the town on a daily basis. Councillor Collins asked that priority be given to works

being carried out in the town of Dromcollogher. Councillor Ruddle also referred to the need

for traffic calming measures in front of the GAA field in Dromcollogher.
---
4

A brief discussion took place on the Draft Public Realm and Tourism Plan which was prepared

for Dromcollogher in 2023. In replying, the Senior Executive Engineer stated that a

comprehensive public consultation process took place as part of this Plan and that any

proposals for traffic calming would potentially result in the loss of car parking spaces. The

Senior Executive Engineer and the Director stated therefore that a holistic approach is

required on this matter.

Following discussion, it was agreed that this Plan be revisited and that the Meetings

Administrator would organise a Workshop on the Draft Public Realm and Tourism Plan for

Dromcollogher.

***I will move at the next Meeting that immediate action be taken to provide pedestrian***

***crossings at three pinch points on the Square, Dromcollogher, in the interest of safety.***

The Motion was seconded by Councillor Collins.

REPLY:

A Draft Public Realm and Tourism Plan was prepared for Dromcollogher in 2023 by the

Community Department of Limerick City and County Council. There was significant public

consultation associated with the preparation of the draft plan which was also presented at a

Workshop to the District Councillors in August 2023. Traffic calming, pedestrian crossings etc.

formed part of the plan which was paused due to concerns over loss of car parking spaces.

In light of this Notice of Motion is it timely to revisit what was proposed in this very detailed

draft plan and consider if the plan or elements of the plan are appropriate and could be

delivered subject to available funding.

pedestrians when crossing The Square in Dromcollogher and requested that measures be

delivering the scheme. A further update will issue following the January Meeting.

In proposing the Motion, Councillor Collins referred to a number road traffic accidents which

The following items were also raised.

An update was sought on the upgrade of the wastewater treatment plant for Newcastle West

and the temporary onsite treatment for the Newcastle West plant. In replying, the Meetings

Administrator read the response received from Uisce Éireann which stated that the Newcastle

West Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) upgrade works are currently progressing as part

of RC4 (2025-2029) investment plan. She stated that until the plant is upgraded, additional

connections might be feasible if pre-treatment is provided by the developers to discharge

standards acceptable to Uisce Éireann and that once Newcastle West WWTP upgrade works

are completed the developers will be able to decommission and remove the temporary

treatment once the upgrade is complete.

A discussion took place on this matter and the ongoing maintenance of the temporary onsite

treatment plant post installation. Following discussion, it was agreed that the Meetings

Administrator would seek further clarification on responsibility for maintenance of the onsite

treatment plant post installation.

The urgent need for social housing in the District was raised.
---
6

The condition of the road surface at South Quay, Newcastle West was raised and to an

Emergency Pavement Scheme report which is with Transport Infrastructure Ireland to address

defected pavement areas outlined. It was agreed that the Council will continue to follow up

with Transport Infrastructure Ireland on this matter. The condition of the road surface outside

the Courtenay School was also raised and a request was made to have an audit of road

surfaces carried out in the town of Newcastle West. In replying, the Senior Executive Engineer

stated that all areas of the town will be considered as part of the Road Works Scheme 2026.

An update was sought on the upgrading of the public lighting on the westward bound bus

stop fronting the Courtenay Lodge and in replying the Senior Executive Engineer stated that

Active Travel is currently exploring the option of enhancement of the bus stop and public

lighting at this bus stop.

Reference was made to the bollards at Calvary cemetery and an update was sought on the

changing of the bollards to a plastic type to alleviate damage to vehicles. In replying, the

Senior Executive Engineer stated that he will follow up on this matter.

An update was sought on the Part VIII housing scheme at Cois Na Féile in Abbeyfeale.

The volume of requests for pedestrian crossings and traffic calming measures in the District

was raised and if a Programme of Works could be put in place together with funding to be

made available for these works.

An update was sought on the carrying out of safety works at the N21 Devon Road Cross and

N21 Ward’s Cross. Reference was also made to traffic calming measures from Moloney’s

Garage towards Dalton’s Cross in Abbeyfeale. It was agreed that further updates would be

sought from the relevant Directorate.

The provision of a pedestrian crossing and bollards at the new playground in Carrickerry was

raised and to a site investigation which is to be carried out at this location. It was agreed that

the Roads Team would revert to the Members on this matter.

Prior to conclusion of the Meeting, it was agreed that the Meetings Administrator would

**(b)**To Discuss Christmas Festivities in Newcastle West.

**(e)**To Discuss Housing in the District.

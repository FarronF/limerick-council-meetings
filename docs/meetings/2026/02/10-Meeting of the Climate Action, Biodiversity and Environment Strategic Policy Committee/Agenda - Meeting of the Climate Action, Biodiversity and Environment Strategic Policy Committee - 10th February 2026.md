**3rd February 2026**

**To: The Chairperson and Each Member of the Climate Action, Biodiversity and Environment**

**Strategic Policy Committee of Limerick City & County Council**

You are invited to attend a blended Meeting of the Climate Action, Biodiversity and Environment

Strategic Policy Committee to be held in the Council Chamber, Dooradoyle and online on MS Teams on

Tuesday 10th February 2026 at 1:30pm. The business to be transacted is set out in the appended

agenda.

**Signed:**

**CLLR JOE LEDDIN**

**CHAIRPERSON OF THE CLIMATE ACTION, BIODIVERSITY AND ENVIRONMENT**

**STRATEGIC POLICY COMMITTEE**

**Agenda**

1.To consider the adoption of the Minutes of the Climate Action, Biodiversity and Environment

Strategic Policy Committee Meeting that was held on 03rd December 2025.

2.Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor M. Donoghue (*referred from December*

*Metropolitan Meeting*). I will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of

Limerick that the current Presentation of Waste Byelaws are updated to reflect the current

volume of required segregation of waste, and that a programme of clear communication

around these updated byelaws is developed.

3.Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor P. Reale (*referred from December Metropolitan*

*Meeting*) - I will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that this

Council provides a public schedule of Bring Bank and Clothes Bins collection days at all

locations across the Metropolitan District so that people looking to recycle can schedule their

trips on days that these bins are emptied and have adequate space to dispose of such items.

This will further allow this Council to alternate collections between various locations.

4.Feasibility Study for New Waste Management System for Limerick City Centre –Stakeholder

Engagement - KEO Consultants – Edward Wilkins.

5.The Million Trees Project – Can tree planting provide a sustainable, long-lasting

environmental legacy to the Ryder Cup – Cathal Brodie, Senior Executive Scientist.

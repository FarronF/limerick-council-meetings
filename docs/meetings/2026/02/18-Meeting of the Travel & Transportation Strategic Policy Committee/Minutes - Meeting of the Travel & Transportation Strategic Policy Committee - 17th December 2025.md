**Item 2: Update on progress of Limerick Shannon Metropolitan**

**Area Transport Strategy (LSMATS 2022) Implementation Plan:**

**– Mr. Hugh McGrath, S.E.**

questions

 With reference to LSMATS bus stops/Bus Connects is

be accommodated as plan develops.

 Met with Bus Éireann however no start date for 310 bus

service yet but will update accordingly.

 Parklets were coming to end-of-life and LCCC will look at

future of each on a case by case basis.

**Item 3: Draft LCCC Accessibility Strategy: – Mr. Toby Goodwin,**

**Behavioral Scientist.**

T.G. delivered a presentation on the Draft LCCC Accessibility

Strategy highlighting the strategic approach required under

statutory obligations to ensure fit-for- purposes access and

delivery of services and facilities.

Mrs. Browne welcomed T.G.’s walkability studies and asked

what was next. She invited T.G. to the Step Forward group in

January 2026.

The members welcomed and supported the strategy. P.L.

appreciated the support, adding that it will be a work in progress.

It will be critical to co-ordinate all capital projects to adhere to

the strategy.

In response to queries raised, T.G. confirmed that it is currently

in draft stage and he will link in with rest of the organization. He

added that a lot has yet to be resolved with regard to accessible

bus stops. He will meet Step Forward in January 2026 and

thanked all members for their support.

**Item 4: Update on the Integration of Active Travel Programme**

**with Emergency Services: - Diarmuid Conway, A.E.**

D.C. delivered a summary of the report on the study of

Integration of the Active Travel Programme with Emergency

Services; the summary was previously delivered on the 26th
---
5

November 2025 to the elected members of the Metropolitan

District by the appointed consultants B.J. Barry Transportation

Ltd.

D.G. summarised how the report examines how the

implementation of Active Travel Infrastructure interacts with

and affects the operations of emergency services within the

Limerick Metropolitan area and was commissioned by LCCC. The

study was undertaken to assess potential implications of

emergency response times, accessibility and operational

efficiency, while ensuring that the delivery of sustainable

transport infrastructure aligns with emergency service

requirements.

He added that the findings could inform a wider national

assessment by the NTA and may guide future Active Travel

Schemes. He confirmed the public launch is planned for January

2026

The members thanked D.C. for the presentation.

**Item 6: Item for discussion – Update on approach to**

**governance and operations of signalised pedestrian crossings**

**in Limerick City and County:**

**submitted by Mr. Farron Flaherty, Community/Voluntary PPN**

Mr. Flaherty queried the approach to governance and

operations of signalised pedestrian crossings and asked what

local policies the Council uses to determine the configuration of

pedestrian crossing technologies.

Following discussion by the members, HMcG clarified the

following: -

 There is priority for pedestrians on the Dock Road / O’

Curry Street junction

 There is also definite priority on Clare Street crossing

Broad Street.

 City centre and the environs will not be completed with

the same level of priority, it takes funding and will be done

on a stage-by-stage basis.

 Prudence is also required in the event of a potential

schemes in an area which would take precedence.

 The executive is currently working on a protocol on how

junction upgrades are prioritised with pedestrian facilities.
---
7

 Specialised consultants have been appointed to meet all the

various schemes for all public realm, Active Travel and

strategy.

HMcG added that the next step is to draft a document which will

address some of the queries raised. It is educational awareness

for the council of what’s out there. He welcomed the item being

raised for discussion and he is sure Mr. Flaherty would be

supportive of a written protocol being put in place which can be

referenced if any concerns raised.

The Chair thanked Mr. Mc Grath for the response.

**A.O.B. - Glyphosate Cllr. S. Hickey O’Mara**

Cllr. S. Hickey-O’Mara raised the issue of using glyphosate and

requested that the Council do the following when using

glyphosate or other herbicides in a community as follows:

1. Inform the community in advance via a letter drop indicating

the precise location that it will be used in.

2. Erect warning signs or notices at the precise location before

and after the use of glyphosate or other toxic herbicides

3. Use any viable alternative to glyphosate or other toxic

herbicides in their work

4. Agree to end use of glyphosate and other toxic herbicides.
---
9

In response P.L. advised the following: –

 As part of the public realm and roadway maintenance, the

Cleansing and Roads Section spray on hard surfaces on

public roads and footpaths and is used in a targeted and

controlled manner.

 It is applied at the appropriate rate under the correct

conditions by professional users registered with the Dept of

Agriculture, Foods and marine and is in accordance with the

requirements of their Risk Assessments and the European

commission did approve its use

 LCCC will review one policy that works for all, examine all

elements and will revert to the next SPC Meeting with an

update.

Following a discussion, the members wished to add their support

on the matter.

Cllr. O’Mara added that the item was originally brought to his

attention by concerned residents who have children and pets in

a residential area. There was no warning of when spraying was

going to take place or details of a location. The issue has been

supported by every member present, and it has been going back

and forth for well over a month. He thanked the executive and

members for the time given to the topic.

The chair wished to remind members that this is not a Motion, it

is an item for discussion
---
10

M.C. advised members that the matter will go forward as a

recommendation to the CPG. On their approval, it will then go

forward to the full council meeting.

The Chair welcomed C.N. who recently joined the team

following John Cregan’s resignation.

**Recommendation from Travel and Transportation SPC:**

**That the Council will research and prepare a draft policy**

**regarding the use of glyphosate and other herbicides and**

**alternatives.**

This concluded the business of the meeting.

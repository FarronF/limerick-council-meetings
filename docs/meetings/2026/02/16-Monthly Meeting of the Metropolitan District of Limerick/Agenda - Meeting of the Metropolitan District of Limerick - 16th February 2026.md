NOTICES OF MOTION

23. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor J. Leddin

I will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City
and County Council provide an update and outline the next steps in the delivery of a new
community centre for Raheen/Dooradoyle following recommendation in the previously
published feasibility report completed in 2023.

24. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor S. Beasley

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City
and County Council conduct a comprehensive audit of all locations across the Metropolitan
District where cycle paths intersect with road junctions, to ensure adequate signage is in
place to clearly indicate right of way for both cyclists and motorists, and where cyclists do
not have right of way, to install additional signage specifically at busy junctions requesting
cyclists to wait and dismount when crossing.

25. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor J. Leddin

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City
and County Council invite Uisce Eireann to a meeting to discuss the upgrade of old clay
sewer pipes currently causing consistent blockages for residents, especially in the
Ballinacurra area of the city.

26. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor S. Kiely

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City
and County Council start the process to extinguish the right of way at Wallers Well Roxboro
Road.

27. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor C. Slattery

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City
and County Council make improvements to the traffic and lighting layout at the entrance to
Woodlawn Park given the fact that two new housing schemes have been established in the
area.

28. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor E. Secas

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City
and County Council urgently adopts a balanced planning approach for the Castletroy area
and prioritises and allocates the resources required to deliver the necessary infrastructure
and community services to support housing developments already delivered, currently
underway, and pending developments approved through existing planning permissions.

29. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor U. Gavan

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City
and County Council write to the Minister for Education regarding changes to the teaching of
Irish in both primary and secondary level Junior and Leaving Certificate cycles, requesting a
greater focus on oral communication.

30. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor U. Gavan

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that a green roof be
installed onto new and existing bus shelters in Limerick City.

31. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor P. Doyle

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City
and County Council undertake a traffic study on the Groody Road, Castletroy to take account
of the large-scale residential development planned, known as Whitebox Student Campus,
Groody Road, reference 25/60113 and for future developments. In addition to plan
measures to avoid traffic congestion on this road.

32. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor S. Hickey O’Mara

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that, as a way of
promoting youth engagement with local culture, a pilot scheme is developed that offers
young people living in Limerick aged 18-22 the opportunity to apply for a Local Culture
Voucher from Limerick Arts Office, of an amount to be agreed, which they can use to attend
events at participating local cultural venues. Cultural venues can then redeem these
vouchers from the Limerick Arts Office.

33. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor E. O'Donovan

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Traffic
Management run a high visibility campaign and presence to address parking enforcement
issues in residential areas outside of the core central business district.

To: The Mayor and Each Member of Limerick City and County Council

A Chomhairleoir, a chara,

The Mayor, Councillor Gerald Mitchell, has called a Special Meeting of Limerick City and
County Council to be held in the Council Chamber, Dooradoyle, and Online, on Monday, 26"
February, 2024, at 2pm. The business to be transacted is set out below.

Meetings Administrator.

1. Draft Patrickswell Local Area Plan 2024-2030

AGENDA

To consider the proposed Material Alterations of the Draft Patrickswell Local Area Plan
2024-2030 and the report of the Chief Executive dated 31* January, 2024, on the
submissions received on the Material Alterations to the Draft Patrickswell Local Area
Pian, and decide whether the Local Area Plan shall be made or amended in accordance
with Section 20(3)(n) of the Planning and Development Act, 2000 (as amended).

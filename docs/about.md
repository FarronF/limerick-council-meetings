# About the Limerick Council Meetings Archive

This archive contains text extracted from publicly available meeting agendas and minutes published by [Limerick City and County Council](https://www.limerick.ie/council/your-council/meetings). The records are organized to make it easier to search the contents of council documents and find meetings by date.

## Why this archive exists

Council decisions affect communities across Limerick, but finding a specific discussion in records spread across many PDF files can be difficult. This project aims to make those public records more accessible and searchable, helping residents, journalists, researchers, and others follow the council's work.

## About the records

- **Scanned documents:** Some PDFs are scans rather than text-based files. Optical character recognition (OCR) is used to extract their text, so errors may occur. Documents processed with OCR include a disclaimer.
- **Minutes and meeting dates:** Documents are organized with the meeting at which they were uploaded or formally adopted. Minutes are often filed with the meeting where they were approved, which may be later than the meeting they record.
- **Images:** Images, maps, and diagrams are omitted from the extracted text and marked as `(Image omitted)`. Refer to the original PDF on the council website for visual details.

The processing pipeline is generated using [limerick-council-tools](https://github.com/FarronF/limerick-council-tools).

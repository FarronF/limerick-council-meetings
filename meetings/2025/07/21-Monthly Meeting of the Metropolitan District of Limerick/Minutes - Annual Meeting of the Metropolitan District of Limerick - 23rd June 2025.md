**MINUTES OF PROCEEDINGS AT ANNUAL MEETING OF THE METROPOLITAN DISTRICT OF LIMERICK**

**HELD IN THE COUNCIL CHAMBER,** **MERCHANT’S QUAY** **AND ONLINE, ON MONDAY, 23RD** **JUNE,**

**2025 AT 9.45AM**

**PRESENT IN THE CHAIR:**

Councillor K. O’Hanlon, Cathaoirleach

**MEMBERS PRESENT:**

Councillors Beasley, Benson, Butler, Daly, Donoghue, Doyle, Gavan, Hartigan, Hickey-O’Mara, Kiely,

Kilcoyne, Leddin, McSweeney, O’Donovan, O’Sullivan, Pond, Reale, Secas, Slattery and Talukder.

**OFFICIALS IN ATTENDANCE:**

Director, Environment, Climate Action and Shared Services (Mr. K. Lehane), Meetings Administrator

(Ms. A. Foley), Senior Executive Officer, Metropolitan District (Ms. S. Reidy), Senior Staff Officer,

Corporate Services, Governance and Customer Services (Ms. C. Sheehy), Assistant Staff Officer,

Corporate Services and Governance (Ms. S. Carroll).

The outgoing Cathaoirleach of the Metropolitan District, Councillor O’Hanlon welcomed the

Members, Officials, Members of the Media and the Public to the Annual Meeting to elect the

Cathaoirleach and Leas Cathaoirleach of the Metropolitan District of Limerick. He also extended a

welcome to Minister Kieran O’Donnell.

The Cathaoirleach stated that it had been a privilege and honour to serve in the role. He thanked

his family and friends for their support during the year. He also expressed his gratitude to the Leas

Chathaoirleach, his Party Colleagues and the Elected Members for their support, throughout what

had been a very busy year. The Cathaoirleach thanked Councillor McSweeney for his support in his

role as Príomh Chomhairleoir. He also extended his appreciation to the Management and Staff for

their help and support during his term.

The Members congratulated Councillor O’Hanlon on his successful year as Cathaoirleach of the

Metropolitan District and commended him on the professional manner in which he chaired the

Metropolitan Meetings and carried out his duties.

The outgoing Cathaoirleach declared Councillor Daniel Butler elected as Cathaoirleach of the

Metropolitan District of Limerick and congratulated him on his election.

Members congratulated Councillor Butler on his election as Cathaoirleach and wished him every

success in the year ahead.

The newly elected Cathaoirleach, Councillor Butler said he was delighted and honoured to be

elected as Cathaoirleach of the Metropolitan District of Limerick. He expressed his gratitude to the

Members that voted for him, and particularly his party colleagues for their continued trust and

support. He thanked his family, some of whom were in the Chamber, for their encouragement and

support.

Councillor Butler acknowledged the work of Councillor O’Hanlon as Cathaoirleach over the previous

twelve months, establishing a platform for this Chamber under the new Council structure.

He highlighted that while housing remains the most pressing issue, the priority during his term will

be Limerick city centre. He stated that though there are challenges, our city centre must be a place

where people want to live, work and spend time. A place that is vibrant, safe, welcoming and full

of life. A place where small businesses and local enterprise can flourish, where public spaces are

alive with activity and where people feel proud to call home. He would be proposing that the city

centre be made a standing agenda item for every Metropolitan meeting during his term. He stated

that this would ensure discussions were structured and efficient and most importantly would ensure

this vital issue gets the consistent focus and attention it deserves. He highlighted that while

everything cannot be done in a one-year term, as a Council, we can make real tangible progress on

this and can support initiatives that bring footfall back into the city centre, improve public spaces

and make it easier for businesses to thrive.

Councillor Butler stated that he will ensure that every Councillor is heard and supported in delivering

for their communities, as when each succeed in their areas, Limerick as a whole succeeds. He

finished by saying it was a great honour to accept the role of Cathaoirleach.

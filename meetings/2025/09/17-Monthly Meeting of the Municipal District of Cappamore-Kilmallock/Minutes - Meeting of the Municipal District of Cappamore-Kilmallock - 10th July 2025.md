Councillor G. Conway, Cathaoirleach.

Councillors Carey, O'Sullivan, E. Ryan, Stokes and Teefy.

their Tailteann Cup Final against Kildare in Croke Park on Saturday 12™ July.

2. Declaration of Interests

The Meetings Administrator stated that, in line with Part 15 of the Local Government Act,
2001, as amended, and the Code of Conduct, he was inviting those present to notify the
Cathaoirleach of any declaration of interest in any item due for discussion at the Meeting.

There was no interest declared.

3. Property Disposal

a) Circulated, report of the Senior Executive Officer, Property Management dated 30”
June, 2025 setting out the intention to bring the Section 183 Notice to Full Council
for approval:

Disposal of property, in the form of Lease Agreement, comprising of 0.0298
Acres/0.012 hectares situate at Sheare’s Street, Kilmallock, Co. Limerick. (see
attached report).

This proposal was welcomed by the Members and the report, as circulated, was noted by
Members.

The Senior Executive Engineer gave a presentation to the Elected Members of the District on
progress to date on the 2025 Municipal District Road Maintenance Works Programme.

He thanked all the engineering, technical, roads and administrative staff for their work to date
in completing a lot of the work in the programme already for the Municipal District. He also
confirmed that the favourable weather conditions in recent months, along with the
availability of contractors, had also assisted in progressing works.

The Members thanked the Senior Executive Engineer for his presentation and commended
all the staff on their work to date on the delivery of the Road Works Programme. They
noted that the provision of adequate resources to complete road works was a priority for
the Municipal District and also that this would be important when considering the Councils
Annual Budget Programme for 2026.

They also stressed the importance of Active Travel works being completed in the Municipal
Districts, as well as in the Metropolitan District area.

5. Question submitted by Councillor Teefy

| will ask at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District if Limerick City
& County Council has any plans to continue the existing Footpath on the R512 road from
Holycross to the Ard na Ri Nursing Home.

Reply:
The Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District Office has no plans or resources to extend the
footpath (in excess of 300m) on the R512 from Holycross to the Ard Na Ri Nursing Home.

6. Any Other Business

The Meetings Administrator confirmed that Liz Moloney, Clerical Officer had replaced Breed
Sheedy on the administration of Housing Grants in the Municipal District. He also confirmed
that applications under the Housing Aid for Older Persons scheme was currently paused due
to a lack of funding, and that additional funding was being sought for same.

An update was also given to the Members on the processing and payment of grants under
the Community Initiatives Grants scheme and this was welcomed by the Members.

The date and time for the next Monthly Meeting was agreed for Wednesday 17”
September, 2025 at 3pm. It was also agreed to invite Superintendent Michael Fleming,
Limerick County Community Engagement Functional Area to attend a Briefing before the
Monthly Meeting to discuss policing matters in the District.

This concluded the business of the meeting.

Signed:
Cathaoirleach

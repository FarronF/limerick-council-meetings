Following the adoption of the Minutes discussion arose pertaining to General Municipal

Allocation Grant Scheme and administration of same. Councillor Teskey was of the opinion

General Municipal Allocation monies should be reimbursed to the Local Authority when

an asset in community group ownership is disposed by way of property sale for monetary

gain.

Councillor Teskey requested the General Municipal Allocations application form be

amended going forward to include a claw back clause to ensure monies revert back to the

Local Authority when a community group decides to dispose of the asset for which they

have received GMA funding.

Following further discussion on the matter by Members, Director Vincent Murray, Director

of Service, Planning and Place-Making advised Members details of the issues outlined by

Councillor Teskey in relation to General Municipal Allocations be forwarded to Corporate

Services Department, City Hall for clarification.

**8.**

**Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor J. O’Donoghue**

**I will move at the next Meeting that we receive a presentation from the N/M20 office**

**on the latest update on the proposed project from the Cork/Limerick boundary to**

**Patrickswell.**

The N/M20 Project Coordinator, Mid West National Road Design Office gave Members a

presentation to update them on the current status of the N/M20 Cork to Limerick

motorway project. Following the presentation Councillor O’Donoghue agreed the new

road system is warranted however was of the opinion of the following:

 The new motorway section from Atyflynn to Charleville would be of no benefit to

the local population due to the number of tolls and local access options which

would divert the local motorist back on to the old N20 road.

 The inclusion of the Active Travel Route element of the project was not necessary

and he queried the upkeep and maintenance costs of same.

 The old N20 would still require significant maintenance due to usage by local

population.

 Noise factors would have implications on the local population due to proximity of

properties to the new motorway.

 Local land quarrying and haulage for the duration of the project construction would

cause significant disruption to local homeowners and land owners.

 Proposed route will introduce significant traffic both vehicular and agricultural to

the town of Croom.

The N/M20 Project Coordinator, Mid West National Road Design Office advised the Active

Travel element of the Project is government policy and five per cent of the cost of all new

motorway projects is provided for an Active Travel greenway element.

Following further discussion The N/M20 Project Coordinator, Mid West National Road

Design Office agreed to take consideration of Councillor O’Donoghue’s observations.

**Housing Strategy & Non-LA Housing Construction**

**Social Housing Delivery**
---
4

Circulated, report of the Housing Strategy & Non-LA Housing Construction Directorate

dated 9th May, 2025 setting out details in relation to the Social Housing Delivery for the

Adare-Rathkeale Municipal District.

The Senior Executive Officer, Strategy & Non LA Construction updated Members on the

social housing progress in the District to date and advised it is estimated 53 per cent of

the Limerick City and County Council social housing target will be achieved by end of 2025

and 70 per cent be achieved by the end of 2026 due to lack of capital funding and also

inadequate waste water services currently hindering development. Members were

disappointed with the proposed outturns for social housing in the Adare-Rathkeale

Municipal District given the number of people currently in need of housing.

Councillor Teskey raised the issue of proposed social housing for Adare and where this

project stands at the present time. The Senior Engineer, LA Housing Construction &

Maintenance advised that tender for 13 units in Deerpark will commence by the end of

July 2025 and be complete by the end of 2026 with negotiations ongoing with three

landowners to enable land transfer to allow for access to local services for this

development. He further advised drainage issues for these units which has not been

straightforward also has to be resolved by the end of July.

**6.**

**Social Housing in Pallaskenry**

The Senior Executive Officer, Strategy & Non LA Construction updated Members on the

social housing development in Pallaskenry advising a funding strategy has been identified

and Limerick City and County Council would be taking over the development due to

certification and warranty difficulties the Approved Housing Body, Focus Housing, could

not resolve with the Housing Finance Agency. She further advised Members that her

Department is confident of closing out the first phase of eight housed by mid to late

August 2025 with the second phase to be allocated by December 2025.

Following discussion Members requested a Special Meeting be convened to discuss:

 Social Housing issued in Adare.

 Properties acquired in the Adare-Rathkeale District under Compulsory Purchase

Order.

 Properties acquired in Adare under the Derelict Sites Legislation.

The Meetings Administrator agreed to liaise with the relevant Departments on this

request and to revert to Members with an agreed date and time for the Special Meeting.

It was also agreed the LA Construction Department would provide updates to members

every three weeks going forward.

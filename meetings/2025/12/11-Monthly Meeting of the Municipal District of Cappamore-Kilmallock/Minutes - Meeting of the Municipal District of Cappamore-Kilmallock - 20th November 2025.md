3. Deputation

The Cathaoirleach welcomed Max Hennessy from Kilmallock Tourism CE Scheme to the
Meeting. He proceeded to give a PowerPoint presentation on the activities of the Kilmallock
Tourism CE Scheme.

He explained that there were currently 23 participants on the Scheme. The CE scheme
participants receive an additional €20.50 to their social welfare payment for their work on
the scheme, while a material budget of €13.67 was paid to the CE Scheme for each
participant per week.

He outlined how the CE Scheme maintained and cut grass on 47.3 acres in the Kilmallock
area and this included Ballingaddy Graveyard and Tankardstown Graveyard.

The scheme was administered from an office based in the Municipal District Offices in
Kilmallock and was supported by an annual €27,600 Service Level Agreement (SLA), between
the CE Scheme and Limerick City & County Council, over the past 5 years.

Specific areas in Kilmallock being maintained included the Memorial Walk; Priory Walk;
West Wall Walkway; the Famine Park; People’s Park; as well as Housing Estates in Kilmallock.

He then outlined the CE Schemes involvement in the Community Climate Action Programme
(CCAP) Project Plan which included the refurbishment and retrofitting of the museum in
Kilmallock with solar PV and energy upgrades, as well as developing a biodiversity-rich
Climate Garden to the rear of the museum. This was with the intention of establishing
Kilmallock as a model rural Climate Action Hub and supporting long-term sustainability,
heritage protection, and community resilience.

The Cathaoirleach and Elected Members thanked Mr Hennessy for the presentation and
praised the work being done in Kilmallock and its environs by the Kilmallock Tourism CE
Scheme and agreed that there was a noticeable improvement in Kilmallock in recent times.
They also commended the work done by the CE Scheme, along with other local groups and
organisations, as part of the preparations for the visit of President Michael D. Higgins earlier
in the year for the National Famine Commemoration ceremony, which had been a great
success for the District and Limerick.

be made available for the collapsed wall at Ballingaddy [Old] Graveyard and, if so, is there a
timeline for commencement of works.

Reply:

A business case has been submitted to the Council to approve appropriate funding through
a loan to carry out restoration works in various cemeteries throughout Limerick, one of
which is Ballingaddy (Old) Graveyard, Boundary Wall Restoration. In advance of
confirmation of this funding, consultants have been procured and are being appointed to
manage the design, tendering, and appointment of the contractor and supervise the works.
Cappamore/Kilmallock Councillors will be appraised of timelines when funding is secured.

5. Question submitted by Councillor M. Ryan

| will ask at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District for the number
of people/families classed as homeless/using homeless services in the Cappamore-
Kilmallock Municipal District area.

Reply:

There are currently eight families with an Area of Choice selected within the Cappamore-
Kilmallock Municipal District who are in receipt of homeless emergency accommodation
supports from Limerick City and County Council. Three of these families have the
Metropolitan area selected as their first Area of Choice, and have selected an area in
Cappamore-Kilmallock as an alternative to their Metropolitan area selection.

There are also two families who have selected Croom as their first Area of Choice, with
Cappamore-Kilmallock selected as an alternative area. Three families have selected an Area
of Choice within the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District as their first selection.

as to when the installation of Cats Eyes will be completed on the sections of the R512 and

The Municipal District Office is currently undertaking the installation of road studs (Cats

continue in 2026 subject to the availability of funding.

Notice of Motions

8. Notice of Motion submitted by Cllr Conway & Cllr Carey

We will move at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District that an
inspection be carried out at Sherins Cross, Dromin to determine what actions are deemed
necessary to make this crossroads safer,

The Notice of Motion was proposed by Cllr Conway, seconded by Cllr Carey, and agreed.


---
*<small>Scanned page, text may contain errors. See original file for clarity</small>*  

Reply:

The Municipal District has carried out various improvement works at Sherins Cross over the
last few years, including resurfacing works, road markings, and the installation of hi-visibility
flexi bollards. It is also planned shortly to replace the large warning signs on both R512
approaches to the junction. The Roads Engineer will also review the junction to determine if
any additional measures are warranted.

9. Notice of Motion submitted by Cllr Teefy

| will move at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District that road
resurfacing and improved safety works be carried out on the R514 Cahercorney road. Sections
of this road are in poor condition and safety measures are particularly needed in the vicinity
of Cahercorney Graveyard.

The Notice of Motion was proposed by Clir Teefy, seconded by Cllr E. Ryan, and agreed.

Reply:

The Municipal District Office commenced surfacing works on the R514, starting on the
boundary with the Adare-Rathkeale Municipal District, in 2023, and a further section was
completed in 2024. With regard to continuing this section of road until it meets the R513,
this will be looked at under the 2026 Road Works Programme. The R514, adjacent to
Cahercorney Graveyard, will also be reviewed to determine what appropriate safety
measures may be required.

The Cathaoirleach and Members extended their condolences to the McCarthy family on the
recent sad passings of Tom and Gerry McCarthy, formerly Carrickettle, Kilteely, who had both
worked for Limerick County Council for more than 40 years each. They paid tribute to their
service with the Council over the years and their work in road maintenance.

They also praised Council staff during the recent storm and their work in minimising flooding
damage, where possible, during both daytime and night-time hours.

The Members also wished Garda Noelle Fitzgerald, Kilmallock Gardai, all the best in her new
role in Limerick and thanked her for her work in Kilmallock while she was there.

Finally, the Members acknowledged the road improvement works carried out In Caherconlish
in recent months and they thanked the Council engineers and staff who were involved with
same.

This concluded the Meeting.

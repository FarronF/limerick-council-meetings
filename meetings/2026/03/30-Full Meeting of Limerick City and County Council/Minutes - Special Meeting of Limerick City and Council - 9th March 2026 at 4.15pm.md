**1.**

**St. Patrick’s Festival Open Funding Call 2026**

Circulated, Report of the Head of Economic and Tourism Development dated 4th March, 2026

seeking the Council’s approval to recommended allocations under the Limerick City and

County Council Festivals and Events St. Patrick’s Festival Open Funding Call 2026.

The Head of Economic and Tourism Development provided an overview of the timeline for

the funding call, the categories under which applications were received, the funding available

and details of the recommended allocations. He outlined the funding categories as follows:

1. Category 1: Limerick St. Patrick’s County Parades Support – Subdivided into:

a. Towns (maximum funding cap at €1,800 per town)

b. Villages (maximum funding cap at €800 per village).

2. Category 2: Cultural Institutions/Venues - St. Patrick’s Festival-themed Event

3. Category 3: Other St. Patrick’s Festival-themed Events/Creations
---
**2**

Members thanked the Head of Economic and Tourism Development for the overview of the

Open Funding Call 2026. Members queried how villages and towns were categorised for the

allocation of funding and emphasised the importance of informing Members in advance of

issuing notice of a Call for Funding, noting that it is a reserved function.

In response to the Members’ queries, the Head of Economic and Tourism Development

advised that applications for funding for towns and villages were categorised based on the

Settlement Hierarchy set out in the Limerick Development Plan 2022 to 2028.

Proposed by Councillor Sheahan;

Seconded by Councillor Ryan (E);

And Resolved:

“That, the approval of the Council be and is hereby given to the recommendations on

the proposed allocation of funding for the St. Patrick’s Festival Open Funding Call 2026, as set

out in the report of the Head of Economic and Tourism Development dated 4th March, 2026.”

This concluded the Meeting.

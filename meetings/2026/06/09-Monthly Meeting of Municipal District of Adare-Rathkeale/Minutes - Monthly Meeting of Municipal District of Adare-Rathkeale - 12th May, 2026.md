**Active Travel Scheme, Adare**

The Executive Engineer, Active Travel provided Members with a presentation on the

proposed Blackabbey Road Active Travel Scheme, which will comprise a kilometre of

footpath along the L1422 from the Village Hall to the GAA pitch in Adare. The scheme is

currently at Preliminary Design Stage and will address the following issues:

• High volume of HGV & agricultural vehicles using the L1422.

• Lack of safe connectivity with residential areas.

• Absence of controlled pedestrian crossing points.

• Substandard footpaths with 5m carriageway width.

• Parking overspill and pinch point at Broad St. Junction.

• Existing utility infrastructure causing obstructions.

• Blind bend on the village side of Adare GAA grounds.

• Sightline restrictions at driveways along the L1422.

The Active Travel team advised they are also engaging with impacted landowners before

progressing to the Part 8 Planning stage and must also complete Road Safety Audit,

Environmental Screenings, Public Lighting Design Report as well as Architectural

Heritage Impact Assessments.

The Executive Engineer, Active Travel concluded by informing Members a workshop

would be held with Elected Members prior to the Part 8 Planning submission in Q3 of

2026 along with an information evening for residents of Adare during the Part 8

consultation period. Subject to Part 8 Planning approval a funding application would be

prepared for the National Transport Authority for 2027 funding allocation to progress

the detailed design, tender & evaluation and construction stages.

Members welcomed the scheme, however, discussion ensued in relation to the number

of large agricultural vehicles travelling through the village of Adare and queried if the six

metre carriageway could extend to the three metre shared space. The Executive

Engineer, Active Travel advised as there is a 50km speed limit on this carriageway the

Active Travel Department are governed by recommended guidelines on road width. The

QUESTIONS
8. Question submitted by Councillor Kiely

| will ask at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick what action plan is
currently in place to treat Japanese knotweed in the Boro Park and in other areas in the
Metropolitan District, by electoral area and in tabular format.

9. Question submitted by Councillor Beasley

| will ask at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick can Limerick City and
County Council provide a breakdown of how Local Property Tax revenue was allocated and
spent in each of the years 2024, 2025, to date, including which services and projects were
funded from this revenue stream in each of those years.

10. Question submitted by Councillor Beasley

| will ask at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick, over the past three
years, how much has Limerick City and County Council spent on providing emergency
accommodation for homeless persons (including hotels, bed and breakfasts, and other
emergency housing arrangements), and how much revenue has the Council received from
persons accessing this accommodation through rental agreements or payment
arrangements based on their circumstances.

Please provide the figures broken down by year (2024, 2025, to date).

11. by Councillor

| will ask at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick when dogs are
released from Limerick pound, are they microchipped and neutered as mandatory
processes, and is the microchip registered in the owner’s name.

12. Question submitted by Councillor O’Donovan

| will ask at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick for an update on the
boules area in the Pocket Park on Parnell Street and if those wishing to use the boules area
can hire boules for use.

13. Question submitted by Councillor O’Donovan

| will ask at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick for an update on work
undertaken at the Corbally Baths considering water quality consistently does not satisfy the
limits set out in the Bathing Water Regulations and what work is required to ensure
regulations are met for bathing.

14. Question submitted by Councillor Gavan

| will ask at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick for an update on the
dockless bike scheme in Annacotty/Castletroy with a view towards the September return of
students to the area.

15. Question submitted by Councillor Gavan

| will ask at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick for an update following
recent requests by Councillors in Limerick City East regarding movement on lane alteration
requests (left lane for Bloodmill and Dunnes) and improvements to Childers Road, in light of
increasing traffic congestion in these areas.

16. Question submitted by Councillor Donoghue

| will ask at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick for a full breakdown of
footfall figures across Limerick City as previously presented by Limerick City and County
Council, including days and times in certain locations, demographics, and events.

17. Question submitted by Councillor Donoghue

| will ask at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick how many living units
has Limerick City and County Council purchased under compulsory purchase order in the
city centre, and how many of these have been sold on for private or affordable housing.

NOTICES OF MOTION
18. N f Mi itted by Councillor Kilco

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that this District
write to Irish Rail requesting the installation of a temporary rail platform at Patrickswell
adjacent to the existing Limerick-Foynes railway line, and that this platform be designed to
operate during the Ryder Cup 2027 and thereafter be retained or repurposed subject to
post-event review, in order to maximise public transport capacity, reduce road congestion,
and future-proof transport infrastructure for the metropolitan area.

19. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Doyle

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that average speed
cameras be trialled on the Limerick to Murroe/Cappamore Road (R506), with cameras
before and after Annacotty Business Park in order to slow traffic approaching the railway
lines and the residential areas of Clyduff, Garraun and Lisnagry.

20. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Kiely

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City
and County Council progress plans to add a bus stop at the Claughaun GAA Club (or in the
immediate vicinity) for the residents of Castletroy View and the surrounding area.

21. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Secas

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City
and County Council urgently engage with the Department of Housing, Local Government and
Heritage, and with Uisce Eireann to secure remediation funding necessary to advance the
taking in charge process for the Hermitage estate in Castleconnell, including works relating
to roads, public lighting, drainage and public open spaces.

22. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Slattery

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that traffic calming
measures are put in place to stop speeding at St. Judes Avenue, Ballysimon Road.

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City
and County Council investigate the feasibility and costs of installing all-weather surfaces in
dog parks across the city, with O’Brien’s Dog Park on Clare Street to be considered as a trial
location.

24. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Beasley

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that Limerick City
and County Council write to An Garda Siochana requesting residential access control at
Caherdavin Lawn entrance on all GAA match days, allowing only residents to enter the
estate.

| will move at the next meeting of the Metropolitan District of Limerick that a plan is
developed to address the significant disparity in CCTV coverage between Limerick City West
and the other Metropolitan Local Electoral Areas, and for increased and additional CCTV
investment in Limerick City West.

26. Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor Gavan

planning applications for more than 1400, 200 units in the Castletroy/Annacotty area, and
another delay of BusConnects, that Limerick City and County Council urgently prioritise a
traffic management plan for Castletroy.

Economy Officer is recruited to help manage the local retail and cultural offering in Limerick

City.

public realm.

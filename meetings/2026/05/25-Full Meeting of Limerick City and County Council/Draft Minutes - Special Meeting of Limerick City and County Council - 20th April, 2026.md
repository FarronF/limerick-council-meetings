The Príomh Chomhairleoir opened the meeting and welcomed those attending in the gallery

and online to the Special Meeting that had been convened to discuss the following issue as

set out in the Agenda and accompanying Requisition:

“To consider a motion under Section 139 of the Local Government Act 2001 (as amended) in

relation to proposed works at Boro Park, Janesboro” as follows;

*“**Section 139 of the Local Government Act, 2001 (as amended) sets out that an Elected*

*Council or Joint Body as the case may be, may be resolution, direct that those works*

*shall not proceed.*

*Taking into consideration that the existing Boro Park, Janesboro is currently zoned as*

*“City Centre”* *in the 2022-2028 Limerick Development Plan. Section 12.4 of the*

*Limerick Development Plan sets out* *in the “Land Use Zoning Matrix” that*

*Leisure/Recreation Facilities is a generally permitted use on lands zoned “City Centre”*

*and is a type of development which that the Council accepts in principle in the relevant*

*zone.*
---
**2**

*Conscious of the increased housing needs across the City and County and to prevent*

*any delay in delivering units by taking staffing resources and public funds to advance*

*a Part 8 Planning Application for the said site when a more suitable site should be*

*advanced as a priority.*

*Accordingly in the common good, of advancing housing delivery as a priority and for*

*the local area and taking into consideration that the Boro Park, Janesboro, is the only*

*safe green space for many existing residents to enjoy we the undersigned wish to*

*propose this Council approves this resolution under Section 139 of the Local*

*Government Act, 2001 (as amended) that the Council shall not proceed with any*

*further such works to advance the delivery of SMART Housing on the said site*.”

At the outset, the Mayor, delivered a presentation to the Members, as requested, which

included the following:

• A discussion on whether the Motion before the Council represented an appropriate

use of Section 139 of the Local Government Act 2001, as amended.

• The context of the housing crisis in Limerick and the challenges of housing delivery

and meeting housing demand.

• Funding and the need for multiannual funding plans.

• The Colbert Quarter Framework which includes the provision of housing, new public

parks and sports facilities and the context of the Boro Park site.

• The capacity of the Boro Park site to accommodate approximately 400 homes as an

interim housing activation strategy for the site.

• The SMART Housing Model as a solution for affordable renting and modern method

of construction prototype as a solution for the rapid delivery method for homes for 1

to 2 person households.

• Responses to community concerns raised at the Janesboro Public Meeting Listening

Exercise.

• The overall framework vision for the Calendonian District, to include better public

amenities and improved connectivity for the area.

On the proposal of the Príomh Chomhairleoir and seconded by Councillor Kiely, it was agreed

to take a 10-minute adjournment to consider the Mayor’s presentation.

Upon resumption, the Motion was proposed by Councillor Kiely and seconded by Príomh

Chomhairleoir.

Councillor Kiely welcomed the representatives from the Janesboro community to the public

gallery in the Council Chamber, as well as those who joined online, and outlined the

importance of Boro Park to the local community.

In proposing the Motion, Councillor Kiely acknowledged the housing needs across the city and

county, highlighting that other sites had been identified which were more suitable than Boro

Park and the importance of advancing housing delivery on these sites. She further outlined

the housing development planned for Carey’s Road, which is adjacent to Janesboro, as well

as other developments led by the Land Development Agency which have not yet commenced

and expressed concern regarding delays in their delivery.

Councillor Kiely outlined that Boro Park was considered the only safe green space for many

existing residents in Janesboro, highlighting the significant value and the need to retain this

community amenity.

Members expressed their support for the Motion noting their disappointment that it had

been necessary to bring a Section 139 before the Council. They stressed the importance of

Boro Park to the local community, which is used by people of all ages, and the need to retain

this important green space for the Janesboro area.

While acknowledging the ongoing housing crisis and strongly emphasising that they did not

want to stop any housing delivery, Members were of the view that more suitable alternative

sites should be progressed.

Members commended the local community for their strong campaign to retain the park and

stressed the importance of clear and ongoing communication with residents in relation to

such developments.

It was proposed by Councillor Kiely that a roll call vote would be taken requesting Members

to vote in favour or against the Section 139 Motion “That the Council shall not proceed with

any works on Boro Park, Janesboro.” This was seconded by Príomh Chomhairleoir.

The Príomh Chomhairleoir instructed that a roll call vote would now be taken.

The Members were advised that under Section 17(5) of the Local Government (Mayor of

Limerick) and Miscellaneous Provisions Act 2024 “The Mayor shall not have a vote in relation

to a motion or resolution under Part VII or under Section 132, 138, 139 or 140 of the Principal

Act (as modified by this Act)”.

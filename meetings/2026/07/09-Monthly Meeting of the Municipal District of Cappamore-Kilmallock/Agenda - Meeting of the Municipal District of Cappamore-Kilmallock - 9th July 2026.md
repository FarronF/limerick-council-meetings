. Notice of Motion submitted by Clir Carey

| will move at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District
that additional car park spaces be identified, and design and costings be
completed for same at Radharc na Cille Estate, Kilmallock, Co. Limerick.

. Notice of Motion submitted by Clir M. Ryan

| will move at the next Meeting of the Cappamore-Kilmallock Municipal District
that further additional works be carried out on the Cross of Five roads junction
in Murroe in order to ensure a safe junction for all users, pedestrian, active
travel and vehicle users at this location.

. Notice of Motion submitted by Cilr Stokes

| will move at the next meeting Cappamore-Killmallock Muncipal District that

and Housing, serving across the Department of Health and the Department of
Housing, Local Government and Heritage, to increase the budget allocated by
the Department of Housing, Local Government and Heritage to ensure that
grant schemes can meet the demand and reflect the increase in construction
costs.

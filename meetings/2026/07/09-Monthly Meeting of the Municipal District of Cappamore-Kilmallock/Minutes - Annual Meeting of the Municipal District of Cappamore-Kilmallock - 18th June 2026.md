MINUTES OF PROCEEDINGS OF ANNUAL MEETING OF THE MUNICIPAL DISTRICT OF
CAPPAMORE-KILMALLOCK HELD IN ARAS MAINCHIN SEOIGE AND ON-LINE, ON THURSDAY
18™ JUNE 2026 AT 2.00PM.

PRESENT IN THE CHAIR:
Councillor Conway, Cathaoirleach.

MEMBERS PRESENT:
Councillors Carey, O'Sullivan, E. Ryan, M. Ryan, Stokes, and Teefy.

OFFICIALS IN ATTENDANCE:

Meetings Administrator (Mr. M. Leahy); A/Senior Engineer (Mr. T. McKechnie); A/Senior
Executive Officer (Ms. K. White), A/Senior Executive Engineer (Mr. D. Corcoran); Clerical
Officer (Ms. L. Moloney).

Apologies: Director of Services (Ms. P. Liddy)
1. Welcome

The Cathaoirleach welcomed everyone to the meeting. He thanked his fellow Councillors for
their work and input over the previous 12 months. He referred to the progress made across
many of the activities of the Municipal District during the past year. He highlighted how the
Members had worked well together on behalf of the District, and that this was key to
delivering on the many objectives across the Municipal District.

He thanked the Management and Staff of Limerick City & County Council for their
engagement and support during the previous 12 months. In particular, he acknowledged the
work of the staff, both indoor and outdoor, working in the Municipal District. He outlined
how working on behalf of the Municipal District had been an honour and privilege. He also
wished to acknowledge his Leas Cathaoirleach, Cllr O’Sullivan, for his support and assistance
during the year, and acknowledged the support of Mayor Moran too during his tenure.

The Members congratulated Cllr Conway on his year as Cathaoirleach, and they
acknowledged the fair and impartial manner that he conducted meetings and discussions
with the Members and staff.

The outgoing Cathaoirleach, Councillor Conway invited nominations for the Office of
Cathaoirleach for the coming year.

Proposed by Councillor Stokes;

Seconded by Councillor M. Ryan.

“That Councillor Tommy O'Sullivan be appointed Cathaoirleach of the Municipal District of
Cappamore-Kilmallock, to hold office until the next Annual Meeting or until a successor has
been appointed.”

As there were no other nominations, the Elected Members declared Councillor O'Sullivan
elected as Cathaoirleach of the Municipal District of Cappamore-Kilmaliock.

Cllr O'Sullivan stated that it was a huge honour for both him and his family to be elected as
Cathaoirleach of the Municipal District, which is the biggest Municipal District in Limerick.
He congratulated the outgoing Cathaoirleach for the way he managed Meetings and events
as Cathaoirleach during his term. He looked forward to working with all his fellow
Councillors to deliver for everyone in the Municipal District during the next 12 months.

He also thanked the Management and Staff of Limerick City & County Council for their
ongoing help and assistance, and he looked forward to working with everyone as
Cathaoirleach in the year ahead.

He highlighted the need for continued progress with housing delivery in the Municipal
District. He also wanted to encourage engagement by secondary school students in the
activities of the local authority, to raise awareness of all the work being done by the local
authority and the Elected Members.

The newly elected Cathaoirleach then invited nominations for the office of Leas-Cathaoirleach
of the Municipal District of Cappamore-Kilmallock for the forthcoming year.

Proposed by Councillor Conway;
Seconded by Councillor M. Ryan.

“That Councillor Noreen Stokes be appointed Leas-Cathaoirleach of the Municipal District of
Cappamore-Kilmallock to hold office until the next Annual Meeting or until a successor has
been appointed.”

As there were no other nominations, the Cathaoirleach declared Councillor Stokes elected
as Leas-Cathaoirleach of the Municipal District of Cappamore-Kilmallock and wished her
well for the forthcoming year.

The Members congratulated the newly elected Cathaoirleach and Leas Cathaoirleach on
their election and wished them well for the twelve months ahead. They agreed on the
importance of all working constructively together in the Municipal District.

The A/Senior Engineer, on behalf of management and staff, congratulated the Cathaoirleach
and Leas Cathaoirleach on their election and wished them well for the year ahead.
This concluded the Meeting.

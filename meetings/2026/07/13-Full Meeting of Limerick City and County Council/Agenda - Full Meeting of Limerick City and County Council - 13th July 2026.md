ADJOURNED NOTICE OF MOTION

Notice of Motion submitted by John Moran, Mayor of Limerick (deferred at
Special Meeting of Limerick City and County Council held on 19° May, 2026)

| will move at the next Meeting that this Council agrees, subject to the necessary
administrative, legal, procurement and data protection arrangements being put in place,
and with financial support secured so as to protect existing core services in 2026, that all
meetings of the Full Council held after the adoption of this resolution shall be live-
streamed and recorded in full by audio and video means, and that such recordings shall
be made publicly available on the Council's official website (www.limerick.ie) no later than
two business days following the conclusion of each meeting, without unnecessary barriers
or registration requirements.

MAYOR'S QUESTIONS
estion by Councillor E.

| will ask the Mayor at the next Meeting, in light of the significant disparity in CCTV
coverage across the three Metropolitan Districts, with Limerick City West accounting for
just 12% of CCTV coverage compared to 34% in Limerick City East and 51% in Limerick City
North, and noting that a portion of the 2025 Mayoral Budget was allocated towards the
expansion of CCTV infrastructure, will the Mayor outline whether there is a plan to ensure
that future CCTV investment is distributed equitably across all Metropolitan Districts, with
particular regard to addressing the current shortfall in Limerick City West.

NOTICES OF MOTION
Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor O. O'Sullivan

I will move at the next Meeting of Limerick City and County Council that this Council will
recognise the value of strategic continuity and the need to maximise previous public
investment in promoting Limerick nationally and internationally and provide a report to
the July Plenary Meeting of Limerick City and County Council on the current status,
governance, ownership, delivery and future direction of the Limerick Place Brand Strategy
~ Atlantic Edge, European Embrace, launched in 2020.

Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor G. Ward (to be referred to the Travel
and Transportation Strategic Policy Committee)

! will move at the next Meeting of Limerick City and County Council that this Council
acknowledges the critical need to improve and protect road users with the necessary
clearance for wide agricultural machinery. | would like to propose that all village and town
centres under the renewal schemes would adopt a hatched road marking in the centre of
the road to allow for the need of a safe passage for larger vehicles. It is the utmost
importance for the protection of pedestrians to stop machinery having to mount or
encroach on pedestrian/cycleways to pass through.

We will move at the next Meeting of Limerick City and County Council that from Budget
2027 all net income generated from burial grounds across the city and county is ringfenced
specifically for graveyard maintenance and improvement works.

We will move at the next Meeting that Limerick City and County Council calls on the Mayor
to initiate proceedings to vary the County Development Plan to facilitate/zone retail sites
on the Killarney Road, Abbeyfeale and that this Motion if passed be forwarded to the
Mayor's Office and also placed on the next full Council agenda.

31.

Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor M. Ryan (to be referred to the Home
and Social Development Strategic Policy Committee)

| will move at the next Meeting that Limerick City and County Council and Mayor Moran,
along with the Department of Housing, would consider options under the first home
scheme of developing serviced site estates throughout the city and county towns and
villages whereby the developed site becomes the equity portion or part thereof in
assisting people to purchase their first home.

Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor S. Beasley

| will move at the next Meeting of Limerick City and County Council that the Council will
allocate vacant Council owned properties that are deemed structurally safe and habitable
to people from the housing waiting list without delay, with minor refurbishment and
upgrade works to be carried out whilst the tenant is in-situ.

Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor C. Slattery

| will move at the next Meeting that Limerick City and County Council write to the HSE
asking if a hub dedicated to menopause can be established in Limerick.

Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor E. O'Donovan

| will move at the next Meeting of Limerick City and County Council that this Council write
to the Minister for Further and Higher Education, Research, Innovation and Science and
the Minister for Justice, Home Affairs and Migration seeking a review of learner
protections for international students following the recent closure of Student Campus in
Limerick, and outlining what measures can be introduced to ensure Limerick remains a
welcoming and trusted destination for international students to live, learn and work.

Notice of Motion submitted by Councillor S. Hickey-O’Mara (to be referred to the
Protocol Committee)

| will move at the next Meeting of Limerick City and County Council that a new theatre
bursary is established in memory of renowned director and actor John Murphy in honour
of his lifelong contribution and commitment to theatre in Limerick, and the decades of
support and mentorship he gave to generations of Limerick artists.
